ringworm. · 癣。

WHY / 为什么

Ringworm is a contagious fungal skin infection. On hands or feet, it may form itchy rings, peeling skin, or small blisters.

ringworm（癣）是一种会传染的真菌性皮肤感染。发生在手足时，可能出现瘙痒的环形斑、脱皮或小水疱。

LOCK THIS / 记重点

ringworm = fungal infection; scabies = animal parasite.

ringworm = 真菌感染；scabies（疥疮）= 动物寄生虫感染。
