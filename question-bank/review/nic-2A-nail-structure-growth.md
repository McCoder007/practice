# Set 2A — Nail Structure and Growth / 指甲结构与生长

Anatomy and Physiology · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. The technical term for the natural nail is**  
natural nail（天然指甲）的专业名称是什么？  
A. lunula. / 甲半月（lunula）  
B. onyx. / 甲（onyx） ✅  
C. matrix. / 甲母质（matrix）  
D. hyponychium. / 甲下皮（hyponychium）  
*Why:* Onyx means the nail plate itself, the hard plate that protects the fingertip. The other choices are other parts of the nail unit: the lunula and matrix at the base, the hyponychium under the tip.  
*为什么:* onyx（甲）指的就是甲板本身，也就是保护指尖的那块坚硬甲板。其他选项只是指甲单元（nail unit）里的其他部分：lunula（甲半月）和 matrix（甲母质）在指甲根部，hyponychium（甲下皮）在指甲尖下面。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Onyx = the nail itself. Onycho- at the start of a word also means nail, as in onycholysis.  
*记重点:* onyx = 指甲本身。很多指甲病名开头的 onycho- 也是“指甲”的意思，例如 onycholysis（甲剥离）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.98 (PDF 122), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.100 (PDF 124), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.102 (PDF 126)

**2. The natural nail is made mainly of**  
天然指甲主要由什么构成？  
A. collagen. / 胶原蛋白（collagen）  
B. melanin. / 黑色素（melanin）  
C. keratin. / 角蛋白（keratin） ✅  
D. elastin. / 弹性蛋白（elastin）  
*Why:* The nail is made mainly of keratin, the fiber-shaped protein also found in skin and hair. Nail keratin is more durable than hair or skin keratin.  
*为什么:* 指甲主要由 keratin（角蛋白）构成，这种纤维状蛋白质也存在于皮肤和头发中。指甲里的角蛋白比头发和皮肤里的更耐用。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Melanin gives skin its color. Collagen and elastin are in the dermis. The hard nail plate is keratin.  
*记重点:* 角蛋白的“角”= 牛角的“角”，又硬又结实。melanin（黑色素）管颜色，collagen（胶原蛋白）和 elastin（弹性蛋白）在真皮里。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.98 (PDF 122)

**3. Where are nail plate cells formed?**  
甲板细胞在哪里生成？  
A. Matrix / 甲母质（matrix） ✅  
B. Nail bed / 甲床（nail bed）  
C. Hyponychium / 甲下皮（hyponychium）  
D. Free edge / 游离缘（free edge）  
*Why:* The matrix sits under the skin at the nail base. Its cells keep making new cells, and those cells become the nail plate. The hyponychium and free edge are out at the tip.  
*为什么:* matrix（甲母质）在指甲根部的皮肤下面。它的细胞不停地制造新细胞，这些新细胞就变成甲板。hyponychium（甲下皮）和游离缘（free edge）都在指甲尖那一头。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* The matrix is like the nail's mother: new nail cells are born here. The nail bed only holds the plate.  
*记重点:* 甲母质的“母”= 母亲：新的甲板细胞在这里“出生”。甲床（nail bed）只是托住甲板。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.100 (PDF 124), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.102 (PDF 126)

**4. The whitish half-moon at the base of the nail plate is the**  
甲板根部发白的半月形叫什么？  
A. cuticle. / 甲小皮（cuticle）  
B. eponychium. / 甲上皮（eponychium）  
C. free edge. / 游离缘（free edge）  
D. lunula. / 甲半月（lunula） ✅  
*Why:* The lunula is the whitish half-moon at the base of the nail plate. It is the visible part of the matrix; light reflecting off the matrix makes it look white.  
*为什么:* lunula（甲半月）是甲板根部发白的半月形。它是 matrix（甲母质）露出来的部分，光线从甲母质表面反射，所以看起来发白。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* Lunula = the little moon. Everyone has one, but some stay hidden under the proximal nail fold.  
*记重点:* 甲半月的“半月”= 半个月亮。人人都有，但有些比较短，藏在近端甲襞下面看不见。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.100 (PDF 124)

**5. Dead, colorless tissue attached to the nail plate is the**  
附着在甲板上、无色的死组织叫什么？  
A. eponychium. / 甲上皮（eponychium）  
B. hyponychium. / 甲下皮（hyponychium）  
C. cuticle. / 甲小皮（cuticle） ✅  
D. lateral nail fold. / 侧甲襞（lateral nail fold）  
*Why:* The cuticle is nonliving, colorless tissue stuck to the nail plate. It forms a seal that keeps germs from getting under the skin.  
*为什么:* cuticle（甲小皮）是粘在甲板上的无色死组织。它形成一道密封，防止病菌钻到皮肤下面。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Of these four, only the cuticle is dead. It rides out on top of the plate as the nail grows.  
*记重点:* 四个选项里只有 cuticle（甲小皮）是死的；eponychium、hyponychium 和侧甲襞都是活皮肤。指甲往外长时，甲小皮就贴在甲板上被带出来。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.100 (PDF 124)

**6. Which living tissue makes the cuticle?**  
哪种活组织生成甲小皮（cuticle）？  
A. Eponychium / 甲上皮（eponychium） ✅  
B. Matrix / 甲母质（matrix）  
C. Hyponychium / 甲下皮（hyponychium）  
D. Nail bed / 甲床（nail bed）  
*Why:* The eponychium is the living skin on the underside of the proximal nail fold. It makes the cuticle. The cuticle is dead; the eponychium is alive.  
*为什么:* eponychium（甲上皮）是近端甲襞（proximal nail fold）底面的活皮肤，甲小皮就是它生成的。甲小皮是死的，甲上皮是活的。  
*Lock this (picture):* A snake leaves its old skin behind. The living eponychium leaves dead cuticle on the plate the same way.  
*记重点:* 蛇蜕皮时，会把旧皮留在身后。活的 eponychium（甲上皮）也是这样，把死的 cuticle（甲小皮）留在甲板上。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.101 (PDF 125)

**7. The thickened skin between the fingertip and the free edge is the**  
指尖与游离缘之间那层稍厚的皮肤叫什么？  
A. eponychium. / 甲上皮（eponychium）  
B. lunula. / 甲半月（lunula）  
C. nail bed. / 甲床（nail bed）  
D. hyponychium. / 甲下皮（hyponychium） ✅  
*Why:* The hyponychium forms a barrier that keeps germs out of the nail bed. If it is damaged, the plate can separate from the bed and infection can start.  
*为什么:* hyponychium（甲下皮）形成一道屏障，阻止病菌侵入甲床。如果它受损，甲板可能与甲床分离，甲下就可能感染。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Hypo- = under, epi- = on top. Hyponychium: under the nail tip. Eponychium: at the nail base.  
*记重点:* hypo- = 在下面，epi- = 在上面。hyponychium（甲下皮）在指甲尖下面；eponychium（甲上皮）在指甲根部。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.102 (PDF 126)

**8. Nail polish is applied to the**  
指甲油涂在哪里？  
A. nail bed. / 甲床（nail bed）  
B. nail plate. / 甲板（nail plate） ✅  
C. matrix. / 甲母质（matrix）  
D. eponychium. / 甲上皮（eponychium）  
*Why:* Polish goes on the nail plate, the hard keratin plate on top. The nail bed is the living skin under the plate.  
*为什么:* 指甲油涂在 nail plate（甲板）上，也就是上面那层坚硬的角蛋白板。nail bed（甲床）是甲板下面的活皮肤。  
*Lock this (contrast):* The bed is living skin full of nerves; polish never touches it.  
*记重点:* 甲床是布满神经的活皮肤，指甲油根本碰不到它。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.100 (PDF 124)

**9. Why does the nail bed look pink?**  
为什么甲床看起来是粉色的？  
A. It is rich in blood vessels. / 它血管丰富 ✅  
B. It contains extra melanin. / 它含有较多黑色素  
C. Light reflects off the matrix. / 光从甲母质反射  
D. The cuticle covers it. / 甲小皮盖在上面  
*Why:* The nail bed is richly supplied with blood vessels, so it looks pink under the plate, from the lunula to just before the free edge.  
*为什么:* nail bed（甲床）血管丰富，所以从甲半月到游离缘之前，透过甲板看起来是粉色的。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Press on a nail: the pink fades as blood is pushed away, then returns when you let go.  
*记重点:* 按一下指甲：血被挤走，粉色变白；一松手血流回来，又变粉。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.100 (PDF 124)

**10. What thin tissue attaches the nail plate to the nail bed?**  
哪层薄组织把甲板连接在甲床上？  
A. Cuticle / 甲小皮（cuticle）  
B. Eponychium / 甲上皮（eponychium）  
C. Bed epithelium / 甲床上皮（bed epithelium） ✅  
D. Hyponychium / 甲下皮（hyponychium）  
*Why:* The bed epithelium is a thin layer of tissue that attaches the nail plate to the nail bed. It helps guide the plate along the bed as it grows.  
*为什么:* bed epithelium（甲床上皮）是一层薄组织，把甲板连接在甲床上，并在指甲生长时引导甲板沿着甲床向前移动。  
*Lock this (picture):* Think of a sliding door's track: it holds the door on and guides it as it slides.  
*记重点:* 想想推拉门的轨道：既把门固定住，又引导门往前滑。甲床上皮对甲板就是这样。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.100 (PDF 124)

**11. What attaches the nail bed and matrix to the bone underneath?**  
什么把甲床和甲母质连接到下面的骨头上？  
A. Bed epithelium / 甲床上皮（bed epithelium）  
B. Nail folds / 甲襞（nail folds）  
C. Cuticle / 甲小皮（cuticle）  
D. Specialized ligaments / 特化韧带（specialized ligaments） ✅  
*Why:* Specialized ligaments attach the nail bed and matrix bed to the bone. They sit at the base of the matrix and around the edges of the nail bed.  
*为什么:* specialized ligaments（特化韧带）把甲床和甲母质（matrix bed）连接到下面的骨头上，位于甲母质根部和甲床边缘周围。  
*Lock this (picture):* A ligament is a tough band, like a strong rope. Ligaments also hold your eight wrist bones together.  
*记重点:* 韧带的“韧”= 结实、拉不断，像一根结实的绳子。手腕的 8 块腕骨也是靠韧带连在一起的。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.102 (PDF 126)

**12. The fold of skin overlapping the side of the nail is the**  
覆盖在指甲侧面的皮肤褶叫什么？  
A. proximal nail fold. / 近端甲襞（proximal nail fold）  
B. lateral nail fold. / 侧甲襞（lateral nail fold） ✅  
C. hyponychium. / 甲下皮（hyponychium）  
D. eponychium. / 甲上皮（eponychium）  
*Why:* The lateral nail fold, also called the sidewall, is the fold of skin overlapping the side of the nail. The nail folds form the nail groove on each side.  
*为什么:* lateral nail fold（侧甲襞）也叫 sidewall（侧壁），是覆盖指甲侧面的皮肤褶。甲襞在指甲两侧形成 nail groove（甲沟）。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Lateral = side; proximal = near the base. Its other name, sidewall, tells you where it is.  
*记重点:* lateral = 侧面，proximal = 靠近根部。侧甲襞（lateral nail fold）的另一个名字 sidewall（侧墙）就说明了它的位置。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.99 (PDF 123)

**13. Compared with adults, children's nails grow**  
与成人相比，儿童的指甲生长  
A. more slowly. / 更慢  
B. at the same rate. / 速度一样  
C. faster in winter than summer. / 冬天比夏天长得快  
D. more rapidly. / 更快 ✅  
*Why:* Children's nails grow more rapidly, while elderly people's nails grow more slowly. Nails also grow faster in summer than in winter.  
*为什么:* 儿童的指甲长得更快，老年人的指甲长得更慢。此外，指甲夏天比冬天长得快。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Kids grow fast: they get taller every year, and their nails grow fast too. With age, the matrix slows down.  
*记重点:* 小孩长得快：每年都在长高，指甲也长得快。年纪大了，matrix（甲母质）就慢下来了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.103 (PDF 127), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.104 (PDF 128)

**14. Even when it looks dry and hard, the proximal nail fold is**  
即使看起来又干又硬，近端甲襞（proximal nail fold）也是  
A. living tissue that must not be cut. / 不能剪的活组织 ✅  
B. dead tissue that can be trimmed. / 可以修剪的死组织  
C. part of the nail plate. / 甲板的一部分  
D. the same tissue as the cuticle. / 与甲小皮是同一种组织  
*Why:* The proximal nail fold is living skin. Nail technicians may gently push it back, but may never cut it. No client or doctor can give permission to cut living skin.  
*为什么:* proximal nail fold（近端甲襞）是活皮肤。美甲师可以轻轻往后推，但绝不能剪。顾客和医生都无权允许美甲师剪活皮肤。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Milady's test: if you cut deep enough, would it bleed? Yes means living skin: push back, never cut.  
*记重点:* Milady 的判断法：假如剪得够深，会流血吗？会，就是活皮肤：只能推，绝不能剪。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.101 (PDF 125), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.102 (PDF 126)

成人甲板的生长速度大约是多少？  
A. 1/2 inch per month / 每月 1/2 英寸

*Why:* In a normal adult, the nail plate grows about 1/10 to 1/8 inch (2.5 to 3 mm) per month.  
*为什么:* 正常成人的甲板每月大约长 1/10 至 1/8 英寸（2.5 至 3 毫米）。  
*Lock this (picture):* On a ruler, cut one inch into 8 equal parts. A nail grows about one part each month.  
*记重点:* 在尺子上，把 1 英寸平均分成 8 小格。指甲每个月大约长 1 小格。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.103 (PDF 127)

**17. Nail growth speeds up dramatically during**  
指甲生长在什么时候会明显加快？  
A. the winter months. / 冬季  
B. old age. / 老年  
C. prenatal vitamin use. / 服用孕期维生素时  
D. the last trimester of pregnancy. / 怀孕的最后三个月（last trimester） ✅  
*Why:* Hormone changes in the last trimester of pregnancy make nails grow much faster. Growth returns to normal after delivery. Prenatal vitamins are not the cause.  
*为什么:* 怀孕最后三个月（last trimester）的荷尔蒙变化会让指甲长得快很多，分娩后恢复正常。孕期维生素并不是原因。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Women take prenatal vitamins all through pregnancy. Nails speed up only in the last three months: hormones, not vitamins.  
*记重点:* 孕期维生素通常整个孕期都在吃，指甲却只在最后三个月猛长：原因是荷尔蒙，不是维生素。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.103 (PDF 127)

**18. About how long does it take to replace a whole fingernail plate?**  
整片手指甲板完全长新大约需要多久？

C. Nine months to one year / 九个月至一年

**19. The thickness, width, and curvature of the nail plate are determined by the**  
甲板的厚度、宽度和弧度由什么决定？  
A. nail bed. / 甲床（nail bed）  
B. cuticle. / 甲小皮（cuticle）  
C. matrix. / 甲母质（matrix） ✅  
D. hyponychium. / 甲下皮（hyponychium）  
*Why:* The length, width, and curvature of the matrix determine the thickness, width, and curvature of the plate. A longer matrix makes a thicker plate.  
*为什么:* matrix（甲母质）的长度、宽度和弧度，决定了甲板的厚度、宽度和弧度。甲母质越长，甲板越厚。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* That is why toenails are thicker: their matrix is longer. No product can make a nail grow thicker.  
*记重点:* 所以脚趾甲更厚：它的甲母质更长。任何产品都不能让指甲长得更厚。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.102 (PDF 126), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.103 (PDF 127)

**20. A healthy nail's water content is about**  
健康指甲的含水量大约是多少？  
A. 2 to 5 percent. / 2% 至 5%  
B. 15 to 25 percent. / 15% 至 25% ✅  
C. 50 to 60 percent. / 50% 至 60%  
D. 75 to 85 percent. / 75% 至 85%  
*Why:* A healthy nail may look dry and hard, but it holds 15 to 25 percent water. The lower the water content, the more rigid the nail becomes.  
*为什么:* 健康的指甲看起来干硬，但含水量在 15% 至 25% 之间。含水量越低，指甲越僵硬。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Like a good tip: 15 to 25 percent of the bill. A healthy nail is about that much water.  
*记重点:* 就像给小费（tip）：一般是账单的 15% 到 25%。健康指甲里的水分也差不多是这么多。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.100 (PDF 124)
