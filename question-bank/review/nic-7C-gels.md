# Set 7C — Gels: Soft, Hard, and Hybrid / 光疗胶：软胶、硬胶与混合胶

Nail Enhancements: Application, Maintenance, and Removal · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. What ingredient makes gel harden when exposed to the lamp's light?**  
光疗胶里哪种成分在灯光照射下让胶变硬？  
A. Benzoyl peroxide / 过氧化苯甲酰（BPO）  
B. Resin accelerator / 树脂加速剂（resin accelerator）  
C. Photoinitiator / 光引发剂（photoinitiator） ✅  
D. Acetone / 丙酮（acetone）  
*Why:* A photoinitiator is a molecule that reacts when exposed to UV or visible light. It is mixed into the gel, so the gel hardens in a curing lamp.  
*为什么:* photoinitiator（光引发剂）是一种在 UV 或可见光照射下发生反应的分子。它加在胶里，所以胶在固化灯里会变硬。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Photo- is Greek for light, as in photograph. An initiator is the starter.  
*记重点:* photo- 是希腊语的“光”，如 photograph（照片）。initiator 就是“启动者”。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.354 (PDF 378)

**2. Compared with UV fluorescent lamps, LED lamps usually**  
和 UV 荧光灯相比，LED 灯通常怎样？  
A. cure faster and have longer-lasting bulbs. / 固化更快，灯珠更耐用。 ✅  
B. light all nails evenly from every side. / 从各个方向均匀照亮所有指甲。  
C. need one to two minutes per layer. / 每层需要 1 到 2 分钟。  
D. cure gel with heat instead of light. / 靠热而不是光来固化。  
*Why:* LED cure times are usually 5 to 60 seconds per layer, versus one to two minutes in UV, and LED bulbs last longer.  
*为什么:* LED 灯每层固化通常只要 5 到 60 秒，UV 灯要 1 到 2 分钟，而且 LED 灯珠更耐用。  
*Lock this (real-acronym):* LED = Light-Emitting Diode. Nail LED lamps shine at about 365–405 nm, so part of their light is UV.  
*记重点:* LED = Light-Emitting Diode（发光二极管）。美甲 LED 灯的波长约 365–405 纳米，其中一部分其实就是紫外线。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.355 (PDF 379)

**3. How must hard gel be removed?**  
硬胶（hard gel）必须怎样卸除？  
A. Soaked off in acetone / 用丙酮泡掉  
B. Peeled off with a wooden pusher / 用木推棒撬下来  
C. Dissolved with nail cleanser / 用甲面清洁剂溶掉  
D. Filed off with a hand or electric file / 用手锉或电动磨甲机磨掉 ✅  
*Why:* Hard gels have a nonporous surface that acetone cannot penetrate, so they must be filed off. Soft, soak-off gels are the ones that come off with acetone.  
*为什么:* hard gel（硬胶）表面无孔，丙酮渗不进去，所以必须磨掉。soft gel（软胶/可卸胶）才能用丙酮卸掉。  
*Lock this (picture):* Acetone on hard gel is like water on a glazed mug: it sits on top and never soaks in.  
*记重点:* 丙酮碰到硬胶，就像水泼在上了釉的瓷杯上：只停在表面，渗不进去。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.356 (PDF 380), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.360 (PDF 384)

**4. Soft gels are also known as**  
soft gel（软胶）又叫什么？  
A. traditional gels. / 传统胶（traditional gel）。  
B. soak-off gels. / 可卸胶（soak-off gel）。 ✅  
C. building gels. / 建构胶（building gel）。  
D. polymer gels. / 聚合物胶（polymer gel）。  
*Why:* Soft gels, also called soak-off gels, overlay the natural nail and come off easily with acetone.  
*为什么:* soft gel（软胶）也叫 soak-off gel（可卸胶），用于覆盖真甲，用丙酮就能轻松卸掉。  
*Lock this (name-trap):* Name trap: traditional gel isn't old-style polish; it is hard gel. Most soft gels are colored gel polish.  
*记重点:* 名字陷阱：traditional gel 不是传统甲油，而是硬胶。大多数软胶其实就是甲油胶。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.356 (PDF 380)

**5. Milady describes hybrid gels as gel polishes that**  
Milady 说混合胶（hybrid gel）是什么样的甲油胶？  
A. can only be removed by filing. / 只能锉掉。  
B. are hard gels for long extensions. / 是用来做长延长的硬胶。  
C. come off faster than regular polish. / 比普通甲油卸得更快。  
D. come off with polish remover, but slowly. / 可以用洗甲水卸，但比较慢。 ✅  
*Why:* Hybrid gels have the durability and gloss of gel but can be removed like polish with polish remover, though it takes longer. Gel polishes originally had to be filed off.  
*为什么:* hybrid gel（混合胶）有光疗胶的持久和光泽，却能像普通甲油一样用洗甲水卸掉，只是时间更长。早期的甲油胶只能靠打磨去除。  
*Lock this (word-origin):* Hybrid comes from Latin hybrida, a cross of two kinds: here, gel and regular polish.  
*记重点:* hybrid 来自拉丁语 hybrida，意思是两种东西的“混种”：这里就是光疗胶和普通甲油的结合。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.151 (PDF 175)

**6. How is the sticky inhibition layer removed from cured gel?**  
固化后光疗胶表面黏黏的浮胶层（inhibition layer）怎样去除？  
A. Wipe with alcohol or nail cleanser / 用酒精或甲面清洁剂擦掉 ✅  
B. Soak in acetone for ten minutes / 泡丙酮十分钟  
C. Rinse it off with warm water / 用温水冲掉  
D. Buff it in with cuticle oil / 用指缘油抛进去  
*Why:* The inhibition layer is uncured gel and/or leftover solvents that leave a tacky surface. Wipe it away with alcohol or nail cleanser on a lint-free wipe.  
*为什么:* inhibition layer（浮胶层）是未固化的胶和/或残留溶剂，在表面留下黏黏的一层。用无尘纸蘸酒精或甲面清洁剂就能擦掉。  
*Lock this (word-origin):* Inhibit comes from Latin inhibere, 'hold back': oxygen in the air holds back the cure, so the top stays tacky.  
*记重点:* inhibit 来自拉丁语 inhibere（阻止）：空气中的氧气阻止表面固化，所以表层黏黏的，中文叫“浮胶”。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.358 (PDF 382), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.359 (PDF 383)

**7. Why is a soak-off gel topcoat used over gel polish?**  
为什么甲油胶上面要用可卸封层（soak-off gel topcoat）？  
A. Hard gel will not cure over color. / 硬胶在色胶上无法固化。  
B. Soak-off topcoats cure without a lamp. / 可卸封层不用照灯。  
C. Hard gel tops can crack as nails flex. / 硬胶封层会在指甲弯曲时裂开。 ✅  
D. Soak-off topcoats leave no shine. / 可卸封层没有光泽。  
*Why:* After gel polish, the nail is still flexible. A traditional hard finishing gel could crack on top of the color when the nail bends, so use a soak-off gel topcoat.  
*为什么:* 涂了甲油胶后，指甲仍然有弹性。传统硬胶封层可能在指甲弯曲时在色胶上裂开，所以要用可卸封层。  
*Lock this (picture):* Paint a stiff coat over a rubber band, then stretch it: the stiff coat cracks.  
*记重点:* 在橡皮筋上涂一层硬漆，一拉就裂。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.358 (PDF 382)

**8. Which gel is thick and used to build an arch or extension?**  
哪种胶很稠，用来做甲拱（arch）或延长？  
A. Bonding gel / 结合胶（bonding gel）  
B. Building gel / 建构胶/延长胶（building gel） ✅  
C. Finishing gel / 封层胶（finishing gel）  
D. Gel polish / 甲油胶（gel polish）  
*Why:* Building gels are thick-viscosity gels for building an arch or extension. Bonding gel helps adhesion, finishing gel adds shine, and gel polish replaces lacquer.  
*为什么:* building gel（建构胶）是高黏度的胶，用来做拱形或延长。bonding gel（结合胶）帮助附着，finishing gel（封层胶）增加光泽，gel polish（甲油胶）用来代替普通甲油。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like a house: bonding gel is the foundation, building gel the walls, finishing gel the paint.  
*记重点:* 像盖房子：结合胶是地基，建构胶是墙，封层胶是外墙漆。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.356 (PDF 380), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.356 (PDF 380), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.358 (PDF 382), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.357 (PDF 381)

**9. Compared with nonself-leveling gel, a self-leveling gel**  
和非自流平胶相比，自流平胶（self-leveling gel）怎样？  
A. is thicker and can leave bumps. / 更稠，可能留下凹凸。  
B. adds more thickness per coat. / 每层加的厚度更多。

*为什么:* self-leveling gel（自流平胶）更流动，能让甲面平滑。nonself-leveling gel（非自流平胶）很稠，可能留下凹凸，但加厚更多。

*记重点:* 像倒进平底锅的煎饼面糊：自己就摊平了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.357 (PDF 381)

**10. Polymer gel, also called polygel, is a mix of**  
polymer gel（聚合物胶），也叫 polygel，是什么的混合物？  
A. polymer powder and hard gel. / 聚合物粉和硬胶。 ✅

*记重点:* acry-gel = 水晶粉（acrylic）+ 胶，装在一支管里。像挤牙膏一样挤出来，再用小铲切下。

**11. Why must gel be kept away from windows and table lamps?**  
为什么光疗胶要远离窗户和台灯？  
A. Light makes the gel runny. / 光会让胶变稀。  
B. Light fades the gel's color. / 光会让胶褪色。  
C. Light can cure it in the container. / 光会让胶在瓶里固化。 ✅  
D. Light gives gel a strong odor. / 光会让胶产生强烈气味。  
*Why:* Gels are light cured. If a gel container is left open near a window or lamp, the gel can harden in the container. Store gels in a cabinet, away from heat and light.  
*为什么:* 光疗胶靠光固化。胶瓶开着放在窗边或灯旁，胶就会在瓶里变硬。要把胶放在柜子里，远离热源和光。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Sunlight contains UV, the same kind of light many curing lamps use.  
*记重点:* 阳光里含有紫外线（UV），很多固化灯用的也是这类光。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.360 (PDF 384)

**12. Why should each finger be cured right after gel is applied?**  
为什么每个手指上完胶要马上照灯？  
A. Gel air-dries within seconds. / 胶几秒钟就会自然风干。  
B. Finger heat makes the gel runnier. / 手指的温度会让胶变稀。 ✅  
C. The lamp must stay warm. / 灯必须一直保持温热。  
D. Uncured gel turns yellow. / 未固化的胶会变黄。  
*Why:* Gel is temperature sensitive: thicker when cold and runnier when warm. Even finger heat affects it, so cure each finger right away, before gel runs onto the skin.  
*为什么:* 光疗胶对温度敏感：冷时稠，热时稀。连手指的温度都会影响它，所以每个手指上完胶要马上照灯，免得胶流到皮肤上。  
*Lock this (picture):* Butter on a warm plate softens and slides. Gel on a warm finger does the same.  
*记重点:* 黄油放在热盘子上会软化、滑开，胶在温热的手指上也一样。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.360 (PDF 384)

*为什么:* 甲油胶能保持约两周不掉色，适合一直戴同一个颜色的客人。经常换颜色的客人更适合普通甲油，因为很容易卸。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Gel polish is hard the moment it leaves the lamp, so she can grab her phone and keys right away.  
*记重点:* 甲油胶一出灯就硬了，客人马上就能拿手机、找钥匙。

**14. Which client is the poorest fit for hard gel extensions?**  
哪位客人最不适合做硬胶延长？

*记重点:* 参加毕业舞会（prom）或婚礼？Milady 推荐树脂系统（nail resin system）：戴几天后就能轻松泡掉。

**18. Why is the color layer of gel polish easy to remove?**  
为什么甲油胶的色胶层容易卸除？  
A. It has no photoinitiator. / 它不含光引发剂。  
B. Its cross-linking is only medium. / 它的交联程度只是中等。 ✅  
C. It is never cured in a lamp. / 它从来不需要照灯固化。  
D. It already contains acetone. / 它本身已经含有丙酮。  
*Why:* Gel polish color has a medium level of cross-linking, so it stays flexible and comes off easily. The tighter the cross-linking, the harder and stronger the gel.  
*为什么:* 甲油胶色胶层的 cross-linking（交联）程度中等，所以有弹性、容易卸除。交联越紧密，胶就越硬越牢。  
*Lock this (picture):* Cross-links form a net. A loose net lets acetone slip through; a tight net keeps it out.  
*记重点:* 交联像一张网：网眼松，丙酮能钻进去；网眼紧，丙酮就进不去。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.354 (PDF 378)
