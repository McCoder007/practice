# Set 6D — Polish and Add-On Services / 上甲油与附加服务

Manicure and Pedicure Services · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. How many coats of color go between the base coat and topcoat?**  
底油和亮油之间要涂几层彩色甲油？  
A. One / 一层  
B. Three / 三层  
C. Four / 四层  
D. Two / 两层 ✅  
*Why:* Milady's most successful application is four coats: base coat, two coats of color, then one topcoat. Building layers improves adhesion and staying power.  
*为什么:* Milady 认为最成功的上甲油方式共四层：底油（base coat）、两层彩色甲油、最后一层亮油（topcoat）。层层叠加能提高附着力和持久度。  
*Lock this (picture):* Sandwich: base coat and topcoat are the bread, two color coats the filling: 1 + 2 + 1.  
*记重点:* 三明治：底油和亮油是面包，两层彩色甲油是馅：1 + 2 + 1。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.159 (PDF 183)

**2. Why is a base coat applied before colored polish?**  
为什么上彩色甲油之前要先涂底油（base coat）？  
A. To add a glossy finish / 增加光泽  
B. To help polish adhere and prevent staining / 帮助甲油附着并防止染色 ✅  
C. To make polish dry faster / 让甲油干得更快  
D. To soften the cuticle / 软化死皮  
*Why:* A base coat helps colored polish adhere to the nail plate and keeps it from staining the nail. Red and dark colors are the most likely to stain.  
*为什么:* 底油能帮助彩色甲油附着在甲板上，并防止甲油把指甲染色。红色和深色最容易染色。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like primer under wall paint: it grips the surface and blocks stains.  
*记重点:* 就像刷墙前的底漆：抓牢表面，又能挡住染色。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.181 (PDF 205), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.152 (PDF 176)

**3. In the three-stroke method, where does the first stroke go?**  
三笔涂法（three-stroke method）中，第一笔涂在哪里？  
A. Down the center of the nail / 指甲正中间往下 ✅  
B. Along the left side / 沿左侧  
C. Across the free edge / 横过游离缘  
D. Along the cuticle line / 沿死皮线  
*Why:* Start at the center, push the brush back slightly to round the edge near the cuticle, then pull it down the middle. Then make one stroke on each side.  
*为什么:* 从中间下笔，先把刷子稍微往后推，让靠近死皮处的边缘呈圆弧，再沿中间往下拉。然后两侧各涂一笔。  
*Lock this (picture):* Stroke 1 is the nose, down the middle. Strokes 2 and 3 are the cheeks, one per side.  
*记重点:* 第一笔像鼻子：在脸的正中间往下。第二、三笔像两边脸颊，左右各一笔。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.159 (PDF 183), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.182 (PDF 206)

**4. Why should polish not touch the eponychium?**  
为什么甲油不能碰到甲上皮（eponychium）？  
A. It stains the skin permanently / 会让皮肤永久染色  
B. It slows nail growth / 会让指甲长得慢  
C. Skin oils can make the polish lift / 皮肤油脂会让甲油翘起 ✅  
D. It dries too fast there / 那里干得太快  
*Why:* Natural oils from the skin can make polish lift within a few days if the polish touches the skin. Leave a tiny, rounded area of unpolished nail at the back.  
*为什么:* 如果甲油碰到皮肤，皮肤的天然油脂会让甲油在几天内翘起。在指甲后端留一小圈圆弧形的空白。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* If polish touches skin, skin oil gets under its edge. Then the polish lifts within a few days.  
*记重点:* 甲油一碰到皮肤，皮肤油脂就会渗到甲油边缘下面。然后几天内甲油就会翘起来。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.159 (PDF 183), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.182 (PDF 206)

**5. Running the brush along the free edge to reduce chipping is called**  
用刷子沿游离缘刷一下以减少剥落，这叫什么？  
A. fanning. / 扇形压刷（fanning）  
B. beveling. / 倒斜边（beveling）  
C. capping. / 包边（capping） ✅  
D. feathering. / 羽化（feathering）  
*Why:* After the first coat, move the brush lightly back and forth on the very end of the free edge. This tip sealing, or capping, reduces chipping. Coat the free edge with topcoat too.  
*为什么:* 第一层涂完后，用刷子在游离缘最末端轻轻来回刷。这叫 tip sealing（封边）或 capping（包边），能减少剥落。亮油也要包到游离缘。  
*Lock this (word-link):* Capping = putting a cap on the tip, like the cap on a pen. The sealed tip chips less.  
*记重点:* capping（包边）= 给指尖戴上"盖子"，就像笔帽套在笔尖上。封好的指尖不容易剥落。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.183 (PDF 207)

**6. Why is base coat applied to the dominant hand first?**  
为什么先给顾客的惯用手涂底油？  
A. It has larger nails / 惯用手的指甲比较大  
B. Its polish needs extra coats / 惯用手要多涂几层  
C. Clients prefer it that way / 顾客喜欢这样  
D. It gets a head start on drying / 惯用手可以先开始晾干 ✅  
*Why:* Apply base coat to the dominant hand first, then place it in a cool dryer while you work on the other hand. The most-used hand gets a head start drying and smudges less.  
*为什么:* 先给惯用手（dominant hand）涂底油，涂完放进冷风烘干机，再做另一只手。最常用的手先开始晾干，就不容易弄花。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Your writing hand is the dominant hand. It does the most, so it gets the longest time to dry.  
*记重点:* 你写字的那只手就是惯用手（dominant hand）。它用得最多，所以要给它最长的晾干时间。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.182 (PDF 206)

**7. What is the minimum drying time for traditional polish?**  
传统甲油最少需要晾干多久？  
A. 1 minute / 1 分钟  
B. 10 minutes / 10 分钟 ✅  
C. 3 minutes / 3 分钟  
D. 60 minutes / 60 分钟  
*Why:* After polishing, seat the client at a separate table under a nail dryer or away from your station. Traditional polish needs at least 10 minutes; gel polish follows the maker's directions.  
*为什么:* 上完甲油后，请顾客到另一张桌子用烘干机，或坐在离你工作台远一点的地方。传统甲油至少要晾 10 分钟；凝胶甲油按厂家说明。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Base, two colors, topcoat: four wet layers. Give them time: 10 minutes at least.  
*记重点:* 底油、两层彩色甲油、亮油：一共四层湿的甲油。给它时间：至少 10 分钟。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.183 (PDF 207), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.139 (PDF 163), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.159 (PDF 183)

**8. How should a bottle of polish be mixed?**  
一瓶甲油应该怎么摇匀？  
A. Roll it upside down between the palms / 倒过来在两掌之间滚动 ✅  
B. Shake it hard for a minute / 用力摇一分钟  
C. Stir it with a wooden pusher / 用木推棒搅拌  
D. Tap it on the table / 在桌上敲一敲  
*Why:* Never shake polish. Shaking traps air that forms bubbles and makes the finish look rough. Gently roll the bottle upside down between your palms instead.  
*为什么:* 绝不要摇甲油。摇晃会把空气困在瓶里形成气泡，让涂出来的效果粗糙。应该把瓶子倒过来，在两掌之间轻轻滚动。  
*Lock this (picture):* Shake a soda and it foams. Shake polish and it fills with bubbles. Roll it instead.  
*记重点:* 摇一摇汽水，就会冒泡沫。摇一摇甲油，里面就全是气泡。所以要滚动，不要摇。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.151 (PDF 175)

**9. Before polish is applied, what should the client do?**  
上甲油之前，顾客应该先做什么？  
A. Put on jewelry and get car keys ready / 戴好首饰，准备好车钥匙 ✅  
B. Wash their hands again / 再洗一次手  
C. Take off all their rings / 把戒指全部摘下  
D. Soak the nails once more / 再泡一次指甲  
*Why:* Ask the client to put on jewelry and outerwear and get car keys ready before polishing. If possible, have them pay now too. This prevents smudging the fresh polish.  
*为什么:* 上甲油前，请顾客戴好首饰、穿好外套，准备好车钥匙。可以的话，先付款。这样能避免弄花刚涂好的甲油。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture wet polish meeting a zipper, a bag, or a ring. Do all of that before the color.  
*记重点:* 想象刚涂好的甲油碰到拉链、包包或戒指。这些事都要在上色之前做完。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.182 (PDF 206)

**10. In a French manicure, the curved line where the pink and white meet is the**  
法式美甲（French manicure）中，粉色和白色交界的弧线叫什么？  
A. free edge. / 游离缘（free edge）

C. smile line. / 微笑线（smile line） ✅  
D. stress area. / 受力区（stress area）

*为什么:* 传统法式美甲中，甲床部分是一种颜色（例如粉色），游离缘是另一种颜色（例如白色）。两色交界的弧线叫 smile line（微笑线）。  
*Lock this (picture):* The white tip curves up at both sides, like a smile.  
*记重点:* 白色指尖两侧向上弯，就像一个微笑。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.395 (PDF 419)

**11. A swirled look made by mixing wet polish colors with a stylus is**  
用点花笔（stylus）把未干的几种甲油颜色搅出漩涡效果，这叫什么？  
A. color blocking. / 色块拼接（color blocking）  
B. ombre. / 渐变（ombre）  
C. a French manicure. / 法式美甲  
D. marbleizing. / 大理石纹（marbleizing） ✅

*为什么:* marbleizing（大理石纹）是把两种或以上未干的颜色，用 stylus（点花笔，两端都是圆球头的工具）在指甲上搅出漩涡。  
*Lock this (picture):* Like the swirls in a marble countertop: colors pulled into each other.  
*记重点:* 就像大理石台面上的纹路：几种颜色互相拉扯交融。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.395 (PDF 419)

**12. Paraffin wax treatment should be avoided on a client with**  
哪种顾客应避免做蜡疗（paraffin wax treatment）？  
A. dry, rough skin. / 皮肤干燥粗糙  
B. impaired circulation. / 血液循环不良 ✅  
C. arthritis pain. / 关节炎疼痛  
D. callused hands. / 手上有老茧  
*Why:* Avoid paraffin on clients with impaired circulation or skin irritations such as cuts, burns, rashes, warts, or eczema. Dry skin, calluses, and arthritis pain are reasons clients enjoy it.  
*为什么:* 血液循环不良，或有割伤、烧伤、皮疹、疣、湿疹等皮肤问题的顾客，要避免蜡疗。皮肤干燥、老茧和关节炎疼痛反而是顾客喜欢蜡疗的原因。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Impaired = not working well. Most clients can have paraffin. Not with poor blood flow or broken skin.  
*记重点:* impaired（受损）= 不能正常工作。大多数顾客都可以做蜡疗。血液循环不良或皮肤有破损的人就不能做。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.165 (PDF 189), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.167 (PDF 191), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.166 (PDF 190)

**13. Why is a client's hand not dipped into the paraffin tub?**  
为什么不能把顾客的手直接伸进蜡疗机（paraffin tub）里？  
A. The wax would cool too fast / 蜡会冷得太快  
B. Dipping uses too much wax / 浸进去太费蜡  
C. It puts clients at risk of infection / 会让顾客有感染风险 ✅  
D. The tub is not hot enough / 蜡疗机温度不够  
*Why:* Dipping a client's hand into the tub puts clients at risk for infection. Scoop the paraffin out, for example into a bag, and throw away wax after it is used.  
*为什么:* 把顾客的手直接伸进蜡疗机，会让顾客有感染风险。要把蜡舀出来使用（例如装进袋子里），用过的蜡要丢掉。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like double-dipping a chip in a shared bowl: everyone after you shares your germs.  
*记重点:* 就像把咬过的薯片再蘸公用酱料：后面的人都会沾上你的细菌。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.166 (PDF 190), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.140 (PDF 164), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.167 (PDF 191)

**14. How is heat tolerance tested before a client's first paraffin treatment?**  
顾客第一次做蜡疗前，怎么测试能不能耐受温度？  
A. Drop a small wax patch on the skin / 在皮肤上滴一小片蜡 ✅  
B. Dip one fingertip into the tub / 把一个指尖伸进蜡疗机  
C. Check the tub's thermostat only / 只看蜡疗机的温控器  
D. Ask the client to touch the tub / 请顾客摸一下蜡疗机  
*Why:* Test heat tolerance on every client the first time. Drop a patch of wax about 1 inch (2.5 cm) across on the skin to see if the temperature can be tolerated.  
*为什么:* 每位顾客第一次做都要测试耐热度。在皮肤上滴一片直径约 1 英寸（2.5 厘米）的蜡，看顾客能否耐受这个温度。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like testing a baby's bottle on your wrist before feeding: try a little first.  
*记重点:* 就像喂奶前先滴几滴奶在手腕上试温：先试一小点。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.166 (PDF 190)

**15. When paraffin goes over a mask or lotion during a manicure, it is applied**  
修手中把蜡敷在面膜或乳液上时，在什么时候做？  
A. while the base coat is still wet. / 底油还没干的时候  
B. after the massage, before polishing. / 按摩之后、上甲油之前 ✅  
C. after the topcoat dries. / 亮油干了之后  
D. before the nails are filed. / 锉指甲之前  
*Why:* The paraffin goes over the lotion or mask to hold moisture in the skin. Lotion goes on during the massage, so paraffin comes after it, and the nails are cleaned before polish.  
*为什么:* 蜡敷在乳液或面膜上，是为了把水分锁在皮肤里。乳液是在按摩时涂上的，所以蜡疗要在按摩之后；之后把指甲清洁干净，再上甲油。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Paraffin before the manicure lets you polish right away, but then it can't go over lotion or a mask.  
*记重点:* 修手前先做蜡疗，结束后可以马上上甲油，但就不能敷在乳液或面膜上。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.167 (PDF 191)

**17. Why should overly abrasive scrubs be avoided?**  
为什么要避免使用颗粒太粗的磨砂膏？

**20. What does a paraffin wax treatment do for the skin?**  
蜡疗（paraffin wax treatment）对皮肤有什么作用？

*记重点:* 温热的蜡把手包起来，就像保鲜膜包住食物：水分跑不出去，皮肤就保持柔软。
