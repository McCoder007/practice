# Set 8A — Aftercare and Cleanup / 售后护理与清洁

Post-Service · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. What is the first step of the post-service procedure?**  
服务后流程（post-service procedure）的第一步是什么？  
A. Advise the client on home care / 告诉客人居家护理方法 ✅  
B. Clean and disinfect the table / 清洁消毒工作台  
C. Collect payment / 收款  
D. Disinfect the implements / 给工具消毒  
*Why:* Procedure 6-2 starts by advising the client on home maintenance and suggesting retail products that keep the service looking good until the next visit.  
*为什么:* Procedure 6-2（操作 6-2）第一步是告诉客人居家保养方法，并推荐能让服务效果维持到下次来店的零售产品。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Order: advise → thank → book and pay → record → clean up. People first, table last.  
*记重点:* 顺序：建议 → 道谢 → 预约和付款 → 记录 → 清理。先顾人，最后才收拾台面。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.175 (PDF 199), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.176 (PDF 200)

**2. Where do you record products used and retail recommendations?**  
用过的产品和零售推荐要记录在哪里？  
A. On the product’s SDS / 产品的 SDS 上  
B. In the disinfection logbook / 消毒记录本上  
C. On the client service form / 客人服务记录表上 ✅  
D. On the appointment card / 预约卡上  
*Why:* Record the service, products used, observations, and retail recommendations on the client service form or computer record.  
*为什么:* 把服务内容、用过的产品、观察到的情况和零售推荐，记录在 client service form（客人服务记录表）或电脑记录里。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Written today, read next visit: you’ll remember what worked, what to avoid, and what to sell.  
*记重点:* 今天写下，下次一看：就知道什么有效、什么要避免、该推荐什么。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.176 (PDF 200)

服务后流程中，带客人到前台是为了：

*为什么:* 谢谢客人之后，陪客人到前台（front desk）预约下次服务并付款，定好日期、时间和服务项目。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Book before they leave: once clients walk out, the next visit is easy to forget.  
*记重点:* 走之前就约好：客人一出门，下次就容易忘了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.176 (PDF 200), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.175 (PDF 199)

**4. After the client leaves, how do you prepare for the next client?**  
客人离开后，怎样为下一位客人做准备？  
A. Disinfect the area, then clear tools and trash / 先消毒台面，再收走工具和垃圾  
B. Clear tools, toss trash, clean and disinfect / 收走工具、丢掉垃圾、清洁并消毒 ✅  
C. Disinfect only the implements / 只给工具消毒  
D. Wipe the table with a dry towel / 用干毛巾擦桌子  
*Why:* Remove your products and tools, throw away all used materials, then clean and disinfect the work area. Clean and disinfect implements following Procedure 5-2.  
*为什么:* 收走产品和工具，丢掉所有用过的耗材，然后清洁并消毒（disinfect）工作区。工具按 Procedure 5-2（操作 5-2）清洁消毒。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like clearing the plates before wiping a table: clear, toss, clean, then disinfect.  
*记重点:* 就像先收盘子再擦桌子：收走、丢弃、清洁，最后消毒。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.176 (PDF 200)

**5. How often should clients be encouraged to get regular pedicures?**  
应该鼓励客人多久做一次常规足疗？  
A. Daily / 每天  
B. Monthly / 每月 ✅  
C. Twice a year / 每年两次  
D. Once a year / 每年一次  
*Why:* Feet are in constant use, so Milady says to encourage regular monthly pedicures. Regular foot care improves both appearance and basic foot comfort.  
*为什么:* 脚每天都在用，所以 Milady 建议鼓励客人每月做一次足疗（pedicure）。定期护理既让脚好看，也让脚更舒服。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Some salons sell 12-pedicure packages: one every month for a year.  
*记重点:* 有的店卖 12 次足疗套餐：一年里每月来一次。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.204 (PDF 228), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.207 (PDF 231)

**6. For heavy calluses, Milady suggests a series of**  
对于很厚的老茧，Milady 建议安排：  
A. weekly visits for four to six weeks. / 连续四到六周、每周一次的护理 ✅  
B. daily visits for one week. / 连续一周每天来  
C. one long session to remove it all. / 一次长时间护理全部去掉  
D. monthly razor callus shaving. / 每月用刀片削一次老茧  
*Why:* Fast callus removal is unsafe. Weekly callus reduction visits for four to six weeks lower the calluses; monthly pedicures then keep them at that level.  
*为什么:* 一下子去掉老茧（callus）不安全。连续四到六周、每周一次的去茧护理把老茧慢慢降低，之后每月做足疗维持。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like losing weight safely: a little each week, then monthly upkeep to keep it off.  
*记重点:* 就像健康减重：每周减一点，之后每月保养来维持。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.206 (PDF 230)

**7. A client’s feet need more than one pedicure can do. What should you do?**  
客人的脚需要的护理，一次足疗做不完。应该怎么做？  
A. Work past the scheduled time / 超出预约时间继续做  
B. Remove all the calluses today / 今天把老茧全部去掉  
C. Sell home care and rebook in 1–2 weeks / 推荐居家护理产品，1 到 2 周后再约 ✅  
D. Use a callus shaver to save time / 用削茧刀节省时间  
*Why:* Fixing neglected feet in one visit can leave them irritated or painful. Stay on schedule, sell home-care products, and book another service in one to two weeks.  
*为什么:* 脚况很差时想一次就弄好，可能会让脚受刺激或疼痛。要按预约时间做完，推荐居家护理产品（home-care products），并在 1 到 2 周后再约一次。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Two gentle visits beat one rough one. Home-care products keep improving the feet in between.  
*记重点:* 两次温和的护理，胜过一次粗暴的护理。中间靠居家护理产品继续改善。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.206 (PDF 230), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.205 (PDF 229)

**8. What should you warn clients about disposable pedicure slippers?**  
关于一次性足疗拖鞋，应该提醒客人什么？  
A. They must be returned. / 必须归还  
B. They will smudge polish. / 会蹭花指甲油  
C. They must be worn with socks. / 必须配袜子穿  
D. They can be slippery. / 可能会打滑 ✅  
*Why:* Single-use paper or foam slippers protect fresh polish, but they can be slippery. Warn clients to take care when walking or driving, and offer a helping hand.  
*为什么:* 一次性纸拖鞋或泡沫拖鞋（pedicure slippers）能保护刚涂的指甲油，但可能打滑。提醒客人走路或开车时小心，并主动扶客人一把。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Thin foam + a wet floor or a gas pedal → a slip, a fall, or a car accident.  
*记重点:* 薄泡沫底 + 湿地板或油门踏板 → 打滑、摔倒，甚至出车祸。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.200 (PDF 224)

**9. After each client, how long should disinfectant circulate in a whirlpool foot spa?**  
每位客人之后，消毒液要在漩涡足浴盆里循环多久？  
A. 1 minute / 1 分钟  
B. 10 minutes / 10 分钟 ✅  
C. 30 minutes / 30 分钟  
D. 1 hour / 1 小时  
*Why:* After scrubbing, rinsing, and refilling with clean water, add EPA-registered hospital disinfectant and circulate it for 10 minutes. Set a timer.  
*为什么:* 刷洗、冲净、重新注入清水后，在漩涡足浴盆（whirlpool foot spa）里加入 EPA 注册的医院级消毒剂，循环 10 分钟。要设定计时器。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Milady says to save the last 10 minutes of every pedicure appointment for the basin.  
*记重点:* Milady 说，每次足疗预约的最后 10 分钟要留给消毒足浴盆。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.222 (PDF 246), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.205 (PDF 229)

**10. After every whirlpool pedicure, which parts do you take off and scrub?**  
每次漩涡足疗后，要把哪些部件拆下来刷洗？  
A. The impeller covers / 叶轮盖 ✅  
B. The motor housing / 电机外壳  
C. The water supply hoses / 进水软管  
D. Only the outside cabinet / 只有外面的柜体  
*Why:* Remove the impeller covers and other removable parts. Scrub them, and the areas behind them, with liquid soap, a clean disinfected brush, and warm water to remove all residue.  
*为什么:* 拆下 impeller covers（叶轮盖）和其他可拆部件，用液体皂（liquid soap）、干净消毒过的刷子和温水，刷洗部件和它们后面的地方，去掉所有残留物。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Dirt hides behind the impeller covers, and a sticky germ layer (biofilm) can grow there.  
*记重点:* 脏东西藏在叶轮盖后面，那里还会长出一层黏黏的细菌膜（biofilm）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.221 (PDF 245), Milady Standard Foundations p.114 (PDF 609)

**11. What is circulated through foot spas at the end of every day?**  
每天营业结束时，要在足浴盆里循环什么？  
A. Polish remover / 洗甲水  
B. Cuticle oil / 指缘油  
C. Chelating detergent / 螯合清洁剂 ✅  
D. Callus softener / 软茧剂  
*Why:* At the end of every day, circulate chelating detergent for 5 to 10 minutes to break down stubborn films and pedicure product residue. Then drain, rinse, refill, and disinfect.  
*为什么:* 每天营业结束时，用 chelating detergent（螯合清洁剂）循环 5 到 10 分钟，分解顽固膜层和足疗产品残留。然后排水、冲洗、注水，再消毒。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* Its Chinese name uses the word for a crab's claw: it grabs hard-water minerals and breaks up stuck-on film.  
*记重点:* 螯 = 螃蟹的大钳子：螯合剂像蟹钳一样，夹住硬水里的矿物质，还能分解顽固的膜层。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.223 (PDF 247), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.224 (PDF 248), Milady Standard Foundations p.125 (PDF 620)

**12. Once a week, what is left in a whirlpool foot spa overnight?**  
每周一次，要把什么留在漩涡足浴盆里过夜？  
A. Chelating detergent / 螯合清洁剂  
B. Plain tap water / 普通自来水  
C. Foot soak solution / 泡脚液  
D. Disinfecting solution / 消毒液 ✅  
*Why:* Once a week, after the end-of-day cleaning, do not drain the disinfectant. Turn the unit off and leave it in overnight. In the morning, drain, rinse, refill, and flush.  
*为什么:* 每周一次，在每日收工清洁之后，不要排掉消毒液（disinfecting solution）。关掉机器，让消毒液留在里面过夜。第二天早上排水、冲洗、注水并冲洗管路。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Three times to remember: every client, 10 minutes; every day, chelating detergent first; every week, overnight.  
*记重点:* 三个时间点：每位客人后循环 10 分钟；每天收工先用螯合清洁剂；每周一次消毒液过夜。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.225 (PDF 249)

**13. In a basic foot tub, what do you do before adding disinfectant?**  
清洁普通足浴盆时，加消毒剂之前要做什么？  
A. Add foot soak to the water / 往水里加泡脚液  
B. Scrub, rinse, and refill with clean water / 刷洗、冲净，再注入清水 ✅  
C. Let it air-dry overnight / 让它自然风干一夜  
D. Spray it with alcohol / 喷酒精  
*Why:* Drain, scrub all surfaces with a clean disinfected brush and liquid soap, rinse and drain, refill with clean water, then add disinfectant for 10 minutes or as the label directs.  
*为什么:* 排水，用干净消毒过的刷子和液体皂（liquid soap）刷洗所有表面，冲净排水，注入清水，再加消毒剂，浸 10 分钟或按标签规定的时间。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Clean before you disinfect: leftover residue weakens the disinfectant, just as on implements.  
*记重点:* 先清洁后消毒：残留物会削弱消毒剂，跟消毒工具的道理一样。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.226 (PDF 250), Milady Standard Foundations p.119 (PDF 614)

**15. When is the best time to close a retail sale?**  
什么时候最适合促成零售产品的销售？  
A. During the consultation only / 只在咨询时  
B. Before the client sits down / 客人坐下之前  
C. At the end of the service / 服务结束时 ✅  
D. Never; wait for the client to ask / 不主动，等客人开口  
*Why:* Mention products while you use them. At the end of the service, place the item in the client’s hand and ask to add it to their ticket. That last step closes the sale.  
*为什么:* 做服务时边用边介绍产品。服务结束时把产品放到客人手里，问能不能加到账单上。最后这一步才能真正促成零售（retail）销售。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Suggest it early but skip the end, and the client often forgets by checkout.  
*记重点:* 只在开头提一下、结尾不跟进，客人到结账时常常就忘了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.129 (PDF 153)

**16. Enhancements that are not maintained on schedule are more likely to**  
不按时补甲（maintenance）的延长甲更容易：  
A. grow out faster. / 长得更快  
B. become thicker. / 变得更厚  
C. harden the natural nail. / 让真甲变硬  
D. lift, crack, or break. / 翘起、裂开或断掉 ✅  
*Why:* Liquid and powder enhancements need a fill every two to three weeks. Without it, they tend to lift, crack, or break, which can damage the natural nail and raise infection risk.  
*为什么:* 液体和粉末（liquid and powder）延长甲每两到三周需要补一次（fill）。不补的话容易翘起、裂开或断掉，会伤到真甲，也增加感染风险。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Clients who wait too long for a fill come back with many more breaks to repair.  
*记重点:* 等太久才来补甲（fill）的客人，回来时会有更多断甲要修补。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.330 (PDF 354), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.283 (PDF 307)

**17. Why do nails feel thin and bendy right after enhancement removal?**  
为什么刚卸完延长甲，指甲感觉又薄又软？  
A. The enhancement weakened them. / 延长甲把它们弄弱了  
B. They absorbed moisture while soaking. / 浸泡时吸了水分 ✅  
C. The primer ate into them. / 结合剂把它们腐蚀了  
D. Acetone dissolved the nail plate. / 丙酮溶解了甲板  
*Why:* After a 30-minute soak, the nail plate holds extra moisture, so it bends more. It is not weakened, and it firms up as the moisture leaves over the next 24 hours.  
*为什么:* 泡了 30 分钟后，甲板（nail plate）里多了水分，所以更容易弯。它并没有变弱，接下来 24 小时水分散掉后就会变硬。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Like hair after a wash: soft while wet, back to normal once dry. Tell clients so they don't worry.  
*记重点:* 就像刚洗完的头发：湿的时候软，干了就恢复正常。告诉客人，免得他们担心。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.332 (PDF 356), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.118 (PDF 142)

*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.126 (PDF 150), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.150 (PDF 174)

**19. At minimum, how often must your station’s trash container be emptied?**  
工作台的垃圾桶至少多久要清空一次？  
A. Once a week / 每周一次  
B. Once a month / 每月一次  
C. At the end of each workday / 每个工作日结束时 ✅  
D. Only when it is full / 只在装满时  
*Why:* Keep a lined trash container with a self-closing lid at your station. Close it when not in use, empty it at the end of each workday, and clean and disinfect it often.  
*为什么:* 工作台旁放一个套好垃圾袋、带自动合盖（self-closing lid）的垃圾桶。不用时盖好，每个工作日结束时清空，并经常清洁消毒。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Self-closing lid + daily emptying → less odor and fewer vapors in the salon air.  
*记重点:* 自动合盖 + 每天清空 → 店里异味少，挥发的蒸气也少。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.139 (PDF 163)

**20. After a paraffin treatment, the used wax should be**  
做完蜡疗后，用过的蜡应该：  
A. returned to the paraffin tub. / 倒回蜡疗机里  
B. saved for that client’s next visit. / 留给这位客人下次用  
C. strained and reused. / 过滤后再用  
D. thrown away. / 丢掉 ✅  
*Why:* Once paraffin touches a client, it is contaminated and must never be reused. Scoop clean wax from the tub into a bag, then discard it after the service.  
*为什么:* 蜡（paraffin）一旦用在客人身上就被污染了，绝不能再用。从蜡疗机里舀出干净的蜡装进袋子，服务后丢掉。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Used wax back in the tub → one client’s germs end up in everyone’s wax.  
*记重点:* 用过的蜡倒回机器 → 一个人的病菌，进了所有人的蜡。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.167 (PDF 191), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.141 (PDF 165), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.140 (PDF 164)
