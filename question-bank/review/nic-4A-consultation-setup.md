# Set 4A — Pre-Service: Consultation and Setup / 服务前：咨询与准备

Pre-Service Process · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. Setting up the table and greeting the client are part of the**  
布置工作台和迎接顾客属于哪个部分？  
A. service procedure. / 服务程序（service procedure）。  
B. post-service procedure. / 服务后程序（post-service procedure）。  
C. client consultation. / 顾客咨询（client consultation）。  
D. pre-service procedure. / 服务前程序（pre-service procedure）。 ✅  
*Why:* The pre-service procedure covers cleaning and disinfecting tools, setting up the table, and meeting, greeting, and escorting the client. The service and post-service come after.  
*为什么:* pre-service procedure（服务前程序）包括清洁消毒工具、布置工作台，以及迎接、问候并引导顾客。之后才是服务程序和服务后程序。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like a restaurant before you order: table set, tools clean, guest greeted and seated.  
*记重点:* 就像餐厅点菜前：桌子摆好、餐具洗净、客人迎进来并安排入座。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.155 (PDF 179)

**2. The client intake form is also called a**  
client intake form（顾客登记表）也叫什么？  
A. service record card. / 服务记录卡（service record card）。  
B. health history form. / 健康史表（health history form）。 ✅  
C. release form. / 免责声明表（release form）。  
D. product purchase record. / 产品购买记录（product purchase record）。  
*Why:* Every new client fills out an intake form, also called a client questionnaire, consultation card, or health history form. It lists medications, medical issues, and allergies that might affect services.  
*为什么:* 每位新顾客都要填写 intake form（登记表），也叫顾客问卷、咨询卡（consultation card）或健康史表。表上列出可能影响服务的药物、疾病和过敏情况。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Intake = what you take in at the start: the client's facts before any service.  
*记重点:* intake = “收进来”：服务开始前，先把顾客的资料收集进来。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.50 (PDF 545), Milady Standard Foundations p.51 (PDF 546)

**3. Who fills out the service record card?**  
谁来填写服务记录卡（service record card）？  
A. The nail technician / 做服务的美甲师 ✅  
B. The client / 顾客  
C. The salon receptionist / 沙龙前台  
D. The client's physician / 顾客的医生  
*Why:* The service record card is the client's permanent record of services, results, and products used. The technician who performs the service completes and updates it at each visit.  
*为什么:* service record card（服务记录卡）是顾客的永久记录，记载做过的服务、效果和用过的产品。由做服务的技师填写，每次来访都要更新。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Intake form: the client writes it before. Record card: you write it after.  
*记重点:* 登记表（intake form）：顾客服务前填；记录卡：技师服务后写。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.52 (PDF 547)

**4. How should paper client intake forms be stored?**  
纸质顾客登记表（intake form）应该怎样保存？  
A. At the front desk for easy access / 放在前台方便拿取  
B. In the nail station drawer / 放在美甲台抽屉里  
C. In a lockable, fireproof cabinet / 放在可上锁的防火柜里 ✅  
D. With the client to take home / 交给顾客带回家  
*Why:* Intake forms are legal documents and must be kept confidential. Lock paper forms in fireproof cabinets, and protect electronic forms with firewalls and passwords.  
*为什么:* 登记表是法律文件，必须保密。纸质表格要锁在防火柜里，电子表格要用防火墙和密码保护。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Lock it up, like a doctor's files about patients. Sharing a client's form can bring legal trouble.  
*记重点:* 像医生锁好病人的病历一样锁好。泄露顾客登记表可能惹上官司。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.52 (PDF 547)

**5. How often should you do a client consultation?**  
应该多久做一次顾客咨询（client consultation）？  
A. Only on the first visit / 只在第一次来时  
B. Before every service / 每次服务之前 ✅  
C. Once a year / 每年一次  
D. Only if the client asks / 只在顾客要求时  
*Why:* A consultation should be part of every service and every visit, done before the service starts. Changes can happen between visits, so record them on the service record card.  
*为什么:* 每次服务、每次来访都要在开始前做咨询。两次来访之间情况可能有变化，要记在服务记录卡上。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Even regulars get asked: a new medicine, allergy, or pregnancy may have started since last time.  
*记重点:* 老顾客也要问：上次之后可能开始吃新药、出现新的过敏或怀孕了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.54 (PDF 549), Milady Standard Foundations p.53 (PDF 548)

**6. Which step of the 10-step consultation is the most critical?**  
10 步咨询法中，哪一步最关键？  
A. Upsell: suggest added services / 追加推荐（Upsell）：建议加做服务  
B. Assess: find the client's needs / 评估（Assess）：了解顾客需求  
C. Show and tell: see styles they like / 展示（Show and tell）：看顾客喜欢的款式  
D. Repeat: review what you agreed on / 复述（Repeat）：回顾双方商定的内容 ✅  
*Why:* Step 10, Repeat, restates everything you agreed on, for example, "What I heard you say is…" It is the most critical step because it determines the final service.  
*为什么:* 第 10 步“复述”（Repeat）要把双方商定的内容再说一遍，例如“我听到您说的是……”。这一步最关键，因为它决定最终做什么服务。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Like a server reading your order back before it goes to the kitchen.  
*记重点:* 就像服务员下单前把你点的菜再念一遍。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.60 (PDF 555)

**7. Repeating in your own words what the client told you is called**  
用自己的话复述顾客说过的内容，叫作什么？  
A. reflective listening. / 反映式倾听（reflective listening）。 ✅  
B. a needs assessment. / 需求评估（needs assessment）。  
C. upselling. / 追加推荐（upselling）。  
D. client analysis. / 顾客分析（client analysis）。  
*Why:* Reflective listening means listening, then repeating in your own words what you think the client said. Don't interrupt, and ask the client to confirm you understood.  
*为什么:* reflective listening（反映式倾听）是先听，再用自己的话复述你理解的顾客意思。不要打断，并请顾客确认你理解得对。  
*Lock this (picture):* Reflect, like a mirror: the client's words come back to them. "So you want short, round nails?"  
*记重点:* reflect = 反射，像镜子一样把顾客的话照回去：“您是想要短的圆形指甲，对吗？”再请顾客确认。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.58 (PDF 553)

**8. When checking a client's nails and skin, a nail technician must never**  
检查顾客的指甲和皮肤时，美甲师绝对不能——  
A. record what they see. / 记录看到的情况。  
B. ask about health conditions. / 询问健康状况。  
C. diagnose a disease or disorder. / 诊断（diagnose）疾病或病症。 ✅  
D. refer the client to a physician. / 建议顾客去看医生。  
*Why:* Nail techs are licensed for cosmetic services, not to diagnose, treat, or prescribe. You may tell a client you see what may be an unhealthy condition and refer them to a physician.  
*为什么:* 美甲师的执照只允许做美容服务，不能诊断、治疗或开处方。你可以告诉顾客你看到了可能不健康的情况，并建议顾客就医。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Naming the disease is a doctor's job. You can say, "Please show this to a doctor."  
*记重点:* diagnose（诊断）= 说出是什么病，这是医生的工作。你可以说：“请给医生看看。”这叫 refer（转介）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.157 (PDF 181), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.104 (PDF 128)

**9. A condition that requires avoiding certain treatments or products is a**  
需要避免某些服务或产品的身体状况叫作什么？  
A. consultation. / 咨询（consultation）。  
B. scope of practice. / 执业范围（scope of practice）。  
C. service record. / 服务记录（service record）。  
D. contraindication. / 禁忌症（contraindication）。 ✅  
*Why:* A contraindication is a condition that requires avoiding certain treatments, procedures, or products to prevent unwanted side effects. Look for them on the intake form before services.  
*为什么:* contraindication（禁忌症）是指需要避免某些疗程、步骤或产品，以防出现不良反应的身体状况。服务前要在登记表上查看。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Contra = against, indicate = point out: the condition points against the service.  
*记重点:* contra = 反对，indicate = 指出：这个状况提示“不要做”这项服务。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.195 (PDF 690), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.161 (PDF 185)

**10. When booking a new client, how early should they arrive?**  
为新顾客预约时，应请顾客提前多久到达？  
A. 15 minutes before the appointment / 预约时间前 15 分钟 ✅  
B. 5 minutes before the appointment / 预约时间前 5 分钟  
C. 1 hour before the appointment / 预约时间前 1 小时  
D. Right at the appointment time / 正好在预约时间到  
*Why:* When booking, tell new clients the salon needs some information first (the client intake form), so they should arrive 15 minutes early. Also plan 5 to 15 minutes for the consultation.  
*为什么:* 预约时要告诉新顾客，服务前需要先填写顾客登记表（client intake form），所以要提前 15 分钟到。另外，日程里还要留出 5 到 15 分钟做顾客咨询（client consultation）。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like a first doctor visit: new patients are often asked to come 15 minutes early to fill out forms.  
*记重点:* 就像第一次去看医生：初诊病人常被要求提前 15 分钟到，先填表格。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.53 (PDF 548)

**11. If you are unsure whether a skin condition is safe to work on, require**  
如果不确定某种皮肤状况能否服务，应该要求顾客提供什么？  
A. a signed school release form. / 签好的学校免责声明表。  
B. a physician's release. / 医生许可（physician's release）。 ✅  
C. the salon manager's approval. / 沙龙经理的同意。  
D. a second consultation. / 再做一次咨询。  
*Why:* If you are in doubt about something you see, the client must get a physician's release before any salon service. Never work on unhealthy skin or nails without written physician permission.  
*为什么:* 对看到的情况有疑问时，顾客必须先拿到医生许可，才能接受任何沙龙服务。没有医生的书面许可，绝不处理不健康的皮肤或指甲。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like a coach asking for a doctor's note before an injured player returns.  
*记重点:* 就像受伤的球员要拿到医生证明，教练才让球员重新上场。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.77 (PDF 101), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.104 (PDF 128)

**12. Hand and arm massage is contraindicated for a client with**  
顾客有以下哪种情况时，禁止（contraindicated）做手部和手臂按摩？  
A. diabetes that is well controlled. / 控制良好的糖尿病。  
B. high blood pressure controlled by medication. / 用药控制良好的高血压。  
C. severe, uncontrolled hypertension. / 严重且未控制的高血压（hypertension）。 ✅  
D. dry skin on the hands. / 手部皮肤干燥。  
*Why:* Clients with high blood pressure, diabetes, or circulation problems can often have massage if stabilized and under a doctor's care. Severe, uncontrolled hypertension is a contraindication.  
*为什么:* 高血压、糖尿病或循环问题的顾客，如果病情稳定并有医生治疗，通常仍可按摩。严重且未控制的高血压则是禁忌症（contraindication）。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Hyper = over, as in hyperactive. Tension here means pressure: hypertension = blood pressure too high.  
*记重点:* hyper = 过度（hyperactive 过度活跃），tension 在这里指压力：hypertension 就是血压过高。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.161 (PDF 185)

**13. For a client with thin skin or poor clotting, you should avoid**  
顾客皮肤薄或凝血差时，应该避免——  
A. nipping the cuticles. / 用死皮剪剪死皮（nip the cuticles）。 ✅  
B. using a fine-grit abrasive. / 使用细砂锉（fine-grit abrasive）。  
C. removing dead tissue with a curette. / 用刮匙（curette）去除死组织。  
D. using gentle massage pressure. / 按摩时用轻柔的力度。  
*Why:* For these clients a small nick can quickly need medical care. Do not use sharp tools or nip cuticles. Use a curette to gently remove nonliving tissue and a fine-grit abrasive.  
*为什么:* 对这类顾客来说，一个小破口就可能需要就医。不要用锋利工具，也不要剪死皮。改用刮匙轻轻去除死组织，并用细砂锉。  
*Lock this (picture):* Thin skin is like wet paper: one small snip tears it, and the cut may not stop bleeding.  
*记重点:* 薄皮肤像湿纸：轻轻一剪就破，而且凝血差，出血可能止不住。所以别剪死皮。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.170 (PDF 194)

**14. Why look closely at the nails right after removing old polish?**  
为什么卸掉旧甲油后要马上仔细检查指甲？  
A. To see which polish brand was used / 看用的是哪个牌子的甲油

C. To decide the massage length / 决定按摩时间长短  
D. To check for hidden abnormalities / 检查被遮住的异常（abnormalities） ✅  
*Why:* In the basic manicure, remove all traces of old polish first. Then look closely at the nails for abnormalities the polish could have hidden.  
*为什么:* 基础修甲时，先把旧甲油彻底卸干净，再仔细检查指甲，看有没有被甲油遮住的异常。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like taking off sunglasses to check someone's eyes: remove the cover first.  
*记重点:* 就像摘下墨镜才看得清眼睛：先卸甲油，才看得到甲面问题。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.177 (PDF 201)

**15. After greeting a client, where do you take them first?**  
迎接顾客之后，先带顾客去哪里？

**16. In proper hand washing, rub lathered hands for at least**  
正确洗手时，起泡（lather）后至少要搓多久？  
A. 5 seconds. / 5 秒。  
B. 10 seconds. / 10 秒。  
C. 20 seconds. / 20 秒。 ✅  
D. 2 minutes. / 2 分钟。  
*Why:* Rub your hands together, all over and vigorously, until a lather forms. Keep going for at least 20 seconds, then rinse in warm running water.  
*为什么:* 双手用力搓遍各处，直到起泡，至少持续 20 秒，然后用温水冲洗。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Humming "Happy Birthday" twice takes about 20 seconds.  
*记重点:* 哼两遍《生日快乐歌》大约就是 20 秒。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.135 (PDF 630), Milady Standard Foundations p.137 (PDF 632)

**17. Why use liquid soap in a pump instead of bar soap?**  
为什么用按压瓶装的液体皂（liquid soap），而不用肥皂块（bar soap）？  
A. Bar soap can harbor bacteria / 肥皂块会滋生细菌 ✅  
B. Liquid soap kills all germs / 液体皂能杀死所有病菌  
C. Bar soap is too acidic / 肥皂块酸性太强  
D. Liquid soap needs no rinsing / 液体皂不用冲洗  
*Why:* Bar soap harbors bacteria and can become a breeding ground for pathogens. Milady recommends liquid soap in pump containers at the hand-washing station.  
*为什么:* 肥皂块容易藏细菌，会变成病原菌（pathogens）的温床。Milady 建议洗手区使用按压瓶装的液体皂。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture a wet, slimy soap bar sitting in a dish everyone touches.  
*记重点:* 想象一块湿滑的肥皂，放在人人都摸过的皂碟里。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.149 (PDF 173), Milady Standard Foundations p.117 (PDF 612)

**18. When may hand sanitizer replace hand washing?**  
什么时候可以用免洗洗手液（hand sanitizer）代替洗手？  
A. Before every service / 每次服务前  
B. When hands are visibly dirty / 手上明显有污垢时  
C. After every pedicure / 每次修脚后  
D. Only when hand washing isn't possible / 只有在无法洗手时 ✅  
*Why:* Sanitizers do not replace proper hand washing. Use them only when water is not available. They don't work on visibly dirty hands, and their alcohol dries the skin.  
*为什么:* 免洗洗手液不能代替正确洗手，只有在没有水时才用。手上有明显污垢时它不起作用，里面的酒精还会让皮肤变干。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Too much alcohol → dry, cracked skin → openings that let germs in.  
*记重点:* 酒精用多了 → 皮肤干裂 → 裂口让病菌趁虚而入。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.156 (PDF 180), Milady Standard Foundations p.117 (PDF 612)

**19. Before a service, clean and disinfect the manicure table with**  
服务前，应该用什么清洁消毒美甲桌？  
A. hand sanitizer gel. / 免洗洗手凝胶。  
B. isopropyl alcohol. / 异丙醇（isopropyl alcohol）。

D. plain warm water. / 清水（温水）。

*为什么:* 程序 6-1 要求按标签说明，用经 EPA 批准的消毒剂清洁消毒美甲桌和抽屉。皮肤抗菌剂（antiseptics）和酒精不能给表面消毒。  
*Lock this (real-acronym):* EPA = Environmental Protection Agency. A salon disinfectant must show an EPA registration number.  
*记重点:* EPA = 美国国家环境保护局。沙龙用的消毒剂标签上必须有 EPA 注册号。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.171 (PDF 195), Milady Standard Foundations p.118 (PDF 613)

*Lock this (real-life):* Tools on your working-hand side: you grab them without reaching across your own work.  
*记重点:* 工具放在惯用手那一侧：伸手就拿到，不用越过正在做的手。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.172 (PDF 196), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.173 (PDF 197)
