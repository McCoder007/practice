# Set 2D — Bones, Muscles, and Joints of Arms, Hands, Legs, and Feet / 手臂、手、腿和脚的骨骼、肌肉与关节

Anatomy and Physiology · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. The uppermost and largest bone of the arm is the**  
手臂最上面、最大的骨头是  
A. humerus. / 肱骨（humerus） ✅  
B. radius. / 桡骨（radius）  
C. ulna. / 尺骨（ulna）  
D. carpus. / 腕骨（carpus）  
*Why:* The humerus is the uppermost and largest bone of the arm. It runs from the elbow to the shoulder.  
*为什么:* humerus（肱骨）是手臂最上面、最大的骨头，从肘部一直延伸到肩部。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Squeeze your upper arm: one big bone from shoulder to elbow. The forearm has two: radius and ulna.  
*记重点:* 捏一捏上臂：从肩膀到手肘只有一根大骨头，就是 humerus（肱骨），“肱”就是上臂。前臂有两根：radius 和 ulna。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.26 (PDF 50)

**2. Which bone is larger at the elbow than at the wrist?**  
哪块骨头肘部一端比腕部一端粗？  
A. Radius / 桡骨（radius）  
B. Humerus / 肱骨（humerus）  
C. Carpus / 腕骨（carpus）  
D. Ulna / 尺骨（ulna） ✅  
*Why:* The ulna is the longer bone of the forearm. It is larger at the elbow than at the wrist and sits on the little-finger side of the hand.  
*为什么:* ulna（尺骨）是前臂较长的骨头，肘部一端比腕部一端粗，位于手的小指一侧。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Feel the point of your elbow: that knob is the ulna. It runs down to your little finger side.  
*记重点:* 摸摸手肘尖：那个骨突就是 ulna（尺骨）。它顺着前臂一直通到小指那一侧。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.26 (PDF 50)

**3. The forearm bone on the thumb side is the**  
前臂位于拇指一侧的骨头是  
A. ulna. / 尺骨（ulna）  
B. radius. / 桡骨（radius） ✅  
C. humerus. / 肱骨（humerus）  
D. metacarpus. / 掌骨（metacarpus）  
*Why:* The radius is the shorter of the two forearm bones. It is largest at the wrist and lies on the thumb side of the hand.  
*为什么:* radius（桡骨）是前臂两根骨头中较短的一根，腕部一端最粗，位于手的拇指一侧。  
*Lock this (real-life):* The radial nerve and radial artery also run on the thumb side. Take a wrist pulse there.  
*记重点:* radial nerve（桡神经）和 radial artery（桡动脉）也在拇指一侧。在手腕拇指侧就能摸到脉搏。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.27 (PDF 51), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.36 (PDF 60), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.40 (PDF 64)

**4. The carpus, or wrist, is made up of how many bones?**  
carpus（腕部，即手腕）由几块骨头组成？  
A. Five / 五块  
B. Seven / 七块  
C. Eight / 八块 ✅  
D. Fourteen / 十四块  
*Why:* The carpus is the wrist: a flexible joint of eight small, irregular bones held together by ligaments.  
*为什么:* carpus（腕骨）就是手腕：由八块不规则的小骨头组成、靠韧带连接的灵活关节。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Wrist: 8 carpals, in two rows of four. Foot: 7 tarsals. Don't swap the two numbers.  
*记重点:* 手腕：8 块腕骨，排成两排、每排四块。脚：7 块跗骨（tarsals）。这两个数字别记混。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.27 (PDF 51), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.28 (PDF 52)

**5. The bones of the palm of the hand are the**  
手掌的骨头是  
A. carpus. / 腕骨（carpus）  
B. phalanges. / 指骨（phalanges）  
C. tarsals. / 跗骨（tarsals）  
D. metacarpus. / 掌骨（metacarpus） ✅  
*Why:* The metacarpus forms the palm: five bones between the wrist bones and the finger bones.  
*为什么:* metacarpus（掌骨）是手掌的骨头：位于腕骨和手指骨之间的五块骨头。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Carpus = wrist. Meta-carpus = the bones after the wrist. Feel five long bones on the back of your hand.  
*记重点:* carpus = 腕，meta- = 在……之后：metacarpus 就是腕骨之后的掌骨。摸摸手背，能摸到五根长骨。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.27 (PDF 51)

**6. The bones of the fingers and toes are called**  
手指和脚趾的骨头叫什么？  
A. metacarpals. / 掌骨（metacarpals）  
B. phalanges. / 指骨/趾骨（phalanges） ✅  
C. tarsals. / 跗骨（tarsals）  
D. carpals. / 腕骨（carpals）  
*Why:* Phalanges are the bones of the fingers or toes, also called digits. Each foot has 14 of them.  
*为什么:* phalanges（指骨/趾骨）是手指或脚趾的骨头，也叫 digits。每只脚有 14 块。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Bend one finger: you see three parts. Each part is one phalanx. The thumb has only two.  
*记重点:* 弯一弯手指：能看到三节，每一节就是一块指骨（phalanx，复数 phalanges）。拇指只有两节。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.27 (PDF 51), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.28 (PDF 52)

**7. Muscles that straighten the wrist, hand, and fingers are the**  
把手腕、手和手指伸直的肌肉是  
A. extensors. / 伸肌（extensors） ✅  
B. flexors. / 屈肌（flexors）  
C. adductors. / 内收肌（adductors）  
D. pronators. / 旋前肌（pronators）  
*Why:* Extensors straighten the wrist, hand, and fingers to form a straight line. Flexors are involved in bending the wrist.  
*为什么:* extensors（伸肌）把手腕、手和手指伸直成一条线。flexors（屈肌）参与弯曲手腕。  
*Lock this (word-link):* Extend your arm and it goes straight; flex your arm and it bends. Same idea at the wrist.  
*记重点:* extend 手臂就是把它伸直，flex 手臂就是把它弯起来。手腕也是同样道理。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.31 (PDF 55)

**8. The larger of the two bones below the knee is the**  
膝盖下方两根骨头中较大的一根是  
A. fibula. / 腓骨（fibula）  
B. femur. / 股骨（femur）  
C. tibia. / 胫骨（tibia） ✅  
D. patella. / 髌骨（patella）  
*Why:* The tibia is the larger bone of the lower leg. You can see it as the bump on the big-toe side of the ankle.  
*为什么:* tibia（胫骨）是小腿较大的骨头，在脚踝大脚趾一侧凸起的那个骨包就是它。  
*Lock this (real-life):* The tibia is your shinbone: the hard front edge you bump on furniture.  
*记重点:* 胫骨就是小腿前面的“迎面骨”：常撞到家具的那条硬边。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.27 (PDF 51)

**9. Which muscle bends the foot up?**  
哪块肌肉把脚往上勾？  
A. Soleus / 比目鱼肌（soleus）  
B. Extensor digitorum longus / 趾长伸肌（extensor digitorum longus） ✅  
C. Peroneus brevis / 腓骨短肌（peroneus brevis）  
D. Abductor hallucis / 踇展肌（abductor hallucis）  
*Why:* The extensor digitorum longus bends the foot up and extends the toes. The soleus and peroneus brevis bend the foot down; the abductor hallucis moves the big toe.  
*为什么:* extensor digitorum longus（趾长伸肌）把脚往上勾并伸展脚趾。比目鱼肌和腓骨短肌把脚往下压；踇展肌活动大脚趾。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Extensors straighten. Pull your toes up toward your leg: toes straighten, foot bends up. Extensor digitorum longus does both.  
*记重点:* 伸肌（extensor）负责“伸直”。把脚趾往小腿方向勾起来：脚趾伸直，脚也往上勾。趾长伸肌（extensor digitorum longus）这两件事都做。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.32 (PDF 56), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.33 (PDF 57)

**10. Which foot bone forms the ankle joint with the tibia and fibula?**  
哪块足骨与胫骨、腓骨一起组成踝关节？  
A. Talus / 距骨（talus） ✅  
B. Cuboid / 骰骨（cuboid）  
C. Navicular / 舟骨（navicular）  
D. Calcaneus / 跟骨（calcaneus）  
*Why:* The ankle joint is made of three bones: the tibia and fibula from the leg, and the talus, or ankle bone, of the foot.  
*为什么:* 踝关节由三块骨头组成：来自小腿的胫骨和腓骨，以及足部的 talus（距骨，即踝骨）。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Talus = ankle bone, right under the tibia. Calcaneus = heel bone. Don't mix them up.  
*记重点:* talus（距骨）= 踝骨，就在胫骨正下方；calcaneus（跟骨）= 脚后跟的骨头。两个别搞混。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.28 (PDF 52)

**11. The heavy, long bone that forms the leg above the knee is the**  
构成膝盖以上腿部的又重又长的骨头是  
A. tibia. / 胫骨（tibia）  
B. fibula. / 腓骨（fibula）  
C. femur. / 股骨（femur） ✅  
D. patella. / 髌骨（patella）  
*Why:* The femur is a heavy, long bone that forms the leg above the knee. The tibia and fibula form the leg below the knee.  
*为什么:* femur（股骨）是构成膝盖以上腿部的又重又长的骨头。胫骨和腓骨构成膝盖以下的小腿。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Put a hand on your thigh: the femur is inside. Your shin is the tibia; your kneecap is the patella.  
*记重点:* 手放在大腿上：里面那根大骨头就是 femur（股骨）。小腿前面是 tibia（胫骨），膝盖骨是 patella（髌骨）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.27 (PDF 51), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.60 (PDF 84)

**12. Which is an example of an immovable joint?**  
下列哪一个是不动关节的例子？  
A. Elbow / 肘（elbow）  
B. Knee / 膝（knee）  
C. Hip / 髋（hip）  
D. Skull / 颅骨（skull） ✅  
*Why:* Joints connect bones. Movable joints include elbows, knees, and hips; immovable joints, like the skull or pelvis, allow little or no movement.  
*为什么:* 关节连接骨头。可动关节包括肘、膝、髋；不动关节，例如颅骨或骨盆，几乎不能活动。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Bend your elbow, knee, and hip: they move. Press on your head: the skull bones don't move.  
*记重点:* 弯一弯手肘、膝盖和髋部：它们都能动。按一按头：颅骨不会动。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.25 (PDF 49), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.26 (PDF 50)

**13. The part of a muscle that does not move is the**  
肌肉中不动的部分叫什么？  
A. origin. / 起点（origin） ✅  
B. insertion. / 止点（insertion）  
C. belly. / 肌腹（belly）  
D. ligament. / 韧带（ligament）  
*Why:* The origin is the fixed part of a muscle attached to the skeleton. The insertion is where more movement occurs, and the belly is the middle.  
*为什么:* origin（起点）是肌肉固定、附着在骨骼上的部分。insertion（止点）是活动较多的地方，belly（肌腹）是中间部分。  
*Lock this (word-link):* Origin = where it starts, like an anchor. In massage, pressure usually moves from insertion toward origin.  
*记重点:* origin = 起点，像锚一样固定。按摩时，压力通常从止点推向起点。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.30 (PDF 54)

**14. Muscles that separate the fingers are the**  
把手指分开的肌肉是  
A. adductors. / 内收肌（adductors）  
B. extensors. / 伸肌（extensors）  
C. abductors. / 外展肌（abductors） ✅  
D. pronators. / 旋前肌（pronators）  
*Why:* Abductors separate the fingers. Adductors, at the base of each finger, draw the fingers together.  
*为什么:* abductors（外展肌）把手指分开。adductors（内收肌）位于每根手指根部，把手指并拢。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Ab = away (as in absent); ad = toward (as in add). Abductors move fingers away from each other.  
*记重点:* ab = 离开（absent 缺席）；ad = 朝向（add 加上）。外展肌让手指彼此分开。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.31 (PDF 55)

**15. Which muscles turn the hand inward so the palm faces down?**  
哪种肌肉让手向内转、掌心朝下？  
A. Supinators / 旋后肌（supinators）  
B. Pronators / 旋前肌（pronators） ✅  
C. Abductors / 外展肌（abductors）  
D. Extensors / 伸肌（extensors）  
*Why:* Pronators turn the hand inward so the palm faces down. The supinator rotates the radius outward so the palm faces up.  
*为什么:* pronators（旋前肌）让手向内转、掌心朝下。supinator（旋后肌）让桡骨向外转、掌心朝上。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Hold your hand out, palm up. Turn it palm down: pronators do that. Supinators turn it back up.  
*记重点:* 手伸出来，掌心朝上；再翻成掌心朝下：这是 pronators（旋前肌）的动作。翻回朝上的是 supinators（旋后肌）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.31 (PDF 55)

**16. The large triangular muscle covering the shoulder joint is the**  
覆盖肩关节的大块三角形肌肉是  
A. bicep. / 肱二头肌（bicep）  
B. tricep. / 肱三头肌（tricep）  
C. pectoralis major. / 胸大肌（pectoralis major）  
D. deltoid. / 三角肌（deltoid） ✅  
*Why:* The deltoid is a large, triangular muscle over the shoulder joint. It lets the arm extend outward and to the side of the body.  
*为什么:* deltoid（三角肌）是覆盖肩关节的大块三角形肌肉，让手臂向外、向身体侧面抬起。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Lift your arm out to the side. Feel the triangle-shaped muscle that gets hard on your shoulder: deltoid.  
*记重点:* 把手臂向侧面抬起，摸摸肩膀：变硬的那块三角形肌肉就是三角肌（deltoid），中文名“三角”直接说出了形状。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.30 (PDF 54)

**17. Which nerve supplies the little-finger side of the arm and palm?**  
哪条神经支配手臂和手掌的小指一侧？  
A. Radial nerve / 桡神经（radial nerve）  
B. Ulnar nerve / 尺神经（ulnar nerve） ✅  
C. Median nerve / 正中神经（median nerve）  
D. Digital nerve / 指神经（digital nerve）  
*Why:* The ulnar nerve and its branches affect the little-finger side of the arm and palm of the hand, the same side as the ulna bone.  
*为什么:* ulnar nerve（尺神经）及其分支支配手臂和手掌的小指一侧，与尺骨在同一侧。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Bump your "funny bone" and your little finger tingles: that is the ulnar nerve at the elbow.  
*记重点:* 撞到手肘的“麻筋”，小指会发麻：那就是肘部的尺神经。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.36 (PDF 60)

**18. Which nerve supplies skin on the outer side and back of the foot?**  
哪条神经支配足部外侧和后面的皮肤？  
A. Sural nerve / 腓肠神经（sural nerve） ✅  
B. Saphenous nerve / 隐神经（saphenous nerve）  
C. Median nerve / 正中神经（median nerve）  
D. Deep peroneal nerve / 腓深神经（deep peroneal nerve）  
*Why:* The sural nerve supplies the skin on the outer side and back of the foot and leg. The saphenous nerve supplies the inner side.  
*为什么:* sural nerve（腓肠神经）支配足部和小腿外侧及后面的皮肤。saphenous nerve（隐神经）支配内侧。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* Sural runs down the back of your calf to the outer foot. Saphenous runs down the inner side.  
*记重点:* 中文“腓肠神经”的“腓肠”就是小腿肚：它管腿和脚的外侧、后侧。saphenous（隐神经）管内侧。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.37 (PDF 61)

**19. Which artery supplies the foot with blood?**  
哪条动脉为足部供血？  
A. Radial artery / 桡动脉（radial artery）  
B. Ulnar artery / 尺动脉（ulnar artery）  
C. Pulmonary artery / 肺动脉（pulmonary artery）  
D. Dorsalis pedis artery / 足背动脉（dorsalis pedis artery） ✅  
*Why:* The dorsalis pedis artery supplies the foot with blood. It continues from the anterior tibial artery, which runs down the lower leg.  
*为什么:* dorsalis pedis artery（足背动脉）为足部供血，它由沿小腿下行的胫前动脉（anterior tibial artery）延续而来。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* The Chinese name means "top of the foot." Radial and ulnar arteries go to the arm and hand.  
*记重点:* 看中文名：足背动脉的“足背”就是脚背。radial artery（桡动脉）和 ulnar artery（尺动脉）供应的是手臂和手。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.41 (PDF 65), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.40 (PDF 64)

**20. Which muscle attaches to the back of the heel and pulls the foot down?**  
哪块肌肉附着在脚跟后方、把脚往下拉？  
A. Tibialis anterior / 胫骨前肌（tibialis anterior）  
B. Extensor digitorum longus / 趾长伸肌（extensor digitorum longus）  
C. Gastrocnemius / 腓肠肌（gastrocnemius） ✅  
D. Abductor hallucis / 踇展肌（abductor hallucis）  
*Why:* The gastrocnemius is attached to the lower rear surface of the heel and pulls the foot down. The tibialis anterior, on the front of the shin, bends the foot upward.  
*为什么:* gastrocnemius（腓肠肌）附着在脚跟后下方，把脚往下拉。胫骨前肌（tibialis anterior）在小腿前面，把脚往上勾。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Stand on your toes and feel your calf get hard. That muscle pulls your foot down: gastrocnemius.  
*记重点:* 踮起脚尖，摸摸小腿肚：变硬鼓起的那块就是腓肠肌（gastrocnemius），它把脚往下压。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.32 (PDF 56)
