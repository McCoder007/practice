# Set 1A — Disease and How It Spreads / 疾病与传播途径

Infection Control and Safety Practices · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. Germs passed by shaking hands are an example of**  
通过握手传播病菌属于哪种传播方式？  
A. indirect transmission. / 间接传播。  
B. direct transmission. / 直接传播。 ✅  
C. airborne transmission. / 空气传播。  
D. respiratory droplet transmission. / 呼吸道飞沫传播。  
*Why:* Direct transmission means germs pass from person to person, for example by touching, shaking hands, or kissing.  
*为什么:* direct transmission（直接传播）是指病菌人传人，例如触摸、握手或亲吻。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* Direct = straight from one person to the next. No tool in between.  
*记重点:* 直 = 一条直线，不拐弯：病菌从人直接到人，中间没有工具。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.99 (PDF 594), Milady Standard Foundations p.100 (PDF 595)

**2. Germs passed on a dirty nipper are an example of**  
通过不干净的死皮剪（nipper）传播病菌，属于哪种传播方式？  
A. direct transmission. / 直接传播。  
B. airborne transmission. / 空气传播。  
C. indirect transmission. / 间接传播。 ✅  
D. respiratory droplet transmission. / 呼吸道飞沫传播。  
*Why:* Indirect transmission happens through a contaminated object, like a nipper, doorknob, or work surface. The next person who touches it can get the germs.  
*为什么:* indirect transmission（间接传播）是通过被污染的物品传播，例如死皮剪（nipper）、门把手或工作台面。下一个接触它的人就可能沾上病菌。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* Indirect = a tool or surface sits between the two people.  
*记重点:* 间接：间 = 中间。病菌通过中间的工具或台面传给你。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.100 (PDF 595)

**3. Which type of transmission spreads small, dry particles that hang in the air?**  
哪种传播方式会散播小而干、悬浮在空气中的颗粒？  
A. Airborne / 空气传播 ✅  
B. Respiratory droplet / 呼吸道飞沫传播  
C. Direct / 直接传播  
D. Indirect / 间接传播  
*Why:* Airborne particles are small and dry, so they hang in the air and travel farther. Respiratory droplets are larger and fall quickly.  
*为什么:* airborne（空气传播）的颗粒小而干，会悬浮在空气中并传得更远。respiratory droplet（呼吸道飞沫）颗粒较大，很快落下。  
*Lock this (picture):* Air-borne = carried by the air, like dust. Droplets drop fast, like rain.  
*记重点:* airborne（空气传播）像灰尘一样被空气带着飘。droplet（飞沫）像雨滴一样很快落下。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.101 (PDF 596)

**4. What is the body's first line of defense against infection?**  
身体抵御感染的第一道防线是什么？  
A. White blood cells / 白细胞  
B. Antitoxins / 抗毒素  
C. Body secretions / 身体分泌物  
D. Healthy, unbroken skin / 健康、完整的皮肤 ✅  
*Why:* Germs cannot get through healthy, unbroken skin. They get in through cuts, scratches, or rashes. So skin is first. Body secretions like sweat, white blood cells, and antitoxins are the next lines of defense.  
*为什么:* 病菌穿不过健康、完整的皮肤，只能从伤口、抓痕或皮疹进入。所以皮肤是第一道防线。body secretions（身体分泌物，例如汗液）、white blood cells（白细胞）和 antitoxins（抗毒素）是后面的几道防线。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Skin is the wall. White blood cells are the guards inside.  
*记重点:* 皮肤是城墙，白细胞是城里的守卫。病菌要先过城墙。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.113 (PDF 608)

**5. A disease that can spread from one person to another is called**  
能从一个人传给另一个人的疾病称为什么？  
A. a contagious disease. / 传染性疾病。 ✅  
B. an occupational disease. / 职业病。  
C. a local infection. / 局部感染。  
D. a systemic infection. / 全身性感染。  
*Why:* A contagious disease spreads from person to person. It is also called a communicable disease. Examples are the common cold, ringworm, and pinkeye.  
*为什么:* contagious disease（传染性疾病）会人传人，也叫 communicable disease（可传染疾病）。例如普通感冒、ringworm（癣）和 pinkeye（红眼病）。  
*Lock this (word-link):* Contagious sounds like "contact." It passes from person to person, like a cold. Also called communicable.  
*记重点:* contagious（传染性的）听起来像 contact（接触）。传染的“染”= 染色：病像染料一样，从一个人染到另一个人。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.104 (PDF 599)

**6. An illness caused by repeated overexposure to products at work is**  
因工作中反复过度接触产品而导致的疾病是什么？  
A. a contagious disease. / 传染性疾病。  
B. an occupational disease. / 职业病。 ✅  
C. a parasitic disease. / 寄生虫病。  
D. a viral disease. / 病毒性疾病。  
*Why:* An occupational disease comes from work conditions, such as long, repeated overexposure to certain products or ingredients.  
*为什么:* occupational disease（职业病）由工作条件引起，例如长期、反复过度接触某些产品或成分。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Occupation = your job. Same products, every workday, for years → occupational disease.  
*记重点:* 职业病：同样的产品，每个工作日都接触，日积月累就病了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.105 (PDF 600)

**7. Harmless bacteria that can do useful work are called**  
无害且能起有益作用的细菌称为什么？  
A. pathogenic. / 致病性细菌。  
B. staphylococci. / 葡萄球菌。  
C. mycobacteria. / 分枝杆菌。  
D. nonpathogenic. / 非致病性细菌。 ✅  
*Why:* Most bacteria are nonpathogenic. They do not cause disease. Some help make yogurt and cheese or help the body digest food.  
*为什么:* 大多数细菌是 nonpathogenic（非致病性）的，不会致病。有些能帮助制作酸奶和奶酪，或帮助身体消化食物。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Non = no, patho = disease, genic = makes. Nonpathogenic = makes no disease.  
*记重点:* 非致病性：非 = non（不），致 = genic（造成），病 = patho（疾病）。不造成疾病。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.106 (PDF 601)

**8. Where do bacteria multiply best?**  
细菌在什么地方繁殖得最好？  
A. Cool, bright, dry places / 凉爽、明亮、干燥的地方  
B. Warm, bright, dry places / 温暖、明亮、干燥的地方  
C. Warm, dark, damp, or dirty places / 温暖、阴暗、潮湿或肮脏的地方 ✅  
D. Cold, dark, clean places / 寒冷、阴暗、干净的地方  
*Why:* Bacteria grow fastest where it is warm, dark, damp, or dirty. Keep tools clean and dry, and keep the station clean.  
*为什么:* 细菌在温暖、阴暗、潮湿或肮脏的地方长得最快。工具要保持干净干燥，工作台也要保持清洁。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture a wet towel left in a dark drawer. That is where bacteria grow.  
*记重点:* 想象一条湿毛巾放在黑暗的抽屉里，细菌就在那里长。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.106 (PDF 601)

**9. Which is a sign of a bacterial infection?**  
以下哪一项是细菌感染的迹象？  
A. Dry skin / 皮肤干燥  
B. Pus / 脓 ✅  
C. A callus / 老茧  
D. A freckle / 雀斑  
*Why:* Pus is a fluid made of white blood cells, bacteria, and dead cells. When you see pus, there is a bacterial infection.  
*为什么:* pus（脓）是由白细胞、细菌和死细胞组成的液体。看到脓，就说明有细菌感染。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Pus is the mess left after white blood cells fight bacteria. See pus, think bacteria.  
*记重点:* 脓是白细胞和细菌打完仗留下的残局。看到脓，就想到细菌。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.106 (PDF 601)

**10. Redness, heat, pain, and swelling are signs of**  
发红、发热、疼痛和肿胀是什么的迹象？  
A. inflammation. / 炎症。 ✅  
B. contamination. / 污染。  
C. decontamination. / 去污染。  
D. immunity. / 免疫。  
*Why:* Inflammation is how body tissue reacts to injury, irritation, or infection. It shows as redness, heat, pain, and swelling.  
*为什么:* inflammation（炎症）是身体组织对受伤、刺激或感染的反应，表现为发红、发热、疼痛和肿胀。  
*Lock this (word-link):* Inflammation looks like "flame." A flame is red and hot, like sore, swollen skin.  
*记重点:* inflammation 里藏着 flame（火焰）。炎 = 两个火：炎症就像身体里着了火，红、热、痛、肿。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.106 (PDF 601)

**11. The body's ability to resist and destroy pathogens is called**  
身体抵抗并消灭病原体的能力称为什么？  
A. infection. / 感染。  
B. inflammation. / 炎症。  
C. contamination. / 污染。  
D. immunity. / 免疫。 ✅  
*Why:* Immunity is the body's power to fight off germs, so you stay well. A vaccine, like a flu shot, builds acquired immunity. Infection is germs getting in. Inflammation is red, hot swelling.  
*为什么:* immunity（免疫）是身体抵抗病菌、让你保持健康的能力。打疫苗（比如流感疫苗）能获得 acquired immunity（获得性免疫）。infection（感染）是病菌进入身体。inflammation（发炎）是红、热、肿。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* The Chinese name means "avoid sickness." Your body fights germs, so you do not get sick.  
*记重点:* immunity = 免疫：免（免除）+ 疫（瘟疫），免于疫病。身体自己打败病菌，你就不生病。疫苗的“疫”就是这个字。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.79 (PDF 574), Milady Standard Foundations p.80 (PDF 575), Milady Standard Foundations p.99 (PDF 594), Milady Standard Foundations p.105 (PDF 600), Milady Standard Foundations p.106 (PDF 601)

**12. MRSA is caused by**  
MRSA 是由什么引起的？  
A. a virus. / 病毒。  
B. a fungus. / 真菌。  
C. staph bacteria. / 葡萄球菌（细菌）。 ✅  
D. a parasite. / 寄生虫。  
*Why:* MRSA is a staph bacteria infection that many antibiotics cannot kill. It often starts as pimples, rashes, or boils on the skin.  
*为什么:* MRSA 是一种 staph（葡萄球菌）细菌感染，很多抗生素都杀不死它。它常以皮肤上的痘痘、皮疹或疖子开始。  
*Lock this (real-acronym):* Read the letters: R = Resistant (antibiotics fail), S = Staph.  
*记重点:* 看字母：R = Resistant（耐药，抗生素没用），S = Staph（葡萄球菌）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.107 (PDF 602)

**13. Mycobacterium infections have been linked especially to**  
分枝杆菌感染尤其与什么有关？  
A. pedicure bowls. / 足浴盆。 ✅  
B. nail polish. / 指甲油。  
C. UV lamps. / UV 灯。  
D. hand lotion. / 护手霜。  
*Why:* Mycobacterium lives in soil and water, so it can be in the water supply. Clean and disinfect every pedicure bowl to protect clients.  
*为什么:* mycobacterium（分枝杆菌）存在于土壤和水中，所以自来水里也可能有。每个足浴盆都要清洁并消毒，保护顾客。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Soil and water → tap water → pedicure bowl. Germ in the water, risk in the bowl.  
*记重点:* 土壤和水 → 自来水 → 足浴盆。水里有菌，盆里就有风险。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.108 (PDF 603)

**14. Contagious diseases are most often spread through**  
传染性疾病最常通过什么传播？  
A. hair. / 头发。  
B. eyeglasses. / 眼镜。  
C. shoes. / 鞋子。  
D. dirty hands. / 脏手。 ✅  
*Why:* Contagious diseases spread most often by dirty hands. Germs hide under the fingernails and in the webs between the fingers. Many states require hand washing before every client.  
*为什么:* 传染性疾病最常通过脏手传播。病菌藏在指甲下面和手指之间的指蹼里。很多州规定每位顾客服务前都要洗手。  
*Lock this (real-life):* What touches every client? Your hands. Scrub under the nails and between the fingers.  
*记重点:* 什么会碰到每一位顾客？你的手。要刷洗指甲下面和手指之间。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.104 (PDF 599)

**15. Which can reproduce only by taking over a host cell?**  
哪一种只能通过接管宿主细胞来繁殖？  
A. Bacteria / 细菌  
B. Viruses / 病毒 ✅  
C. Fungi / 真菌  
D. Mildew / 霉（mildew）  
*Why:* A virus must get inside a living cell to make copies of itself. Bacteria can live and reproduce on their own.  
*为什么:* virus（病毒）必须进入活细胞才能复制自己。bacteria（细菌）可以自己生存和繁殖。  
*Lock this (comparison):* A virus has no copy machine of its own. It takes over your cell's. Bacteria have their own.  
*记重点:* 病毒自己没有复印机，要占用你细胞的复印机。细菌有自己的。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.109 (PDF 604)

**16. Which can usually be treated with antibiotics?**  
哪一种通常可以用抗生素治疗？  
A. Viral infections / 病毒感染  
B. Head lice / 头虱  
C. Bacterial infections / 细菌感染 ✅  
D. Influenza / 流感  
*Why:* Antibiotics work on bacteria. They do not work on viruses, such as the flu.  
*为什么:* antibiotics（抗生素）对细菌有效，对 virus（病毒）无效，例如流感。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Cold or flu (influenza)? Antibiotics will not help. Those are viruses.  
*记重点:* 感冒或流感（influenza）？抗生素没用，因为它们是病毒。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.109 (PDF 604)

**17. Hepatitis and HIV are examples of**  
肝炎和 HIV 属于哪一类？  
A. bloodborne pathogens. / 血源性病原体。 ✅  
B. fungi. / 真菌。  
C. parasites. / 寄生虫。  
D. nonpathogenic bacteria. / 非致病性细菌。  
*Why:* Bloodborne pathogens are germs carried in blood or body fluids, such as hepatitis and HIV. They can spread when skin is broken, so avoid cutting the client's skin.  
*为什么:* bloodborne pathogens（血源性病原体）是存在于血液或体液中的病菌，例如 hepatitis（肝炎）和 HIV。皮肤破损时就可能传播，所以要避免割伤顾客皮肤。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Blood-borne = carried by blood. HIV and hepatitis travel in blood; broken skin opens the door.  
*记重点:* 血源性 = 病菌由血液带着走。HIV 和肝炎都在血里，皮肤一破，门就开了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.110 (PDF 605), Milady Standard Foundations p.111 (PDF 606)

**18. Sticky colonies of microbes on surfaces are called**  
附着在表面、有黏性的微生物群落称为什么？  
A. toxins. / 毒素。  
B. biofilms. / 生物膜。 ✅  
C. viruses. / 病毒。  
D. antitoxins. / 抗毒素。  
*Why:* A biofilm is a group of germs stuck together under a sticky coating. Disinfectants have trouble getting through it. Foot spas, especially piped ones, can hold biofilm.  
*为什么:* biofilm（生物膜）是一群被黏性保护层包住、粘在一起的微生物，消毒剂很难穿透。足浴盆，尤其是带管道的，可能藏有生物膜。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Bio = living, film = thin layer. Think of the slime in a drain.  
*记重点:* 生物膜 = 生物 + 膜（薄薄一层）。想想下水道里那层滑滑的东西。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.113 (PDF 608), Milady Standard Foundations p.114 (PDF 609)

**19. Ringworm is caused by**  
癣是由什么引起的？  
A. a worm. / 蠕虫。  
B. a virus. / 病毒。  
C. bacteria. / 细菌。  
D. a fungus. / 真菌。 ✅  
*Why:* Despite its name, ringworm is not a worm. It is a contagious fungal skin infection that makes round, ring-shaped patches.  
*为什么:* ringworm（癣）虽然名字里有 worm（虫），但它不是虫，而是一种会传染的真菌性皮肤感染，会形成圆形的环状斑块。  
*Lock this (name-trap):* The name lies: no worm. A fungus grows in a ring.  
*记重点:* 名字骗人：没有虫。“癣”是疒字旁（病），不是虫字旁。是真菌长成一个圈。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.112 (PDF 607)

**20. Scabies is caused by**  
疥疮是由什么引起的？  
A. a fungus. / 真菌。  
B. a virus. / 病毒。  
C. the itch mite. / 疥螨。 ✅  
D. bacteria. / 细菌。  
*Why:* Scabies is a contagious skin disease caused by the itch mite, a parasite that burrows under the skin. Do not serve the client; refer them to a doctor.  
*为什么:* scabies（疥疮）是由 itch mite（疥螨）引起的传染性皮肤病。疥螨是一种会钻到皮肤下面的寄生虫。不要为这位顾客服务，应建议对方去看医生。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture a tiny bug digging a tunnel under the skin. That bug is the itch mite: scabies.  
*记重点:* 想象一只小虫在皮肤下面挖隧道：这就是疥螨（itch mite）。“螨”是虫字旁，是小虫，不是真菌、病毒或细菌。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.113 (PDF 608)
