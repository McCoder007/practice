# Set 5B — Implements and Safe Use / 工具与安全使用

Nail Service Tools · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. Why are multiuse implements usually made of stainless steel?**  
为什么可重复使用的工具（multiuse implements）通常用不锈钢做？  
A. They can be disinfected without corroding. / 消毒时不会腐蚀生锈 ✅  
B. They only need rinsing between clients. / 顾客之间只需冲洗一下  
C. They cost less than nickel-plated tools. / 比镀镍工具便宜  
D. They can be thrown away after use. / 用完可以扔掉  
*Why:* Multiuse implements must be cleaned and disinfected before the next client, and stainless steel holds up. Less expensive nickel-plated implements corrode during disinfection and sterilization.  
*为什么:* 可重复使用的工具在下一位顾客前必须清洁消毒，不锈钢经得起。较便宜的镀镍（nickel-plated）工具在消毒和灭菌时会腐蚀。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Stain + less (= without): steel that will not get rust spots. The Chinese name just says "no rust."  
*记重点:* stain（污斑）+ less（没有）= 不生锈斑；中文“不锈钢”说得更直接：不锈 = 不生锈。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.142 (PDF 166)

**2. A metal pusher is designed to**  
钢推（metal pusher）是用来做什么的？  
A. cut away live skin at the nail fold. / 剪掉甲沟（nail fold）上的活皮  
B. gently scrape cuticle tissue off the nail plate. / 轻轻刮掉甲面上的死皮（cuticle）组织 ✅  
C. shorten the free edge quickly. / 快速剪短指甲前缘（free edge）  
D. lift implements out of disinfectant. / 把工具从消毒液里夹出来  
*Why:* A stainless-steel metal pusher is designed to gently scrape cuticle tissue from the nail plate. Used roughly, it can groove the plate, damage the matrix, or make tiny skin openings that let microbes in.  
*为什么:* 不锈钢钢推用来轻轻刮掉甲面上的死皮组织。用力过猛会在甲面刮出沟槽、伤到甲母（matrix），或在皮肤上造成细小伤口，让微生物进入。  
*Lock this (picture):* Hold it like a pencil, tipped low at 20 to 30 degrees: scrape, never dig.  
*记重点:* 像握铅笔一样握住，压低到 20–30 度：是刮，不是挖。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.142 (PDF 166)

**3. When holding nippers, where does your index finger go?**  
握死皮剪时，食指应该放在哪里？  
A. On the box joint / 放在剪轴（box joint）上 ✅  
B. On the tips of the blades / 放在刀刃尖上  
C. Around the same handle as the thumb / 和拇指握同一边手柄  
D. Curled under the lower handle / 勾在下边手柄下面  
*Why:* Hold your thumb around one handle and three fingers around the other, with the blades facing the nail plate. The index finger rests on the box joint to control and guide the blade.  
*为什么:* 拇指握住一边手柄，另外三根手指握住另一边，刀刃朝向甲面。食指放在剪轴（box joint）上，控制并引导刀刃。  
*Lock this (picture):* The box joint is like the middle of scissors, where the two halves cross. Your index finger steadies it.  
*记重点:* box joint（剪轴）就像剪刀中间两片交叉、转动的那个点。食指压稳这个点。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.143 (PDF 167)

**4. Why should nail nippers always be kept sharp?**  
为什么死皮剪要一直保持锋利？  
A. So they can cut live skin cleanly / 这样才能干净利落地剪活皮  
B. So they need disinfecting less often / 这样就不用常消毒  
C. To prevent ripping and tearing live tissue / 防止撕扯到活组织 ✅  
D. So they can trim the eponychium / 这样能修剪甲上皮（eponychium）  
*Why:* Always keep a sharp edge on your nippers to prevent accidental ripping and tearing into live tissue. Nippers only trim dead skin; live nail fold tissue keeps out microbes.  
*为什么:* 死皮剪要保持刃口锋利，防止不小心撕扯到活组织。死皮剪只修死皮；甲沟（nail fold）的活组织能挡住微生物。  
*Lock this (picture):* Dull scissors chew and tear paper; sharp ones snip clean. Dull nippers tug dead skin and tear live skin.  
*记重点:* 钝剪刀剪纸会把纸扯毛；快剪刀一刀就干净。钝死皮剪会拉扯死皮，连带撕伤活皮。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.142 (PDF 166), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.143 (PDF 167)

**5. When cleaning and disinfecting nippers, take special care to**  
清洁和消毒死皮剪时，要特别注意什么？  
A. keep them tightly closed. / 把它紧紧合上  
B. soak only the blades. / 只泡刀刃部分  
C. rinse them in hot water only. / 只用热水冲洗  
D. open the hinges. / 把剪子张开，露出剪轴（hinge） ✅  
*Why:* Nippers must be cleaned and disinfected before every client. Open the hinges so cleaner and disinfectant reach every surface, and fully immerse the whole implement, handles included.  
*为什么:* 每位顾客前都要清洁消毒死皮剪。把剪子张开，让清洁剂和消毒液接触到剪轴里外每个表面，并把整件工具（包括手柄）完全浸没。  
*Lock this (picture):* A closed hinge is a hidden pocket. Dirt inside keeps the disinfectant away from the germs.  
*记重点:* 合着的剪轴是个暗袋。藏在里面的碎屑会挡住消毒液，让病菌躲过去。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.143 (PDF 167), Milady Standard Foundations p.119 (PDF 614)

**6. Which implement can lift implements out of disinfectant?**  
哪种工具可以把其他工具从消毒液里夹出来？  
A. Tweezers / 镊子（tweezers） ✅  
B. Nail clippers / 指甲钳  
C. Curette / 刮匙（curette）  
D. Metal pusher / 钢推  
*Why:* Tweezers lift debris from the nail plate, place nail art, and remove implements from disinfectant. They must be stainless steel and disinfected before use on any client.  
*为什么:* 镊子可以夹走甲面碎屑、放置美甲饰品，也能把工具从消毒液里夹出来。镊子必须是不锈钢的，每位顾客使用前都要消毒。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Disinfectant must never touch your skin. Tweezers or tongs keep your fingers out of the jar.  
*记重点:* 消毒液绝不能碰到皮肤。用镊子或夹子，手指就不用伸进消毒缸。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.143 (PDF 167), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.237 (PDF 261)

**7. How are toenail clippers different from fingernail clippers?**  
脚指甲钳（toenail clippers）和手指甲钳有什么不同？  
A. They are single-use plastic. / 是一次性塑料的  
B. They are larger, with a wider jaw opening. / 更大，钳口张开更宽 ✅  
C. They are used to trim dead skin. / 是用来修死皮的  
D. They need no disinfecting. / 不需要消毒  
*Why:* Toenail clippers are larger and have a wider space between the jaws, so they can cut thicker nails. Use professional stainless-steel clippers, and clean and disinfect them after each use.  
*为什么:* 脚指甲钳更大，钳口之间空间更宽，能剪更厚的指甲。要用专业不锈钢指甲钳，每次用后清洁消毒。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Feel the tip of your big toenail, then your thumbnail: the toenail is thicker, so its clippers open wider.  
*记重点:* 摸一摸大脚趾甲的前端，再摸摸拇指指甲：脚指甲更厚，所以脚指甲钳（toenail clippers）的钳口张得更开。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.197 (PDF 221), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.198 (PDF 222)

**8. Which grit is used to shorten and shape natural nails?**  
修短、修形真甲用哪种目数的锉条？  
A. Coarse grit / 粗目数（coarse）  
B. Fine grit / 细目数（fine）  
C. Medium grit / 中目数（medium） ✅  
D. A no-grit shine surface / 无砂粒的抛光面  
*Why:* Medium-grit abrasives smooth and refine surfaces and are used to shorten and shape natural nails. Finish with a fine grit to smooth the edge. Coarse grits are generally not used on natural nails.  
*为什么:* 中目数锉条用来打磨、修整表面，也用来修短、修形真甲。最后用细目数把边缘磨顺。粗目数一般不用在真甲上。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Coarse is too rough for natural nails. Fine is for polishing. Medium is the middle: it shortens and shapes.  
*记重点:* 粗目数（coarse）对真甲太粗。细目数（fine）用来抛光。中目数（medium）在中间：用来修短、修形。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.147 (PDF 171), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.177 (PDF 201), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.199 (PDF 223)

**9. What is a curette used for?**  
刮匙（curette）是用来做什么的？  
A. Smoothing thick heel calluses / 磨平脚跟的厚茧  
B. Removing debris from the nail folds / 清除甲沟（nail folds）里的污垢 ✅  
C. Shortening thick toenails / 剪短厚脚指甲  
D. Applying cuticle oil to the nail / 给指甲涂营养油  
*Why:* A curette has a small scoop-shaped end for removing debris from the nail folds, eponychium, and hyponychium areas. It is ideal around the edges of the big toenail.  
*为什么:* 刮匙一头是小勺形，用来清除甲沟（nail folds）、甲上皮（eponychium）和甲下皮（hyponychium）周围的污垢，特别适合大脚趾甲边缘。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* The Chinese name means "scraping spoon": a tiny spoon that scrapes dirt out of the nail folds.  
*记重点:* 刮匙：刮 = 刮除，匙 = 勺子，就是一把刮污垢的小勺，用来清理甲沟（nail folds）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.198 (PDF 222)

**10. What is a wooden pusher used for?**  
木棒（wooden pusher）是用来做什么的？  
A. Removing cuticle from the nail plate / 推除甲面上的死皮 ✅  
B. Trimming loose tags of dead skin / 剪掉翘起的小死皮  
C. Shortening the free edge / 剪短指甲前缘  
D. Smoothing calluses on the feet / 磨平脚上的茧  
*Why:* The wooden pusher removes dead cuticle tissue from the nail plate, cleans under the free edge, and applies products on a cotton-wrapped tip. Hold it like a pencil at 20 to 30 degrees.  
*为什么:* 木棒用来推掉甲面上的死皮组织、清理前缘下方，头上裹棉花还能涂产品。像握铅笔一样，以 20–30 度角拿着。  
*Lock this (contrast):* It pushes, it doesn't cut: the wooden pusher moves cuticle off the plate. Trimming dead skin is the nippers' job.  
*记重点:* 它是推，不是剪：木棒（wooden pusher）把死皮（cuticle）从甲面推掉。剪掉死皮是死皮剪（nippers）的活。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.144 (PDF 168), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.180 (PDF 204), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.198 (PDF 222)

**11. The narrow metal nail rasp is used to**  
窄窄的金属甲沟锉（nail rasp）是用来做什么的？  
A. treat a toenail that is ingrown. / 治疗嵌甲（ingrown nail）  
B. file the top surface of the nail. / 锉指甲的上表面  
C. push back the cuticle. / 推死皮  
D. file the sides of the free edge. / 锉前缘的两侧 ✅  
*Why:* A nail rasp is a metal file that files in one direction. It smooths the sides of the free edge to help prevent ingrown nails. Never use it on ingrown nails or the nail top.  
*为什么:* 甲沟锉是只朝一个方向锉的金属锉，用来磨平前缘两侧，预防嵌甲。不能用在已经嵌甲的指甲上，也不能锉甲面。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Sharp side edge → grows into the skin → ingrown nail. Rasp it smooth first; already ingrown? Podiatrist.  
*记重点:* 前缘侧边尖 → 长进肉里 → 嵌甲。先用甲沟锉磨平；已经嵌甲？交给足科医生（podiatrist）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.198 (PDF 222), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.199 (PDF 223)

**12. A foot file or pedicure paddle is used to**  
脚锉（foot file / pedicure paddle）是用来做什么的？  
A. clean debris from the nail folds. / 清理甲沟里的污垢  
B. shape the toenail free edge. / 修整脚指甲前缘的形状  
C. push cuticle tissue off the nail. / 把死皮从甲面推掉  
D. reduce and smooth thick calluses. / 磨薄、磨平厚茧 ✅  
*Why:* A large foot file, or pedicure paddle, reduces and smooths thicker calluses. Clean and disinfect it between uses, or throw it away after one use if it cannot be disinfected.  
*为什么:* 大的脚锉（foot file），也叫磨脚板（pedicure paddle），用来磨薄、磨平较厚的茧。每次用后要清洁消毒；无法消毒的用一次就扔。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Calluses protect feet that stand all day. Smooth them down, but don't strip them off.  
*记重点:* 茧是保护整天站立的脚的。可以磨平，但别磨光。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.199 (PDF 223), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.202 (PDF 226)

**13. Which is a job for a nail brush?**  
下列哪一项是指甲刷（nail brush）的用途？  
A. Applying cuticle oil / 涂营养油  
B. Trimming dead skin / 修剪死皮  
C. Scrubbing implements before disinfection / 消毒前刷洗工具 ✅  
D. Shaping the free edge / 修整前缘形状  
*Why:* Clients scrub under the free edges with a nail brush when washing hands. It also removes surface debris and dust during services, and scrubs implements clean before disinfection.  
*为什么:* 顾客洗手时用指甲刷刷指甲前缘下方；服务中用它清除表面碎屑和粉尘；消毒前也用它把工具刷干净。  
*Lock this (picture):* Stiff bristles reach into the small cracks and joints of an implement. Dirt hides there from disinfectant.  
*记重点:* 硬硬的刷毛能伸进工具（implement）的小缝和关节处。脏东西就藏在那里，消毒液碰不到。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.144 (PDF 168), Milady Standard Foundations p.116 (PDF 611), Milady Standard Foundations p.136 (PDF 631)

**14. A single-use implement is one that**  
一次性工具（single-use implement）是指什么样的工具？  
A. is used once a day, then disinfected. / 每天用一次，然后消毒  
B. can be reused after a rinse. / 冲洗后可以再用  
C. is made only of stainless steel. / 只用不锈钢做的  
D. can't be disinfected, so it's thrown away. / 无法消毒，用一次就扔 ✅  
*Why:* Single-use implements cannot be properly cleaned and disinfected, or are damaged by it, so they are discarded after one use, preferably where the client can see. Multiuse implements can be disinfected and reused.  
*为什么:* 一次性工具无法彻底清洁消毒，或消毒会把它弄坏，所以用一次就丢，最好当着顾客的面丢。可重复使用的工具（multiuse）则可以消毒后再用。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Milady's examples: wooden pushers and paper-based files can't be disinfected, so both are single-use.  
*记重点:* Milady 举的例子：木棒和纸质锉条都无法消毒，所以都是一次性工具。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.189 (PDF 213), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.143 (PDF 167), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.199 (PDF 223)

**15. Which of these can be cleaned and disinfected by immersion?**  
下列哪一项可以清洁后浸泡消毒？  
A. A natural hair nail brush / 天然毛刷  
B. A synthetic hair nail brush / 合成毛刷 ✅  
C. A wooden pusher / 木棒  
D. A paper abrasive file / 纸质指甲锉  
*Why:* Synthetic hair brushes can be cleaned and disinfected by immersion. Natural hair brushes cannot, so they are thrown away after use. Paper abrasives cannot be disinfected either.  
*为什么:* 合成毛（synthetic hair）刷可以清洁后浸泡消毒。天然毛（natural hair）刷不行，用后丢弃。纸质锉条也无法消毒。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Natural hair is like your own hair: it soaks up liquid. Synthetic bristles are plastic that rinses clean.  
*记重点:* 天然毛像人的头发，会吸水；合成毛是塑料丝，冲洗得干净。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.144 (PDF 168), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.147 (PDF 171)

**16. The lower the grit number on an abrasive file, the**  
指甲锉的 grit（目数）越低，就越怎样？  
A. finer and gentler it is. / 越细、越温和  
B. larger its particles and more aggressive. / 颗粒越大、研磨越猛 ✅  
C. safer it is on natural nails. / 对真甲越安全  
D. fewer scratches it leaves. / 留下的刮痕越少  
*Why:* Lower grit means larger abrasive particles and a more aggressive file. Coarse grits quickly reduce a surface and leave deeper scratches, so they are generally not used on natural nails.  
*为什么:* 目数（grit）越低，砂粒越大，锉得越猛。粗目数锉条磨得快、刮痕深，所以一般不用在真甲上。  
*Lock this (picture):* Low number = few, big grains, like gravel. High number = many tiny grains, like flour.  
*记重点:* 数字低 = 颗粒少而大，像碎石；数字高 = 颗粒多而小，像面粉。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.146 (PDF 170), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.147 (PDF 171)

**17. Fine-grit abrasives (240 grit and higher) are designed for**  
细目数锉条（240 目及以上）是用来做什么的？  
A. quickly reducing thick enhancement product. / 快速磨薄厚的人造甲产品（enhancement）  
B. reducing thick heel calluses. / 磨掉脚跟的厚茧

D. shortening the free edge fast. / 快速磨短前缘

*为什么:* 细目数（fine-grit，240 目及以上）锉条用于抛光、上光和去除极细的刮痕。目数越高，越是靠抛磨让表面变光滑。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Like sandpaper at the hardware store: rough paper first, the finest paper last for the glossy finish.  
*记重点:* 就像五金店的砂纸：先用粗的，最后用最细的磨出光亮。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.147 (PDF 171)

**18. Why must you prep the edges of a new abrasive file?**  
为什么新指甲锉的边缘必须先处理（prep）？  
A. To raise its grit number / 提高它的目数  
B. To make it disinfectable / 让它可以消毒  
C. To remove sharp edges that can cut / 去掉可能割伤人的锋利边缘 ✅  
D. To soften its foam core / 让泡棉芯变软  
*Why:* Files are stamped from large sheets, leaving very sharp edges. Rub a clean, unused file across the edges, and check buffer corners too. This file prepping protects clients from cuts.  
*为什么:* 锉条是从大片材料上冲压下来的，边缘非常锋利。用另一根干净、没用过的锉条把边缘磨一磨，抛光块的角也要处理。这叫 file prepping（锉条预处理），防止割伤顾客。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Like a paper cut from a fresh sheet of paper: brand-new edges are the sharpest.  
*记重点:* 就像新纸会划破手：越新的边越锋利。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.147 (PDF 171)

**19. When using a three-way buffer, which surface do you use first?**  
使用三面抛光条（three-way buffer）时，先用哪一面？  
A. The lowest-grit surface / 目数最低的一面 ✅  
B. The shine surface / 抛光（上光）面  
C. The highest-grit surface / 目数最高的一面  
D. Any surface, in any order / 任意一面，顺序不限  
*Why:* Begin with the lowest-grit surface to smooth, move to the higher grit, then finish on the shine surface, which usually has no grit. The result is a glossy shine.  
*为什么:* 先用目数最低的一面打磨，再换更高目数的一面，最后用通常没有砂粒的抛光面。这样指甲会很亮。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Each finer grit erases the scratches the coarser one left. Go backward and the last scratches stay.  
*记重点:* 每换一面更细的，都会磨掉上一面留下的刮痕。顺序倒过来，最后的刮痕就留在指甲上。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.147 (PDF 171)

**20. If a client feels burning while you buff, what should you do?**  
抛光时顾客感到发烫，你应该怎么做？  
A. Press harder to finish faster / 用力压，快点结束  
B. Switch to a lower-grit buffer / 换更低目数的抛光条  
C. Buff longer in one spot / 在同一个地方多抛一会儿  
D. Lighten pressure and slow down / 减轻力度、放慢速度 ✅  
*Why:* Too much pressure or buffing too long with low-grit abrasives creates painful heat that can lead to onycholysis. Lighten the pressure, slow the buffing, and lift the buffer between passes.  
*为什么:* 用力过大或用低目数锉条抛太久，会产生灼痛的热，可能导致甲剥离（onycholysis）。要减轻力度、放慢速度，每次抛光之间把抛光条抬起来。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Rub a fingertip hard and fast on your arm: it burns. Lighter and slower: no burn. Same on the nail.  
*记重点:* 用指尖在手臂上又重又快地搓：会烫。轻一点、慢一点：就不烫了。指甲上也一样。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.148 (PDF 172)
