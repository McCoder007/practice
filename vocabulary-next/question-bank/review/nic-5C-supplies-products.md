# Set 5C — Supplies and Professional Products / 耗材与专业产品

Nail Service Tools · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. For a manicure and pedicure on the same client, which glove rule is correct?**  
为同一位顾客做美甲和修脚时，哪种戴手套的做法是对的？  
A. One pair for both services / 两项服务用同一副  
B. A new pair for each service / 每项服务换一副新手套 ✅  
C. Gloves only for the pedicure / 只有修脚时戴手套  
D. The same pair, washed between / 同一副手套，中间洗一洗再用  
*Why:* Gloves are single-use PPE. Wear a new set for each service, even on the same client. Wash your hands, or use antimicrobial gel cleanser, between sets of gloves.  
*为什么:* 手套是一次性的个人防护装备（PPE）。即使是同一位顾客，每项服务都要换一副新手套。换手套之间要洗手，或使用抗菌凝胶清洁剂。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Gloves that touched feet and foot water carry those germs. A fresh pair keeps them off the hands.  
*记重点:* 碰过脚和泡脚水的手套带着那里的病菌。换新手套，病菌就不会带到手上。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.146 (PDF 170), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.200 (PDF 224)

**2. Why do some polish removers contain aloe, vitamin E, or oils?**  
为什么有些洗甲水里加了芦荟、维生素 E 或油？  
A. To keep nails and skin from drying / 防止指甲和皮肤变干 ✅  
B. To make the remover work faster / 让洗甲水卸得更快  
C. To disinfect the nail plate / 给甲面消毒  
D. To harden weak nails / 让软甲变硬  
*Why:* Acetone and non-acetone removers can both dry the cuticle and skin. Added aloe, vitamin E, or oils help prevent drying. Some technicians feel these extras slow removal.  
*为什么:* 含丙酮和无丙酮的洗甲水都会让死皮和皮肤变干。添加芦荟、维生素 E 或油可以帮助防止干燥。有些美甲师觉得这些添加物会让卸甲变慢。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Removers dissolve skin oils along with polish → dry, rough cuticles. The added oils and vitamin E put some back.  
*记重点:* 洗甲水溶掉甲油的同时，也溶掉皮肤的油脂 → 死皮变干变糙。加进去的油和维生素 E 能补回一些。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.149 (PDF 173), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.150 (PDF 174)

**3. Why is a white terry cloth towel best for nail services?**  
为什么白色毛巾（terry cloth towel）最适合美甲服务？  
A. It never needs to be laundered. / 永远不用洗。  
B. It absorbs acetone without damage. / 吸了丙酮也不会坏。  
C. It can be bleached between uses. / 每次用后可以漂白。 ✅  
D. It is single-use and thrown away. / 是一次性的，用后扔掉。  
*Why:* Cloth towels must be washed between clients; paper towels are thrown away. White terry cloth is best because it can be bleached during washing. Each client gets a fresh towel.  
*为什么:* 布毛巾每位顾客之间都要洗；纸巾用后扔掉。白色毛巾最好，因为洗的时候可以漂白。每位顾客都用一条干净的毛巾。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Bleach ruins the dye in a colored towel. A white towel has no color to lose.  
*记重点:* 漂白剂会把彩色毛巾的颜色漂掉；白毛巾本来就没颜色可掉。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.148 (PDF 172)

为什么用背面带塑料膜的棉片（plastic-backed pads）卸指甲油？

D. They add shine to the nail plate. / 让甲面更亮。

*为什么:* 无尘、背面带塑料膜的棉片常用来卸甲油。塑料背膜保护美甲师的指尖，避免过度接触会让皮肤干燥的溶剂和其他化学品。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Bare cotton soaks remover straight through to your fingertip. The plastic layer stops it, pad after pad, all day.  
*记重点:* 纯棉片会让洗甲水直接渗到你的指尖。那层塑料把它挡住，一片又一片，一整天都如此。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.148 (PDF 172)

**6. Why should you wear gloves when using an exfoliating scrub?**  
为什么用去角质磨砂膏（exfoliating scrub）时要戴手套？

B. So the scrub works more deeply / 让磨砂膏作用更深  
C. Because scrubs are always alkaline / 因为磨砂膏都是碱性的  
D. To protect your hands from irritation / 保护你的手不受刺激 ✅

*为什么:* 去角质磨砂膏通常是含研磨颗粒（如浮石或海沙）的水性乳液。使用时必须戴手套，因为反复接触会刺激手部皮肤。  
*Lock this (real-life):* One scrub feels nice to the client. Ten scrubs a day is sandpaper on your own hands.  
*记重点:* 顾客做一次觉得舒服；你一天做十次，等于拿砂纸磨自己的手。

*记重点:* 脚趾挤在一起，湿甲油会蹭到隔壁脚趾。分开了，甲油才干净。

**9. How does a protein hardener protect the nail?**  
蛋白硬甲油（protein hardener）是怎样保护指甲的？  
A. It cross-links the keratin strands. / 让角蛋白链交联。  
B. It dissolves dead tissue on the plate. / 溶解甲面上的死组织。  
C. It forms a hard coating on the surface. / 在甲面形成一层坚硬的保护膜。 ✅  
D. It is absorbed deep into the nail. / 深深渗入指甲内部。  
*Why:* A protein hardener is clear polish plus protein, such as collagen. Collagen molecules are too large to be absorbed, so it forms a clear, hard coating without changing the nail plate itself.  
*为什么:* 蛋白硬甲油是透明甲油加蛋白质（如胶原蛋白 collagen）。胶原蛋白分子太大，进不了甲板，所以它只在表面形成透明坚硬的膜，不改变甲板本身。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Nylon-fiber hardeners work the same way: a coating only, not a treatment.  
*记重点:* 尼龙纤维硬甲油也一样：只是表面一层膜，不是治疗。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.152 (PDF 176)

**10. Why apply cuticle oil with a disposable brush or dropper?**  
为什么要用一次性刷子或滴管涂营养油（cuticle oil）？  
A. Oil dissolves synthetic bristles. / 油会溶解合成刷毛。  
B. Oil must be warmed before use. / 油使用前必须加热。  
C. Droppers give a shinier finish. / 滴管涂出来更亮。  
D. Oils can support bacterial growth. / 油可能滋生细菌。 ✅

*为什么:* 甲油、水晶液（monomer）和凝胶不会滋生病原体，但油会。涂过油的刷子要视为已污染，所以用一次性刷子或滴管，用完就扔。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Oil brush touches skin, goes back in the bottle → germs grow in the oil → next client.  
*记重点:* 刷子碰过皮肤又伸回油瓶 → 病菌在油里繁殖 → 传给下一位顾客。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.144 (PDF 168), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.145 (PDF 169)

**11. Which product is a barrier that seals in the skin's moisture?**  
哪种产品是一层屏障，能锁住皮肤水分？  
A. Nail cream / 护甲霜（nail cream） ✅  
B. Nail oil / 营养油（nail oil）  
C. Cuticle remover / 死皮软化剂  
D. Polish remover / 洗甲水  
*Why:* Nail creams are barrier products: they seal the skin's surface and hold in moisture. Nail oils absorb into the nail plate and skin. Oils and lotions usually last longer than creams.  
*为什么:* 护甲霜（nail cream）是屏障类产品：封住皮肤表面、锁住水分。营养油（nail oil）会渗入甲面和皮肤。油和乳液的效果通常比霜持久。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Cream is a lid on a pot: it keeps water in. Oil is a drink: it soaks into the nail.  
*记重点:* 霜像锅盖，把水分盖在里面；油像饮料，被指甲“喝”进去。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.150 (PDF 174)

**15. Methylene glycol nail hardeners should never be used on nails that are**  
含甲二醇（methylene glycol）的硬甲油绝不能用在什么样的指甲上？  
A. thin and weak. / 又薄又软的。  
B. bare, with no polish. / 没涂甲油的裸甲。  
C. already very hard and brittle. / 本来就很硬、很脆的。 ✅  
D. short and natural. / 短的真甲。  
*Why:* Methylene glycol cross-links keratin, making the nail stiffer. It helps thin, weak nails but can make already hard, brittle nails so rigid they split and shatter. Stop if problems appear.  
*为什么:* 甲二醇（methylene glycol）让角蛋白（keratin）交联，指甲变得更硬。它适合薄软的指甲，但会让本来就硬、脆的指甲变得太僵，裂开甚至碎掉。出现问题就停用。  
*Lock this (picture):* A brittle nail plus hardener is like dry spaghetti made stiffer: it snaps instead of bending.  
*记重点:* 脆指甲再用硬甲油，就像干意面更硬了：一弯就断。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.152 (PDF 176), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.153 (PDF 177)

**16. Why can't a tablet in pedicure water replace disinfecting the footbath?**  
为什么往修脚水里放药片，不能代替给泡脚盆消毒？  
A. Germ-killing strength would also harm skin. / 强到能杀菌的化学品也会伤皮肤。 ✅

C. Tablets cost more than disinfectant. / 药片比消毒剂贵。  
D. Tablets stain the basin. / 药片会把盆染色。  
*Why:* No tablet or additive in pedicure water replaces cleaning and disinfecting after each pedicure. Any chemical strong enough to kill pathogens can also harm the skin.  
*为什么:* 任何放进修脚水里的药片或添加剂，都不能代替每次修脚后的清洁消毒。强到能杀死病原体的化学品也会伤皮肤。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Disinfectants are pesticides and must never touch skin. So real disinfecting happens after the feet leave the tub.  
*记重点:* 在美国，消毒剂按 pesticide（杀生物剂，由 EPA 登记管理）归类，绝不能接触皮肤；所以真正的消毒要等脚离开、盆空了以后才做。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.201 (PDF 225), Milady Standard Foundations p.119 (PDF 614), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.237 (PDF 261)

**20. Why should hydrogen peroxide never be used on an open cut?**  
为什么双氧水（hydrogen peroxide）绝不能用在开放性伤口上？  
A. It destroys cells that start healing. / 会破坏启动伤口愈合的细胞。 ✅  
B. It is too weak to kill germs. / 太弱，杀不了病菌。  
C. It stains the skin brown. / 会把皮肤染成棕色。  
D. It is illegal in all salons. / 在所有沙龙都违法。  
*Why:* Hydrogen peroxide, usually 3 percent, works well as an antiseptic on skin. But never put it on an open cut: it destroys the cells that begin the healing process in a wound.  
*为什么:* 双氧水一般用 3% 浓度，作为皮肤抗菌剂（antiseptic）效果不错。但绝不能用在开放性伤口上：它会破坏伤口里启动愈合的细胞。  
*Lock this (real-life):* The bubbles on a cut look like cleaning, but peroxide also kills the cells that close the wound.  
*记重点:* 伤口上冒泡看起来像在清洁，其实双氧水也在杀死让伤口愈合的细胞。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.118 (PDF 613)
