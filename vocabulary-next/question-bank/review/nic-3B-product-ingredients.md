# Set 3B — Product Ingredients and Harmful Ingredients / 产品成分与有害成分

Chemistry of Nail Products · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. In "volatile organic compound," what does volatile mean?**  
在 volatile organic compound（挥发性有机化合物，VOC）中，volatile 是什么意思？  
A. Contains carbon / 含有碳  
B. Explodes on contact / 一碰就爆炸  
C. Evaporates very easily / 非常容易蒸发 ✅  
D. Is highly toxic / 毒性很强  
*Why:* VOCs contain carbon (organic) and evaporate very easily (volatile). Ethyl acetate and isopropyl alcohol are VOC solvents in polish, base coats, top coats, and removers.  
*为什么:* VOC 含碳（organic，有机），并且非常容易蒸发（volatile，挥发性）。乙酸乙酯（ethyl acetate）和异丙醇（isopropyl alcohol）就是指甲油、底油、亮油和洗甲水里的 VOC 溶剂。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Rubbing alcohol on your skin is gone in seconds. It went into the air. That's volatile, not explosive.  
*记重点:* 酒精（rubbing alcohol）擦在皮肤上，几秒就不见了，跑到空气里去了，这就是 volatile（易挥发），不是“会爆炸”。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.164 (PDF 659)

**2. In chemistry, an organic substance is one that**  
在化学中，organic（有机）物质是指——  
A. contains carbon. / 含有碳的物质。 ✅  
B. comes only from plants. / 只来自植物的物质。  
C. is always safe. / 一定安全的物质。  
D. contains no water. / 不含水的物质。  
*Why:* Organic simply means a substance contains carbon. It does not mean natural, healthy, or safe. All nail enhancements are made from organic substances.  
*为什么:* organic（有机）只表示物质含有碳，不代表天然、健康或安全。所有加固甲（nail enhancements）都是由有机物制成的。  
*Lock this (name-trap):* "Organic" on a food label describes farming methods, not chemistry.  
*记重点:* 超市食品上的“有机”讲的是种植和养殖方式，和化学里的“有机”不是一回事。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.155 (PDF 650), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.126 (PDF 150)

**3. Which ingredient keeps nail products flexible?**  
哪种成分能让美甲产品保持柔韧？  
A. UV stabilizers / 紫外线稳定剂（UV stabilizers）  
B. Plasticizers / 增塑剂（plasticizers） ✅  
C. Pigments / 色素（pigments）  
D. Solvents / 溶剂（solvents）  
*Why:* Plasticizers keep polishes and nail enhancement products flexible. UV stabilizers protect color, pigments give color, and solvents evaporate to leave the film behind.  
*为什么:* plasticizers（增塑剂）让指甲油和加固甲产品保持柔韧。紫外线稳定剂保护颜色，色素提供颜色，溶剂蒸发后留下涂膜。  
*Lock this (word-link):* Plasticizer starts with "plastic." A soft plastic bag bends and doesn't crack. Plasticizers keep polish bendable.  
*记重点:* plasticizer（增塑剂）开头就是 plastic（塑料）：软塑料袋能弯、不会裂。增塑剂让涂膜能弯、不发脆。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.125 (PDF 149), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.131 (PDF 155)

**4. What do UV stabilizers in nail polish do?**  
指甲油中的紫外线稳定剂（UV stabilizers）有什么作用？  
A. Keep the film flexible / 让涂膜保持柔韧  
B. Make polish dry faster / 让指甲油干得更快  
C. Help polish stick to nails / 帮助指甲油附着在指甲上  
D. Prevent fading from sunlight / 防止阳光导致褪色 ✅  
*Why:* UV stabilizers control color stability and stop sunlight from fading or discoloring the color. Nail enhancement products use them for the same job.  
*为什么:* UV stabilizers（紫外线稳定剂）保持颜色稳定，防止阳光让颜色褪色或变色。加固甲产品里也用它做同样的事。  
*Lock this (real-acronym):* UV = ultraviolet: sun rays you can't see. Like a shirt left in the sun, the color fades.  
*记重点:* UV = ultraviolet（紫外线），“紫光之外”：肉眼看不见，却会让颜色褪掉，就像衣服晒久了会褪色。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.125 (PDF 149)

**5. All nail enhancements and nail adhesives belong to which chemical family?**  
所有加固甲和美甲胶水都属于哪个化学家族？  
A. Silicones / 硅酮类（silicones）  
B. Acrylics / 丙烯酸类（acrylics） ✅  
C. Proteins / 蛋白质类（proteins）  
D. Alcohols / 醇类（alcohols）  
*Why:* Liquid and powder, UV gels, wraps, and glues are all acrylics. Many nail techs use the word only for liquid and powder, but the name covers all of them.  
*为什么:* 液粉系统（liquid and powder，俗称水晶甲）、UV 凝胶、包甲（wraps）和胶水都属于 acrylics（丙烯酸类）。很多美甲师只把液粉叫 acrylic，其实这个名字涵盖全部。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Hear "acryl" in all three names: methacrylates, acrylates, cyanoacrylates.  
*记重点:* 三种名字里都有 acryl：methacrylates、acrylates、cyanoacrylates，都是丙烯酸类。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.124 (PDF 148)

**6. Nail adhesives (glues) and wraps are based on**  
美甲胶水（adhesives）和包甲（wraps）以什么为基础？  
A. cyanoacrylates. / 氰基丙烯酸酯（cyanoacrylates）。 ✅  
B. methacrylates. / 甲基丙烯酸酯（methacrylates）。  
C. acrylates. / 丙烯酸酯（acrylates）。  
D. silicones. / 硅酮（silicones）。  
*Why:* All nail glues and wraps use cyanoacrylates. Methacrylates make liquid and powder systems and at least one UV gel. Other UV gels use acrylates.  
*为什么:* 所有美甲胶水和包甲都用 cyanoacrylates（氰基丙烯酸酯）。methacrylates 用于液粉系统和至少一种 UV 凝胶；其他 UV 凝胶用 acrylates。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* A wrap is fabric stuck on with resin, a nail glue. So glue and wraps share one base: cyanoacrylate.  
*记重点:* 包甲（wrap）就是用树脂胶（resin，一种美甲胶水）把布料粘在指甲上。所以胶水和包甲的基础是同一种：氰基丙烯酸酯（cyanoacrylate）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.124 (PDF 148), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.131 (PDF 155), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.283 (PDF 307), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.282 (PDF 306)

**7. What is the most important reason not to use MMA monomer?**  
不应使用 MMA（甲基丙烯酸甲酯，methyl methacrylate）单体最重要的原因是什么？  
A. It causes cancer / 它会致癌  
B. It is absorbed into the blood / 它会被吸收进血液  
C. FDA and state boards say don't use it / FDA 和州美容委员会（state boards）都说不要用 ✅  
D. It dissolves too easily in remover / 它在卸甲液里溶解得太快  
*Why:* Milady says MMA is not a carcinogen and is not absorbed into the blood. The problems are poor adhesion, rigid nails, hard removal, and, most important, the FDA's position.  
*为什么:* Milady 指出 MMA 不致癌，也不会被吸收进血液。问题在于附着差、太硬、难卸除，而最重要的原因是 FDA 和州美容委员会不允许使用。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like a red light: you stop because the law says so. The FDA and state boards say stop using MMA.  
*记重点:* 就像红灯：你停下来，是因为法规这么规定。FDA 和州美容委员会（state boards）说的就是：停用 MMA。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.124 (PDF 148), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.125 (PDF 149)

**8. When an MMA nail is jammed or caught, what often breaks?**  
MMA 指甲被撞到或勾到时，通常什么会断？  
A. The MMA enhancement / MMA 加固甲  
B. The nail adhesive layer / 胶水层  
C. The cuticle / 甲小皮（cuticle）  
D. The natural nail plate / 真甲甲板 ✅  
*Why:* MMA nails are rigid and difficult to break. The natural nail was thinned to make MMA stick, so it often breaks instead, causing serious nail damage.  
*为什么:* MMA 指甲又硬又难断。为了让 MMA 粘住，真甲已被磨薄，所以断的往往是真甲，造成严重损伤。  
*Lock this (comparison):* A chain breaks at its weakest link. MMA is too hard to break, so the thinned natural nail breaks.  
*记重点:* 链条总是在最弱的一环断开。MMA 硬得很难断，所以断的是被磨薄的真甲（natural nail plate）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.125 (PDF 149)

**9. Which ingredient makes acid-based primers corrosive?**  
哪种成分让酸性结合剂（acid-based primer）具有腐蚀性？  
A. Methacrylic acid / 甲基丙烯酸（methacrylic acid） ✅  
B. Methylene glycol / 甲二醇（methylene glycol）  
C. Ethyl acetate / 乙酸乙酯（ethyl acetate）  
D. Cyanoacrylate / 氰基丙烯酸酯（cyanoacrylate）  
*Why:* Acid-based primers contain methacrylic acid and can burn and scar skin and eyes. Acid-free primers contain no acids and have a neutral pH, but still avoid skin contact.  
*为什么:* 酸性结合剂含甲基丙烯酸（methacrylic acid），会灼伤皮肤和眼睛并留疤。无酸结合剂（acid-free）不含酸、pH 中性，但同样要避免接触皮肤。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Acid-based primer, acid in the name: methacrylic acid. Methylene glycol is a nail hardener, not a primer.  
*记重点:* 酸性结合剂（acid-based primer），就找名字里带“酸”的：甲基丙烯酸（methacrylic acid）。甲二醇（methylene glycol）是指甲硬化剂，不是结合剂。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.118 (PDF 142), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.153 (PDF 177)

**10. Which is true about nail primers and the nail plate?**  
关于结合剂（primer）和甲板，哪项说法正确？  
A. They etch tiny grooves into the nail / 结合剂会在甲面蚀出细小沟纹  
B. They are needed for every client / 每位顾客都必须用结合剂  
C. They do not etch or eat the nail / 结合剂不会蚀刻（etch）或“吃掉”甲板 ✅  
D. They work best in several coats / 结合剂涂好几层效果最好  
*Why:* The idea that primers eat or etch the nail is a myth. One very thin coat is enough for most clients. Needing two or more means nail prep or application has a problem.  
*为什么:* 结合剂会蚀刻或“吃掉”指甲是一种误解。大多数顾客涂一层极薄的就够了。如果需要两层或更多，说明甲面准备或操作有问题。  
*Lock this (picture):* Nail clippings can soak in primer for many years without dissolving.  
*记重点:* 剪下来的指甲泡在结合剂里很多年都不会溶解。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.118 (PDF 142)

**11. What causes skin allergies to nail enhancement products?**  
对加固甲产品的皮肤过敏是由什么引起的？  
A. Breathing product vapors / 吸入产品蒸气  
B. Repeated skin contact / 反复接触皮肤 ✅  
C. Product touching only the nail plate / 产品只碰到甲板  
D. Removing product with acetone / 用丙酮卸除产品  
*Why:* Prolonged, repeated skin contact causes these allergies over time. Product vapors do not cause skin allergies. If the product never touches skin, an allergy is extremely unlikely.  
*为什么:* 长期、反复接触皮肤，久而久之会引起过敏。产品蒸气不会引起皮肤过敏。只要产品从不碰到皮肤，过敏的可能性极低。  
*Lock this (picture):* Each skin touch adds a drop to a cup. When the cup is full, allergy starts. Vapors add none.  
*记重点:* 想象一个杯子一滴一滴装满：产品每碰一次皮肤就加一滴，杯子满了，过敏就开始了。只闻到气味（vapors）不加滴。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.118 (PDF 142)

**12. What decides whether a substance has a toxic effect?**  
什么决定一种物质是否产生毒害作用？  
A. How natural it is / 它是否天然  
B. How strong it smells / 气味有多重  
C. How many chemicals it contains / 含有多少种化学物质  
D. How much and how long you're exposed / 接触多少、接触多久 ✅  
*Why:* The overexposure principle says overexposure (prolonged, repeated, or long-term exposure) determines toxicity. Salt water is very toxic to drink, yet you can safely swim in the ocean.  
*为什么:* 过度接触原则（overexposure principle）认为，决定毒性的是过度接触，即长期、反复或长时间的接触。海水喝下去毒性很大，但在海里游泳却很安全。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Even plain water is a chemical, and too much of it can be toxic.  
*记重点:* 连水都是化学物质，喝得太多也会中毒：关键在剂量。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.127 (PDF 151), Milady Standard Foundations p.165 (PDF 660), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.116 (PDF 140)

**13. How long should cuticle remover stay on the nail?**  
死皮软化剂（cuticle remover）应在指甲上停留多久？  
A. The manufacturer's recommended time / 按厂家建议的时间 ✅  
B. Until the living skin softens / 直到活皮肤变软

*Lock this (comparison):* No fixed number to memorize: the maker sets the time. Like pasta: cook it as long as the box says.  
*记重点:* 没有固定的数字要背：时间由厂家定。就像煮面条：包装上写煮多久，就煮多久。

**14. What do nail monomer liquids give off into the air?**  
美甲单体液（monomer liquid）会向空气中释放什么？  
A. Fumes / 烟雾（fumes）  
B. Gases / 气体（gases）  
C. Dust / 粉尘（dust）  
D. Vapors / 蒸气（vapors） ✅  
*Why:* Vapors form when liquids evaporate. Monomers, UV gels, wraps, and adhesives all form vapors. Fumes come from burning things, like candles, so "monomer fumes" is incorrect.  
*为什么:* 液体蒸发就形成 vapors（蒸气）。单体、UV 凝胶、包甲和胶水都会产生蒸气。fumes（烟雾）来自燃烧，比如蜡烛，所以说“单体烟雾”是错误的。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Is the monomer burning? No. It evaporates, like water from a wet towel, so it gives off vapor, not fumes.  
*记重点:* 单体在燃烧吗？没有。它像湿毛巾上的水一样蒸发，所以放出的是蒸气（vapors），不是烟雾（fumes）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.116 (PDF 140)

**15. What is the main hazard of acetone?**  
丙酮（acetone）的主要危险是什么？  
A. It is highly corrosive / 腐蚀性很强  
B. It is strongly acidic / 酸性很强  
C. It is highly flammable / 高度易燃 ✅  
D. It is strongly alkaline / 碱性很强  
*Why:* Acetone works quickly and is one of the safest solvents used in nail salons. But it is highly flammable, so keep it away from heat, open flames, and sparks.  
*为什么:* 丙酮起效快，是美甲沙龙最安全的溶剂之一。但它高度易燃，必须远离高温、明火和火花。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Used acetone goes in a metal can the fire department approves. Why fire? It burns very easily.  
*记重点:* 用过的丙酮要装进消防部门认可的金属容器。为什么是消防部门？因为丙酮非常容易着火（flammable）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.127 (PDF 151), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.150 (PDF 174)

**16. Which sweet, colorless, oily ingredient is used as a moisturizer in creams?**  
哪种带甜味、无色、油状的成分在护肤霜中用作保湿剂？  
A. Glycerin / 甘油（glycerin） ✅  
B. Ammonia / 氨（ammonia）  
C. Acetone / 丙酮（acetone）  
D. Isopropyl alcohol / 异丙醇（isopropyl alcohol）  
*Why:* Glycerin is a sweet, colorless, oily substance used as a solvent and moisturizer in skin and body creams. Cuticle removers add it to counteract their skin-drying effects.  
*为什么:* glycerin（甘油）是一种带甜味、无色、油状的物质，在护肤霜和身体霜中用作溶剂和保湿剂。死皮软化剂（cuticle remover）里也会加甘油，用来抵消死皮软化剂本身让皮肤变干的作用。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* The Chinese name means "sweet oil." Sweet and oily? Pick glycerin.  
*记重点:* glycerin = 甘油：甘 = 甜（甘甜），油 = 油。“又甜又油”就是甘油，它还能保湿。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.164 (PDF 659), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.151 (PDF 175)

**17. Cuticle removers contain sodium or potassium hydroxide, so they are**  
死皮软化剂（cuticle remover）含氢氧化钠或氢氧化钾，因此它们——  
A. low in pH and acidic. / pH 低，呈酸性。  
B. high in pH and caustic. / pH 高，具有腐蚀性（caustic）。 ✅  
C. neutral, with a pH of 7. / 中性，pH 为 7。  
D. water-free, with no pH. / 不含水，没有 pH。  
*Why:* Cuticle removers have a high pH (caustic) and irritate skin. They dissolve dead tissue on the nail plate, so keep them off the living skin of the eponychium.  
*为什么:* 死皮软化剂 pH 高（caustic，腐蚀性），刺激皮肤。它们用来溶解甲板上的死组织，所以不要碰到甲上皮（eponychium）的活皮肤。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Sodium hydroxide is a very strong alkali, also used in drain cleaners. Strong alkali means very high pH.  
*记重点:* 氢氧化钠（烧碱）是很强的碱（alkali），通下水道的疏通剂里也有它。强碱 = pH 很高。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.150 (PDF 174), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.151 (PDF 175), Milady Standard Foundations p.167 (PDF 662)

**18. How does a methylene glycol nail hardener make nails stiffer?**  
含甲二醇（methylene glycol）的指甲硬化剂怎样让指甲变硬？  
A. It coats the nail with protein / 给指甲包上一层蛋白质  
B. It soaks water into the nail / 让水分渗进指甲  
C. It cross-links the keratin strands / 在角蛋白链之间形成交联（cross-links） ✅  
D. It dissolves the top nail layer / 溶掉甲板表层  
*Why:* Methylene glycol creates bridges, or cross-links, between the keratin strands of the natural nail. This makes the plate stiffer and more resistant to bending and breaking.  
*为什么:* methylene glycol（甲二醇）在真甲的角蛋白（keratin）链之间搭“桥”，也就是交联，让甲板更硬，更不易弯折和断裂。  
*Lock this (picture):* Loose straws bend easily. Tape them together and the bundle is stiff. That is cross-linking the nail's keratin.  
*记重点:* 一根根散开的吸管很容易弯。用胶带把它们绑在一起，整捆就变硬了。这就是在指甲的角蛋白（keratin）之间形成交联（cross-links）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.152 (PDF 176), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.153 (PDF 177)

**19. Why should disinfectants never be used as hand cleaners?**  
为什么绝不能把消毒剂当洗手液用？  
A. They are too weak to kill germs / 杀菌力太弱  
B. They leave hands too oily / 会让手太油  
C. They have no pH / 没有 pH 值  
D. They can be harmful if absorbed through skin / 经皮肤吸收会造成伤害 ✅  
*Why:* Disinfectants are pesticides made for surfaces, not skin. As hand cleaners they can irritate skin, cause allergic reactions, and be harmful if absorbed through the skin.  
*为什么:* 消毒剂（disinfectants）属于 pesticides（杀生物剂类产品），是给物体表面用的，不是给皮肤用的。拿来洗手会刺激皮肤、引起过敏，经皮肤吸收还会造成伤害。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* If you need gloves just to mix it, never wash your hands with it: skin can soak it in.  
*记重点:* 连调配时都要戴手套，就绝不能拿它洗手：皮肤会把它吸收进去。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.119 (PDF 614)

**20. Why keep tabletops and product containers free of dust and residue?**  
为什么要让桌面和产品容器上没有粉尘和残留？  
A. Dust makes primer corrosive / 粉尘会让结合剂变得有腐蚀性  
B. Handling them repeatedly causes overexposure / 反复接触会造成过度接触（overexposure） ✅  
C. Dust makes polish thicken / 粉尘会让指甲油变稠  
D. Dust turns into fumes / 粉尘会变成烟雾  
*Why:* Repeatedly handling dusty supplies, containers, and tabletops causes overexposure. Nail enhancement products are not designed for skin contact, and avoiding contact prevents allergic reactions.  
*为什么:* 反复接触沾有粉尘的用品、容器和桌面，会造成过度接触。加固甲产品不是设计来接触皮肤的，避免接触才能防止过敏。  
*Lock this (picture):* Like flour on a kitchen counter: every time you touch it, some gets on your hands. Product dust does too.  
*记重点:* 就像撒在厨房台面上的面粉：每碰一次，手上就沾一点。产品粉尘也一样。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.126 (PDF 150)
