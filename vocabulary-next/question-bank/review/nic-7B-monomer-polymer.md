# Set 7B — Monomer Liquid and Polymer Powder / 水晶甲：单体液与聚合物粉

Nail Enhancements: Application, Maintenance, and Removal · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. A medium bead uses about how much liquid compared with powder?**  
中等湿度的水晶球（medium bead）里，液体大约是粉的多少？  
A. Half as much liquid / 液体是粉的一半  
B. Equal amounts / 液体和粉一样多  
C. One and a half times as much / 液体是粉的 1.5 倍 ✅  
D. Twice as much liquid / 液体是粉的 2 倍  
*Why:* Equal liquid and powder makes a dry bead; twice as much liquid makes a wet bead. A medium bead is halfway between and is usually the ideal mix ratio.  
*为什么:* 液体和粉等量是 dry bead（干球）；液体是粉的两倍是 wet bead（湿球）。medium bead（中球）介于两者之间，通常是最理想的 mix ratio（液粉比）。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Medium, like a medium T-shirt: between small and large. Dry = 1:1, wet = 2:1, medium = halfway, 1.5:1.  
*记重点:* medium 就像衣服的 M 码：介于 S 和 L 之间。干球 1:1，湿球 2:1，中球在正中间：1.5:1。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.324 (PDF 348)

**2. Too little powder in the bead can cause**  
水晶球里粉太少，会造成什么问题？  
A. a weak nail and skin irritation. / 指甲变弱，并可能刺激皮肤 ✅  
B. brittleness and discoloration. / 变脆和变色  
C. many trapped air bubbles. / 里面有很多气泡  
D. a chalky white surface. / 表面发白像粉笔  
*Why:* Too little powder makes the enhancement weak and raises the risk of skin irritation or sensitivity. Too much powder cures incorrectly and can cause brittleness or discoloration.  
*为什么:* 粉太少会让甲变弱，并增加皮肤刺激或过敏的风险。粉太多则固化不正确，可能变脆或变色。  
*Lock this (picture):* Milady's cookie rule: too much flour, dry and crumbly; too little flour, soft and gooey.  
*记重点:* Milady 的饼干比喻：面粉太多，又干又碎；面粉太少，又软又黏。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.324 (PDF 348)

**3. What does the perfect bead look like when placed on a form?**  
理想的水晶球放到纸托上时是什么样子？  
A. Dry, with loose powder around it / 偏干，周围有没吸透的粉  
B. Runny, spreading across the form / 很稀，在纸托上流开  
C. Lumpy and full of air bubbles / 疙疙瘩瘩，满是气泡  
D. Round and shiny; settles but doesn't run / 圆润有光泽，会稍微摊开但不流动 ✅  
*Why:* A perfect bead is round and shiny, and it settles on the form without running. Dry powder around it means too little liquid; dripping means too wet.  
*为什么:* 理想的水晶球圆润有光泽，放在纸托上会稍微摊开但不会流动。周围有干粉说明液体太少；会滴落说明太湿。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture a spoon of thick hair gel on a plate: round and shiny, it spreads a little, then stops.  
*记重点:* 想象盘子上的一小坨发胶：圆润有光泽，稍微摊开，然后就定住不动。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.324 (PDF 348), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.328 (PDF 352)

**4. A monomer liquid is called odorless when**  
单体液（monomer liquid）在什么情况下被称为 odorless（无味）？  
A. it has no odor at all. / 它完全没有气味  
B. it is made with MMA. / 它是用 MMA 做的  
C. others in the salon can't smell it. / 店里的其他人闻不到它 ✅  
D. a perfume covers its odor. / 加了香料盖住气味  
*Why:* Odorless products still have a slight odor. If others in the salon can't detect it, it is called odorless; if they notice a slight odor, it is called low odor.  
*为什么:* odorless（无味）产品其实仍有轻微气味。店里其他人闻不出来，就叫无味；能闻到一点，就叫 low odor（低味）。  
*Lock this (name-trap):* Name trap: "odorless" liquids use a different monomer with little smell. The smell is weak, not gone.  
*记重点:* 名字陷阱：odorless 产品换用了气味很小的单体。味道变淡了，但没有消失。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.323 (PDF 347)

**5. When shaping an odorless bead on the nail, you should never**  
在指甲上塑形无味水晶球时，绝对不能做什么？  
A. re-wet the brush with monomer. / 再把笔刷蘸湿单体液 ✅  
B. wipe the brush on a towel. / 在纸巾上擦笔  
C. use a dry mix ratio. / 用偏干的液粉比  
D. press and smooth the bead. / 按压并抹平水晶球  
*Why:* Odorless products use a dry mix ratio. Re-wetting the brush changes the ratio and can cause discoloration, service breakdown, and skin irritation. Wiping the brush often is correct.  
*为什么:* 无味产品要用 dry mix ratio（偏干的液粉比）。再蘸湿笔刷会改变液粉比，可能导致变色、美甲提前损坏和皮肤刺激。勤擦笔刷才是对的。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Each extra dip adds liquid on the nail, so the dry bead slowly becomes a wet one.  
*记重点:* 每多蘸一次，指甲上的液体就多一点，干球就慢慢变成了湿球。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.323 (PDF 347)

**6. Acid-free primer works most like**  
无酸结合剂（acid-free primer）的作用最像什么？  
A. sandpaper that roughs up the nail. / 把指甲磨粗的砂纸  
B. a glossy sealing topcoat. / 一层亮面封层  
C. a dehydrator that dries the nail. / 让指甲干燥的脱水剂  
D. two-sided sticky tape. / 双面胶 ✅  
*Why:* One end of the acid-free primer molecule bonds to nail protein; the other end bonds to the monomer as it cures. That is why Milady compares it to two-sided tape.  
*为什么:* acid-free primer（无酸结合剂）分子的一端和指甲蛋白结合，另一端在单体固化时和单体结合，所以 Milady 把它比作双面胶。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Hang a poster with two-sided tape: wall on one side, poster on the other. Nail = wall, product = poster.  
*记重点:* 用双面胶贴海报：一面粘墙，一面粘海报。真甲就是墙，产品就是海报。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.325 (PDF 349)

**7. When preparing nails for liquid and powder, which order is correct?**  
做水晶甲前处理时，哪个顺序是对的？  
A. Primer, then dehydrator / 先结合剂，再脱水剂  
B. Dehydrator, then primer / 先脱水剂，再结合剂 ✅  
C. Dehydrator, then nail cleanser / 先脱水剂，再甲面清洁剂  
D. Primer, then buff off the shine / 先结合剂，再磨掉光泽  
*Why:* Milady's steps apply nail dehydrator first to remove moisture and oil, then nail primer to improve adhesion. Product goes on only after the primer is dry.  
*为什么:* Milady 的步骤是先涂 dehydrator（脱水剂）去除水分和油分，再涂 primer（结合剂）增强附着。结合剂干了之后才上产品。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Dehydrator takes away water and oil. The nail is dry. Now primer can grip it.  
*记重点:* 脱水剂（dehydrator）去掉水分和油分。指甲变干了。这时结合剂（primer）才抓得牢。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.344 (PDF 368), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.325 (PDF 349)

**8. Nail primer is especially useful for a client with**  
结合剂（nail primer）对哪种客人特别有用？  
A. very short nail beds. / 甲床很短  
B. thick, curved toenails. / 脚趾甲又厚又弯  
C. oily nail plates. / 甲面偏油 ✅  
D. very sensitive skin. / 皮肤非常敏感  
*Why:* Primer improves adhesion, so it is especially useful when a client has oily nail plates, where adhesion is a problem. Use it sparingly; one thin coat is enough for most clients.  
*为什么:* primer（结合剂）能增强附着，所以对甲面偏油、不容易附着的客人特别有用。要少量使用，大多数客人一薄层就够了。  
*Lock this (picture):* An oily nail is like a slippery floor. Primer works like non-slip shoes: it helps product hold on.  
*记重点:* 偏油的甲面就像湿滑的地板。结合剂（primer）就像防滑鞋：帮产品牢牢抓住指甲。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.118 (PDF 142), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.117 (PDF 141)

**9. Why should nail primer be kept off plastic nail tips?**  
为什么结合剂不能涂到塑料甲片上？  
A. It stops the overlay from curing. / 会让加固层无法固化  
B. It makes the tip stick too tightly. / 会让甲片粘得太紧  
C. It turns the tip yellow in sunlight. / 会让甲片在阳光下变黄  
D. It can melt the tip and weaken adhesive. / 会让甲片融化，并削弱甲片胶 ✅  
*Why:* Primer goes only on the natural nail, and only once. On plastic tips it can melt the tip, make it brittle, and break down the tip adhesive.  
*为什么:* primer（结合剂）只涂在真甲上，而且只涂一次。涂在塑料甲片上，可能让甲片融化、变脆，并破坏甲片胶。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Primer is made to grab the natural nail, not plastic. On a plastic tip, it can melt the tip instead.  
*记重点:* 结合剂（primer）是用来抓住真甲的，不是塑料。涂在塑料甲片上，它反而可能让甲片融化。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.325 (PDF 349), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.331 (PDF 355)

**10. Before using acid-based primer, what should you put on?**  
使用酸性结合剂之前，应该戴上什么？  
A. Protective gloves and safety eyewear / 防护手套和护目镜 ✅  
B. A dust mask only / 只戴防尘口罩  
C. Barrier cream on the cuticle only / 只在甲小皮处涂隔离霜  
D. A hairnet and an apron / 发网和围裙  
*Why:* Acid-based primer is corrosive and can cause serious, sometimes irreversible, damage to skin and eyes. Always wear protective gloves and safety eyewear.  
*为什么:* acid-based primer（酸性结合剂）有腐蚀性，可能对皮肤和眼睛造成严重、有时无法逆转的伤害。务必戴防护手套和护目镜。  
*Lock this (picture):* Corrosive means it eats away, like rust on metal. It hurts skin and eyes, so protect hands and eyes.  
*记重点:* corrosive（腐蚀性）= 会把东西慢慢“吃掉”，就像铁锈吃掉铁。它伤皮肤也伤眼睛，所以手和眼睛都要保护。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.325 (PDF 349)

**11. Before applying enhancements, how much should the natural nail be filed?**  
做美甲加固前，真甲应该锉到什么程度？  
A. Until the surface feels rough / 锉到表面摸起来很粗  
B. Only enough to remove the shine / 只锉掉表面光泽 ✅  
C. Until the nail plate is thin / 锉到甲面变薄  
D. Not at all; leave the shine / 完全不锉，保留光泽  
*Why:* It is a myth that products stick only to a roughed-up nail. Adhesion is best on a clean, dry plate, so remove only the surface shine. A thinner plate is a weaker base.  
*为什么:* 认为指甲要磨粗产品才粘得住，是一种误解。甲面干净、干燥时附着最好，所以只需去掉表面光泽。甲面越薄，基础越弱。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Milady: the nail tech's first duty is to keep the nail plate thick, strong, and healthy.  
*记重点:* Milady 说：美甲师的第一职责，是让甲面保持厚实、坚固、健康。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.119 (PDF 143)

**12. Why are MMA enhancements usually pried off the nail?**  
为什么 MMA 美甲通常是被硬撬下来的？  
A. They don't dissolve well in removers. / 它们在卸甲液里不容易溶解 ✅  
B. They are too soft to file. / 它们太软，没法锉  
C. They come off in water. / 它们遇水就会脱落  
D. They are glued on with resin. / 它们是用树脂粘上去的  
*Why:* MMA does not dissolve well in product removers, so it is usually pried from the nail plate, causing more damage. MMA is not to be used on nails.  
*为什么:* MMA 在卸甲液里不容易溶解，所以通常被从甲面上硬撬下来，造成更多损伤。指甲上不能使用 MMA。  
*Lock this (picture):* Like chewing gum stuck in hair: it won't wash out, so it gets pulled, and hair comes with it.  
*记重点:* 就像口香糖粘在头发上：洗不掉，只能硬扯，结果连头发一起扯下来。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.321 (PDF 345), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.124 (PDF 148)

**13. How should a small amount of leftover monomer liquid be thrown away?**  
少量剩余的单体液应该怎样丢弃？  
A. Pour it straight into a plastic bag / 直接倒进塑料袋  
B. Pour it back into the bottle / 倒回原瓶  
C. Pour onto an absorbent towel, then bag it / 倒在吸水纸巾上，再装进塑料袋 ✅  
D. Pour it down the sink / 倒进水槽  
*Why:* Pour it into a very absorbent paper towel, place that in a plastic bag, and seal the bag in a closed waste container. Never pour it back into the original container.  
*为什么:* 把它倒在吸水性很强的纸巾上，放进塑料袋，再封好丢进有盖垃圾桶。绝不能倒回原瓶。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Every brush dip leaves powder in the dish, so pouring it back would spoil the whole bottle.  
*记重点:* 笔刷每蘸一次都会把粉带进杯里，倒回去会污染整瓶液体。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.329 (PDF 353), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.327 (PDF 351)

**14. What kind of brush is best for liquid and powder?**  
做水晶甲（liquid and powder）最好用哪种笔刷？  
A. Synthetic nylon bristles / 合成尼龙毛  
B. Natural hair, like kolinsky or sable / 天然毛，如貂毛（kolinsky）或紫貂毛（sable） ✅  
C. A foam-tipped applicator / 海绵头涂抹棒  
D. An extra-large size 16 brush / 特大号 16 号笔  
*Why:* Natural hair absorbs and holds monomer like a reservoir. Synthetic brushes don't pick up enough liquid or release it properly, and size 12 to 16 brushes hold too much.  
*为什么:* 天然毛能像水库一样吸住并保存单体液。合成毛吸不够单体液，放液也不顺畅；而 12 到 16 号的大笔又吸得太多。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Gel uses a nylon brush; acrylic uses natural hair. The most common acrylic brush is a #8 oval.  
*记重点:* 光疗胶用尼龙笔，水晶甲用天然毛笔。最常用的水晶笔是 8 号椭圆笔。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.327 (PDF 351), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.321 (PDF 345), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.359 (PDF 383)

**15. How should you wipe your liquid and powder brush on the towel?**  
做水晶甲时，笔刷应该怎样在纸巾上擦？  
A. Rapidly back and forth / 快速来回擦  
B. In small, quick circles / 打小圈快速擦  
C. Pressed flat and twisted / 压扁后再扭转  
D. In one direction, pulling it along / 朝一个方向拖着擦 ✅  
*Why:* Wipe in one direction, pulling the brush along the towel. Rubbing back and forth presses the hairs against the sharp metal ferrule and can cut them off.  
*为什么:* 朝一个方向、拖着笔刷在纸巾上擦。来回猛擦会把笔毛压在锋利的金属笔箍（ferrule）边上，可能把毛割断。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Scrubbing hair over a metal edge works like sawing: each pass cuts a few more hairs.  
*记重点:* 在金属边上来回擦，就像拉锯：每来回一次，就多割断几根毛。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.328 (PDF 352)

**16. Which initiator in polymer powder starts the chain reaction that cures the product?**  
聚合物粉里哪种引发剂（initiator）启动让产品固化的链式反应？  
A. Benzoyl peroxide (BPO) / 过氧化苯甲酰（BPO） ✅  
B. A photoinitiator / 光引发剂（photoinitiator）  
C. Methacrylic acid / 甲基丙烯酸（methacrylic acid）  
D. Cyanoacrylate / 氰基丙烯酸酯（cyanoacrylate）  
*Why:* BPO is the initiator mixed into the powder. It starts the chain reaction when liquid meets powder. A photoinitiator is gel's starter and needs lamp light. Cyanoacrylate is nail glue.  
*为什么:* BPO（过氧化苯甲酰）是加在聚合物粉里的引发剂（initiator）。单体液一碰到粉，它就启动链式反应（chain reaction）。光引发剂（photoinitiator）是凝胶的引发剂，要照灯才起作用。氰基丙烯酸酯（cyanoacrylate）是甲片胶。  
*Lock this (real-life):* BPO is the same ingredient as in some acne creams; nail powders use much less of it.  
*记重点:* BPO 和一些祛痘膏里的成分一样，只是美甲粉里的用量少得多。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.322 (PDF 346), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.323 (PDF 347), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.354 (PDF 378), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.282 (PDF 306)

**17. Where is the apex of a nail enhancement built?**  
美甲延长的甲峰（apex）做在哪里？  
A. At the cuticle / 在甲小皮处  
B. At the very end of the free edge / 在游离缘的最末端  
C. Along the sidewalls / 沿着两侧  
D. Over the stress area / 在受力区（stress area）上方 ✅  
*Why:* The stress area, where the nail grows past the finger, needs extra product. That highest point is the apex, and it lets the base, sidewalls, and tip stay thin.  
*为什么:* stress area（受力区）是指甲长出手指的地方，需要额外的产品。这里最高的点就是 apex（甲峰/拱点），有了它，根部、两侧和前端都可以做薄。  
*Lock this (picture):* A diving board bends most where it leaves the platform. That is where it needs the most support.  
*记重点:* 跳水板在离开跳台的地方弯得最厉害，那里最需要加强。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.262 (PDF 286)

**18. How thick should the sidewalls and edge of an enhancement be?**  
美甲延长的两侧和前缘应该做多厚？  
A. As thick as the apex / 和甲峰一样厚  
B. Credit-card thin / 像信用卡一样薄 ✅  
C. Twice as thick as the apex / 甲峰的两倍厚  
D. Thickest at the edge for strength / 前缘最厚，以增加强度

*为什么:* 美甲延长应从甲小皮处平滑过渡到末端，两侧和前缘像信用卡一样薄。薄才舒服，强度靠甲峰提供。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Take a credit card from your wallet: about 0.76 mm thick. That is the edge you are aiming for.  
*记重点:* 从钱包里拿出一张信用卡：大约 0.76 毫米厚，这就是前缘要的厚度。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.263 (PDF 287)

**19. How can you tell liquid and powder nails have hardened?**  
怎样判断水晶甲已经变硬了？  
A. They look wet and shiny. / 看起来湿润有光泽  
B. They feel warm and soft. / 摸起来温热、柔软  
C. They click when tapped with a brush handle. / 用笔杆轻敲时发出“咔嗒”声 ✅  
D. They turn chalky white. / 变成白垩色  
*Why:* Nails are hard when they make a clicking sound when lightly tapped with a brush handle. Then you can shape them with an abrasive or electric file.  
*为什么:* 用笔杆轻敲指甲时发出清脆的“咔嗒”声，就说明已经变硬，这时可以用锉条或电动磨甲机修形。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Knock on a hard table and it clicks; knock on soft clay and it makes almost no sound.  
*记重点:* 敲硬桌子会“咔咔”响，敲软泥只会发出闷闷的声音。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.345 (PDF 369)

**20. Why does a dappen dish usually have a narrow opening?**  
水晶杯（dappen dish）为什么通常开口很窄？  
A. To measure the mix ratio / 用来量液粉比  
B. To reduce evaporation of the liquid / 减少液体挥发 ✅  
C. To keep the brush hairs pointed / 让笔毛保持尖头  
D. To help the monomer cure / 帮助单体液固化  
*Why:* Narrow openings cut down evaporation of liquids into the air. Wide-mouth jars increase evaporation and let dust and debris in. The dish should also have a lid.  
*为什么:* 窄口能减少液体挥发到空气中。广口容器会加快挥发，还会让灰尘杂质掉进去。水晶杯还应该有盖子。  
*Lock this (picture):* A puddle dries fast, but water in a narrow bottle lasts for days. Less open surface, less escapes.  
*记重点:* 地上一滩水很快就干，窄口瓶里的水却能放好几天。暴露的面越小，挥发得越少。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.327 (PDF 351)
