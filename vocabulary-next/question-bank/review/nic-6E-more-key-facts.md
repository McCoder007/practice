# Set 6E — More Key Facts / 更多重点

Manicure and Pedicure Services · 11 questions · status: reviewed

**1. In a basic manicure, why are the fingertips soaked in the finger bowl?**  
基础修手中，为什么要把指尖泡在泡手碗（finger bowl）里？  
A. To remove the old polish / 卸除旧甲油  
B. To soften the eponychium and cuticle / 软化甲上皮（eponychium）和甲小皮（cuticle） ✅  
C. To disinfect the nail plate / 给甲板消毒  
D. To harden the free edge / 让游离缘（free edge）变硬  
*Why:* Polish comes off first, in step 1. The soak then softens the eponychium (living skin around the nail) and cuticle (dead tissue on the plate), so they can be gently pushed back.  
*为什么:* 第 1 步就已经卸掉甲油了。泡手是为了软化甲上皮（eponychium，指甲周围的活皮肤）和甲小皮（cuticle，粘在甲板上的死组织），之后才能把它们轻轻往后推。  
*Lock this (picture):* Dip hard, dry bread in water and it turns soft. The finger bowl does the same to the cuticle.  
*记重点:* 把又干又硬的面包泡进水里，它就变软了。泡手碗对甲小皮（cuticle）也是一样。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.178 (PDF 202), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.177 (PDF 201), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.179 (PDF 203)

**2. When brushing the nails over the finger bowl, which way do you stroke?**  
在泡手碗（finger bowl）上方刷指甲时，应该朝哪个方向刷？  
A. From the free edge up to the knuckle / 从游离缘往上刷到指节  
B. Side to side across the nail / 在指甲上左右横刷  
C. In small circles on the nail / 在指甲上小圈打转  
D. From the knuckle down to the free edge / 从指节往下刷到游离缘（free edge） ✅  
*Why:* Use a wet nail brush to clean dirt off the fingertips. Use downward strokes, starting at the first knuckle and brushing toward the free edge.  
*为什么:* 用湿的指甲刷（nail brush）刷掉指尖上的脏东西。向下刷，从第一个指节开始，朝游离缘（free edge）方向刷。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Same direction as wiping off old polish: toward the free edge.  
*记重点:* 和擦掉旧甲油的方向一样：朝游离缘（free edge）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.178 (PDF 202)

**3. In a basic manicure, what are nippers used to remove?**  
基础修手中，死皮剪（nippers）是用来剪掉什么的？  
A. Loose, hanging tags of dead skin / 松松挂着的死皮 ✅  
B. The living skin around the nail / 指甲周围的活皮肤  
C. Rough spots on the free edge / 游离缘上粗糙的地方  
D. Dried polish on the nail plate / 甲板上干掉的甲油  
*Why:* Use sharp nippers only on loosely hanging tags of dead skin. Never rip or tear cuticle tags or living skin, because this may lead to infection.  
*为什么:* 锋利的死皮剪只用来剪松松挂着的死皮。绝不能撕扯死皮或活皮肤，因为这可能导致感染。  
*Lock this (picture):* Nip like cutting a loose thread off a shirt: snip only what hangs loose, never pull.  
*记重点:* 剪死皮就像剪掉衣服上松掉的线头：只剪松松挂着的部分，绝不硬扯。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.179 (PDF 203), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.170 (PDF 194)

**4. Why has the three-way buffer replaced the chamois for shining nails?**  
为什么三面抛光条（three-way buffer）取代了麂皮（chamois）来给指甲抛光？  
A. A chamois cannot shine natural nails / 麂皮不能给真甲抛光  
B. A chamois is only for enhancements / 麂皮只能用在加固甲上  
C. A chamois gets contaminated with reuse / 麂皮重复使用会被污染 ✅  
D. A chamois leaves deep scratches / 麂皮会留下很深的刮痕  
*Why:* Milady says the two- or three-way buffer replaces the chamois. A chamois becomes contaminated after many uses. The buffer still gives a beautiful, glossy shine.  
*为什么:* Milady 说两面或三面抛光条取代了麂皮（chamois）。麂皮用过很多次后会被污染（contaminated）。抛光条一样能抛出漂亮的光泽。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Unlike a chamois, most three-way buffers are single-use: throw each one away after one client.  
*记重点:* 和麂皮不同，大多数三面抛光条是一次性的：用完一位顾客就丢掉。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.147 (PDF 171), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.148 (PDF 172)

**5. Before beginning a massage on any client, what should you always do?**  
给任何顾客按摩之前，一定要先做什么？  
A. Apply the base coat / 涂底油（base coat）  
B. Ask the client's permission / 征求顾客的同意 ✅  
C. Soak the hands in hot water / 把手泡在热水里  
D. File the nails once more / 再锉一次指甲  
*Why:* A massage means touching the client's body, so always ask permission first. Also ask if the client has any concerns about receiving a massage.  
*为什么:* 按摩要接触顾客的身体，所以一定要先征求同意，还要问顾客对按摩有没有顾虑。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Like a hug, a massage is touch: ask before you give one.  
*记重点:* 按摩和拥抱一样都是身体接触：给之前先问一声。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.170 (PDF 194), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.211 (PDF 235)

**6. In the hand massage, how is the joint movement of the fingers done?**  
手部按摩中，手指的关节活动（joint movement）怎么做？  
A. Gently rotate each finger in circles / 轻轻转动每根手指画圈 ✅  
B. Pull each finger hard to stretch it / 用力拉每根手指来拉伸  
C. Tap each finger quickly and firmly / 快速用力地拍打每根手指  
D. Squeeze each fingertip for one minute / 每个指尖捏一分钟  
*Why:* Support the arm at the wrist. Hold each finger at the base of the nail and gently rotate it in circles, three to five times, from little finger to thumb.  
*为什么:* 托住手腕处的手臂。捏住每根手指的指甲根部，轻轻转动画圈，每根三到五次，从小指做到大拇指。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Hold your own little finger at the nail base and gently turn it in small circles: that is joint movement.  
*记重点:* 捏住自己小指的指甲根部，轻轻转小圈：这就是关节活动（joint movement）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.184 (PDF 208), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.217 (PDF 241)

**7. How is massage lotion removed from the nail plate before polishing?**  
涂甲油之前，怎样把甲板上的按摩乳液去掉？  
A. Rub it in with a buffer / 用抛光条把它磨进去  
B. Wipe it off with nail oil / 用指甲营养油（nail oil）擦掉  
C. Leave it under the base coat / 留着，涂在底油下面  
D. Cotton with alcohol, acetone, or remover / 用沾了酒精、丙酮或卸甲水的棉花 ✅  
*Why:* Use a small piece of cotton soaked with alcohol, acetone, or polish remover. Clean under the free edge too, or the polish will not stick well.  
*为什么:* 用一小块沾了酒精、丙酮（acetone）或卸甲水的棉花擦。游离缘（free edge）下面也要擦干净，否则甲油粘不牢。  
*Lock this (picture):* Wipe firmly, like when you take off red polish that is hard to remove.  
*记重点:* 擦的时候要用力，就像卸掉很难擦的红色甲油那样。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.181 (PDF 205)

**8. How should the second coat of color polish be applied?**  
第二层彩色甲油应该怎么涂？  
A. In one thick, heavy coat / 涂一层又厚又重的  
B. With the brush fanned again / 再把刷子压开成扇形  
C. In a thin, even coat / 涂一层薄而均匀的 ✅  
D. Only along the free edge / 只涂在游离缘上  
*Why:* For the second coat, do not fan the brush or cap the tip again. Start at the base of the polish curve, move toward the free edge, and apply a thin, even coat.  
*为什么:* 涂第二层时，不要再把刷子压成扇形，也不要再封边。从甲油弧线的底部开始，朝游离缘（free edge）刷，涂一层薄而均匀的甲油。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Build thin layers, one on another. They stick better, so the polish lasts longer.  
*记重点:* 一层层薄涂叠加。这样附着更好，所以甲油更持久。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.183 (PDF 207), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.159 (PDF 183)

**9. After lotion or a mask is applied, how do electric mitts help?**  
涂上乳液或面膜（mask）之后，电热手套（electric mitts）有什么作用？  
A. Warmth helps the products soak in / 温热帮助产品渗入皮肤 ✅  
B. They make the polish dry faster / 让甲油干得更快  
C. They disinfect the client's skin / 给顾客的皮肤消毒  
D. They form a hard wax barrier / 形成一层硬的蜡膜  
*Why:* The hands or feet go into a plastic cover, then into the heated mitts. The warmth helps the conditioning ingredients soak into the skin and adds comfort and relaxation.  
*为什么:* 先把手或脚套进塑料套，再放进电热手套。温热帮助护理成分渗入皮肤，也让顾客更舒服、更放松。  
*Lock this (picture):* Think of a heating pad shaped like a glove: it only adds warmth, and warmth helps lotion soak in.  
*记重点:* 把它想成手套形状的电热垫：它只负责加温，而温热能帮助乳液渗进皮肤。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.140 (PDF 164), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.195 (PDF 219)

**10. Why are terry cloth mitts placed over paraffin?**  
为什么要在蜡（paraffin）外面套上毛巾布手套（terry cloth mitts）？  
A. They melt the paraffin again / 能把蜡重新融化  
B. They hold in heat and let it harden / 能留住热量，又能让蜡变硬 ✅  
C. They are thrown away after use / 用完就丢掉  
D. They peel the paraffin off later / 之后能把蜡剥下来  
*Why:* Terry cloth mitts hold in the heat from the paraffin and let it harden, so it can work as a barrier. Electric mitts do not let paraffin harden.  
*为什么:* 毛巾布手套能留住蜡的热量，又能让蜡变硬，起到屏障作用。电热手套不会让蜡变硬。  
*Lock this (picture):* A terry mitt is a winter coat for the paraffin: heat stays in, but the paraffin still sets hard.  
*记重点:* 毛巾布手套就像给蜡（paraffin）穿上冬天的外套：热量留在里面，蜡照样会变硬。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.195 (PDF 219)

**11. Which hot stones are the safest to use?**  
哪种热石用起来最安全？  
A. Natural stones, because they are porous / 天然石，因为它们多孔  
B. Any stone that feels smooth / 任何摸起来光滑的石头  
C. Synthetic stones that can be disinfected / 可以消毒的合成石 ✅  
D. Stones rinsed in warm water only / 只用温水冲洗的石头  
*Why:* Natural stones are porous (full of tiny holes), so they cannot be disinfected. Synthetic stones can be disinfected and are the safest. Scrub-clean and disinfect the stones between clients.  
*为什么:* 天然石是多孔的（porous，布满小孔），所以不能消毒。合成石（synthetic stones）可以消毒，是最安全的选择。每位顾客之间都要刷洗并消毒石头。  
*Lock this (name-trap):* Name trap: natural sounds safer, but here synthetic wins. Only synthetic stones can be disinfected.  
*记重点:* 名字陷阱：“天然”听起来更安全，但这里是合成石（synthetic stones）胜出。只有合成石可以消毒。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.196 (PDF 220)
