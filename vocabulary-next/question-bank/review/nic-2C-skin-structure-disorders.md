# Set 2C — Skin Structure and Disorders / 皮肤结构与病症

Anatomy and Physiology · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. Which layer of the skin has no blood vessels?**  
皮肤的哪一层没有血管？  
A. Dermis / 真皮（dermis）  
B. Subcutaneous tissue / 皮下组织（subcutaneous tissue）  
C. Reticular layer / 网状层（reticular layer）  
D. Epidermis / 表皮（epidermis） ✅  
*Why:* The epidermis is the outermost and thinnest skin layer. It has no blood vessels and gets its food from the dermis below.  
*为什么:* epidermis（表皮）是皮肤最外层、最薄的一层。它没有血管，靠下面的真皮供给营养。  
*Lock this (real-life):* A shallow scrape that doesn't bleed stayed in the epidermis. Bleeding means the injury reached the dermis.  
*记重点:* 浅浅擦破却不流血，说明只伤到表皮；一流血，就说明伤到真皮了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.61 (PDF 85)

**2. Which skin layer is called the true skin?**  
皮肤的哪一层被称为 true skin（真正的皮肤）？  
A. Epidermis / 表皮（epidermis）  
B. Dermis / 真皮（dermis） ✅  
C. Stratum corneum / 角质层（stratum corneum）  
D. Subcutaneous tissue / 皮下组织（subcutaneous tissue）  
*Why:* The dermis, or true skin, is the inner layer under the epidermis. It is about 25 times thicker and holds blood vessels, nerves, glands, and hair follicles.  
*为什么:* dermis（真皮）是表皮下面的内层，约比表皮厚 25 倍，里面有血管、神经、腺体和毛囊。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* Chinese 真皮 means true skin, and also real leather. Leather is made from animal dermis.  
*记重点:* dermis（真皮）的“真皮”也是“真皮皮鞋”的真皮：皮革就是用动物的真皮层做的。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.63 (PDF 87)

**3. The fatty layer found below the dermis is the**  
真皮下面的脂肪层叫什么？  
A. subcutaneous tissue. / 皮下组织（subcutaneous tissue）。 ✅  
B. epidermis. / 表皮（epidermis）。  
C. papillary layer. / 乳头层（papillary layer）。  
D. stratum lucidum. / 透明层（stratum lucidum）。  
*Why:* Subcutaneous tissue, also called adipose tissue, is the fatty layer under the dermis. It gives the body a smooth shape, stores energy, and cushions the skin.  
*为什么:* subcutaneous tissue（皮下组织）也叫 adipose tissue（脂肪组织），是真皮下面的脂肪层，让身体轮廓平滑、储存能量，并为皮肤提供缓冲。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Sub = under, like a subway under the street. This fat lies under the dermis, like padding under a carpet.  
*记重点:* sub = 在下面，像 subway（地铁）在街道下面。subcutaneous tissue（皮下组织）在真皮下面，就像地毯下面的软垫。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.64 (PDF 88)

**4. Which layer of the epidermis do beauty practitioners care for?**  
美容从业者护理的是表皮的哪一层？  
A. Stratum germinativum / 生发层（stratum germinativum）  
B. Stratum spinosum / 棘层（stratum spinosum）  
C. Stratum corneum / 角质层（stratum corneum） ✅  
D. Stratum lucidum / 透明层（stratum lucidum）  
*Why:* The stratum corneum, or horny layer, is the outermost, visible layer of the epidermis, and the one beauty practitioners care for. Its scalelike cells are always being shed.  
*为什么:* stratum corneum（角质层），也叫 horny layer，是表皮最外面、肉眼可见的一层，也是美容从业者护理的那一层。它的鳞状细胞不断脱落。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* Chinese 角质层: 角 = horn, tough dead cells. Scrubbing off 角质 (去角质) works on this layer.  
*记重点:* 角质层的“角”= 牛角的角，是坚韧的死细胞。平时说的“去角质”，就是在护理这一层 stratum corneum（角质层）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.61 (PDF 85), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.62 (PDF 86)

**5. The clear epidermal layer found mostly on the palms and soles is the**  
主要分布在手掌和脚底的透明表皮层是  
A. stratum granulosum. / 颗粒层（stratum granulosum）。  
B. stratum lucidum. / 透明层（stratum lucidum）。 ✅  
C. stratum corneum. / 角质层（stratum corneum）。  
D. stratum germinativum. / 生发层（stratum germinativum）。  
*Why:* The stratum lucidum is a clear layer just under the surface, made of small oval cells that light can pass through. It is found mostly on the palms and soles.  
*为什么:* stratum lucidum（透明层）是紧贴表面下方的透明层，由光线可以穿过的小椭圆形细胞组成，主要在手掌和脚底。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* Chinese 透明层: 透明 = see-through, like glass. Only thick skin, like your palms and heels, has this layer.  
*记重点:* 透明层的“透明”= 像玻璃一样能透光。只有厚皮肤才有 stratum lucidum（透明层），比如手掌和脚跟。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.62 (PDF 86)

**6. Which epidermal layer produces new skin cells?**  
表皮的哪一层产生新的皮肤细胞？  
A. Stratum corneum / 角质层（stratum corneum）  
B. Stratum lucidum / 透明层（stratum lucidum）  
C. Stratum granulosum / 颗粒层（stratum granulosum）  
D. Stratum germinativum / 生发层（stratum germinativum） ✅  
*Why:* The stratum germinativum, or basal layer, is the deepest living layer of the epidermis. It makes new cells by mitosis to replace those shed from the surface.  
*为什么:* stratum germinativum（生发层），也叫 basal layer（基底层），是表皮最深的活细胞层，通过有丝分裂产生新细胞，补充表面脱落的细胞。  
*Lock this (picture):* Like seeds in the ground: new cells start in this bottom layer, then rise to the top.  
*记重点:* 像埋在土里的种子：新细胞在这最底层生出来，再慢慢升到表面。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.62 (PDF 86)

**7. What is the medical term for athlete's foot?**  
athlete's foot 的医学名称是什么？  
A. Tinea pedis / 足癣（tinea pedis） ✅  
B. Tinea manus / 手癣（tinea manus）  
C. Verruca / 疣（verruca）  
D. Eczema / 湿疹（eczema）  
*Why:* Tinea pedis is a fungal infection of the feet, often between the toes or along the sole. It grows well in dark, wet places. Do not perform services.  
*为什么:* tinea pedis（足癣）是脚部的真菌感染，常出现在脚趾之间或脚底。它在阴暗潮湿的地方容易滋生。不能为顾客做服务。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Pedis = of the foot (pedicure); manus = hand (manicure). Tinea manus is the hand's fungal infection.  
*记重点:* pedis = 足（pedicure 足部护理）；manus = 手（manicure 手部护理）。tinea manus（手癣）是手部的真菌感染。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.86 (PDF 110)

**8. Sudoriferous glands are also called**  
sudoriferous glands 又叫什么？  
A. oil glands. / 油脂腺（oil glands）。  
B. lymph nodes. / 淋巴结（lymph nodes）。  
C. sweat glands. / 汗腺（sweat glands）。 ✅  
D. endocrine glands. / 内分泌腺（endocrine glands）。  
*Why:* Sudoriferous glands are sweat glands. Each has a coiled base and a tube-like duct that opens at the skin surface as a sweat pore.  
*为什么:* sudoriferous glands（汗腺）就是 sweat glands。每个汗腺有一个盘绕的底部和一根管状导管，开口在皮肤表面形成汗孔。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Sudoriferous = sweat; sebaceous = oil. Palms and soles have many sweat glands but no oil glands.  
*记重点:* sudoriferous = 汗，sebaceous = 油。手掌和脚底汗腺很多，却没有皮脂腺：所以手心会出汗，但不出油。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.66 (PDF 90)

**9. Which areas have no sebaceous (oil) glands?**  
哪些部位没有皮脂腺（sebaceous glands，油脂腺）？  
A. Palms and soles / 手掌和脚底 ✅  
B. Face and scalp / 面部和头皮  
C. Arms and legs / 手臂和腿  
D. Chest and back / 胸部和背部  
*Why:* Sebaceous glands are found all over the body except the palms of the hands and soles of the feet. Most of them are on the face and scalp.  
*为什么:* 除了手掌和脚底，全身都有 sebaceous glands（皮脂腺），其中面部和头皮最多。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Most oil glands empty into hair follicles. Palms and soles grow no hair, and have no oil glands.  
*记重点:* 大多数皮脂腺开口在毛囊里。手掌和脚底不长毛，也没有皮脂腺。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.67 (PDF 91)

**10. Sebum flowing from the oil glands is which skin function?**  
皮脂从油脂腺流出，属于皮肤的哪项功能？  
A. Excretion / 排泄（excretion）  
B. Secretion / 分泌（secretion） ✅  
C. Absorption / 吸收（absorption）  
D. Sensation / 感觉（sensation）  
*Why:* Secretion is the skin releasing sebum from the sebaceous glands. Sebum oils the skin and keeps it soft. Sweat leaving the skin is excretion.  
*为什么:* secretion（分泌）是皮脂腺释放皮脂，皮脂润滑皮肤、让它保持柔软。汗液排出皮肤则属于 excretion（排泄）。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Ex = out: excretion pushes waste out with water and salts. Secretion makes something useful.  
*记重点:* ex = 向外：excretion 是把废物连同水和盐排出去；secretion 是制造有用的东西。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.69 (PDF 93)

**11. A flat spot of color on the skin, such as a freckle, is a**  
皮肤上平的色斑，例如雀斑，叫什么？  
A. papule. / 丘疹（papule）。  
B. vesicle. / 水疱（vesicle）。  
C. wheal. / 风团（wheal）。  
D. macule. / 斑疹（macule）。 ✅  
*Why:* A macule is a flat spot or discoloration on the skin, such as a freckle or age spot. It is not raised, and not lower than the skin.  
*为什么:* macule（斑疹）是皮肤上平的斑点或变色，例如雀斑或老年斑，既不凸起也不凹陷。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Touch a freckle: you can't feel it, it's flat. That's a macule. A papule is a bump you can feel.  
*记重点:* 摸摸雀斑：平的，摸不出来，这就是 macule（斑疹）。papule（丘疹）是能摸到的小鼓包。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.78 (PDF 102), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.93 (PDF 117)

**12. Which skin condition is not contagious?**  
哪种皮肤病不会传染？  
A. Warts / 疣（warts）  
B. Herpes simplex / 单纯疱疹（herpes simplex）  
C. Eczema / 湿疹（eczema） ✅  
D. Athlete's foot / 足癣（athlete's foot）  
*Why:* Eczema is an inflammatory, itchy skin disease that is not contagious. Warts, herpes simplex, and athlete's foot are infectious.  
*为什么:* eczema（湿疹）是一种发炎、发痒的皮肤病，不会传染。疣、单纯疱疹和足癣都具有传染性。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Eczema often runs in families and gets worse with things like dry skin or soap, not germs. Nothing to catch.  
*记重点:* 湿疹常有家族遗传倾向，会因皮肤干燥、肥皂等刺激而发作，不是病菌引起的，所以没什么可传染的。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.77 (PDF 101), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.82 (PDF 106)

**13. A crack in the skin that reaches the dermis, like chapped hands, is a**  
深达真皮的皮肤裂口，例如手部皲裂，叫什么？  
A. fissure. / 皲裂（fissure）。 ✅  
B. scale. / 鳞屑（scale）。  
C. crust. / 痂（crust）。  
D. excoriation. / 抓伤（excoriation）。  
*Why:* A fissure is a crack in the skin that goes down into the dermis, as in badly cracked or chapped hands, lips, or feet.  
*为什么:* fissure（皲裂）是深入到真皮的皮肤裂口，例如严重开裂或皲裂的手、嘴唇或脚。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture dry ground cracked by the sun. A crack in the heel deep enough to bleed is a fissure.  
*记重点:* 想象被太阳晒裂的干地。脚跟上的裂口深到会流血（伤到真皮），就是 fissure（皲裂）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.81 (PDF 105), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.104 (PDF 128)

**14. Which is a secondary skin lesion?**  
下列哪一项是继发性皮损（secondary skin lesion）？  
A. Vesicle / 水疱（vesicle）  
B. Crust / 痂（crust） ✅  
C. Macule / 斑疹（macule）  
D. Papule / 丘疹（papule）  
*Why:* A crust, such as a scab, is a secondary lesion: dried matter that builds up on the skin as a wound heals. Vesicles, macules, and papules are primary lesions.  
*为什么:* crust（痂，比如伤口结的 scab）是继发性皮损：伤口愈合时在皮肤表面堆积、变干的物质。水疱、斑疹和丘疹都是原发性皮损（primary lesions）。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Primary comes first, secondary later. A scab forms only after skin has broken open or leaked fluid.  
*记重点:* 原发在先，继发在后。必须先有皮肤破溃或渗出，之后才会结痂。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.80 (PDF 104), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.94 (PDF 118)

**15. Foul-smelling sweat, often noticed on the feet, is**  
气味难闻的汗液，常见于脚部，叫什么？  
A. anhidrosis. / 无汗症（anhidrosis）。  
B. hyperhidrosis. / 多汗症（hyperhidrosis）。  
C. miliaria rubra. / 红痱（miliaria rubra）。  
D. bromhidrosis. / 臭汗症（bromhidrosis）。 ✅  
*Why:* Bromhidrosis is bad-smelling sweat, usually noticed in the armpits or on the feet.  
*为什么:* bromhidrosis（臭汗症）是气味难闻的汗液，通常在腋下或脚上比较明显。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* All -hidrosis words are about sweat. Anhidrosis = too little, hyperhidrosis = too much, bromhidrosis = bad smell.  
*记重点:* 带 -hidrosis 的词都和汗有关：anhidrosis（无汗症）= 汗太少，hyperhidrosis（多汗症）= 汗太多，bromhidrosis（臭汗症）= 汗有臭味。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.82 (PDF 106)

**16. What is the largest organ of the human body?**  
人体最大的器官是什么？  
A. Liver / 肝脏  
B. Brain / 大脑  
C. Skin / 皮肤 ✅  
D. Lungs / 肺  
*Why:* The skin is the largest organ of the body. It protects the muscles, bones, nerves, and blood vessels inside, and is our only barrier against the environment.  
*为什么:* 皮肤是人体最大的器官。它保护体内的肌肉、骨骼、神经和血管，是我们抵御外界环境的唯一屏障。  
*Lock this (picture):* Stretched out, an adult's skin would cover over 3,000 square inches and weigh about 6 to 9 pounds.  
*记重点:* 把成人的皮肤摊开，面积超过 3,000 平方英寸，重约 6 到 9 磅。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.59 (PDF 83), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.60 (PDF 84)

**18. The most common and least severe type of skin cancer is**  
最常见、也最不严重的皮肤癌是  
A. malignant melanoma. / 恶性黑色素瘤（malignant melanoma）。  
B. basal cell carcinoma. / 基底细胞癌（basal cell carcinoma）。 ✅  
C. squamous cell carcinoma. / 鳞状细胞癌（squamous cell carcinoma）。  
D. a normal mole. / 普通的痣（mole）。  
*Why:* Basal cell carcinoma is the most common and least severe skin cancer, often light, shiny nodules. Squamous cell is more serious; malignant melanoma is the most serious.  
*为什么:* basal cell carcinoma（基底细胞癌）是最常见、最不严重的皮肤癌，常呈浅色、珍珠样结节。鳞状细胞癌更严重，恶性黑色素瘤最严重。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Basal cell carcinoma grows slowly and almost never spreads to other organs, so it ranks least severe.  
*记重点:* 基底细胞癌长得慢，几乎不会转移到其他器官，所以最不严重。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.84 (PDF 108)

**20. Verruca is the technical term for a**  
verruca 是什么的专业名称？  
A. freckle. / 雀斑（freckle）。  
B. birthmark. / 胎记（birthmark）。  
C. mole. / 痣（mole）。  
D. wart. / 疣（wart）。 ✅  
*Why:* Verruca is a wart, caused by a virus. It is infectious and can spread from one spot to another, especially along a scratch in the skin.  
*为什么:* verruca（疣）是由病毒引起的。它有传染性，会从一处扩散到另一处，尤其容易沿着皮肤上的抓痕蔓延。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture a tiny cauliflower on the skin: many warts look like that. Don't scratch it, or it can spread.  
*记重点:* 想象皮肤上长了一朵小小的花椰菜：很多疣（verruca）就长这样。别去抓它，会传开。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.86 (PDF 110)
