# Set 6A — Basic Manicure Procedure / 基础修手流程

Manicure and Pedicure Services · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. In a basic manicure, what is done first?**  
基础修手（basic manicure）中，第一步做什么？  
A. Shape the nails with a file / 用锉刀修指甲形状  
B. Remove the old polish / 卸除旧甲油 ✅  
C. Soak the fingertips / 浸泡指尖  
D. Apply cuticle remover / 涂去死皮液  
*Why:* Step 1 of Milady's basic manicure is removing old polish and inspecting the nails. Polish can hide problems you need to see before the service.  
*为什么:* Milady 基础修手的第一步是卸除旧甲油并检查指甲。甲油可能遮住指甲问题，必须先看清楚再继续服务。  
*Lock this (picture):* Like a painted wall: you can't see the cracks until the paint comes off.  
*记重点:* 就像刷过漆的墙：漆不去掉，就看不到裂缝。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.177 (PDF 201)

**2. How long should remover-soaked cotton rest on each nail before wiping?**  
浸透卸甲水的棉片要在每个指甲上停留多久再擦？  
A. About 2 seconds / 约 2 秒  
B. About 1 minute / 约 1 分钟  
C. About 5 minutes / 约 5 分钟  
D. About 10 seconds / 约 10 秒 ✅  
*Why:* Hold the soaked cotton on each nail for about 10 seconds. The remover softens the old polish so it wipes off easily.  
*为什么:* 把浸透卸甲水（polish remover）的棉片按在每个指甲上约 10 秒。卸甲水先软化旧甲油，之后就容易擦掉。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Count silently to 10: that's Milady's timer. Acetone and non-acetone removers can both dry the skin.  
*记重点:* 心里默数到 10，这就是 Milady 的计时方法。含丙酮和不含丙酮的卸甲水都可能让皮肤干燥。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.177 (PDF 201), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.150 (PDF 174)

**3. When wiping off old polish, which way should you stroke?**  
擦掉旧甲油时，应该朝哪个方向擦？  
A. Toward the free edge / 朝游离缘（free edge）方向 ✅  
B. Toward the cuticle / 朝死皮（cuticle）方向  
C. Side to side across the nail / 在指甲上左右横擦  
D. In small circles / 小圈打转  
*Why:* Wipe with a downward stroke toward the free edge. Keep going until all traces of polish are gone.  
*为什么:* 朝游离缘（free edge）方向向下擦，直到甲油完全擦干净。  
*Lock this (picture):* Sweep the color out the open end of the nail, like sweeping dirt out a door.  
*记重点:* 像把灰尘扫出门口一样，把颜色从指甲开口的那一端扫出去。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.177 (PDF 201)

**4. Which product does Milady say is especially helpful for brittle, dry nails?**  
Milady 说哪种产品对脆弱、干燥的指甲特别有帮助？  
A. Acetone polish remover / 含丙酮的卸甲水  
B. Cuticle remover / 去死皮液  
C. Penetrating nail oil / 渗透型指甲营养油（nail oil） ✅  
D. Nail bleach / 指甲漂白剂  
*Why:* Nail creams, lotions, and oils soften dry skin and make natural nails more flexible. Milady says they are especially effective on brittle or dry nails.  
*为什么:* 指甲护理霜、乳液和营养油能软化干燥的皮肤，让天然指甲更有柔韧性。Milady 说它们对脆弱（brittle）或干燥的指甲特别有效。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Brittle = too stiff. Oil adds bend; a methylene glycol hardener adds stiffness and can make brittle nails shatter.  
*记重点:* brittle = 太硬太脆。营养油增加柔韧；含甲二醇（methylene glycol）的硬甲剂只会更硬，可能让脆甲碎裂。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.150 (PDF 174), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.153 (PDF 177)

**5. Why are nails filed before they are soaked?**  
为什么要先锉指甲，再浸泡？  
A. Wet nails are softer and damage easily / 湿指甲较软，容易受损 ✅  
B. Water makes nails too hard to file / 水会让指甲太硬，难以锉  
C. Soaking removes the old polish / 浸泡可以去掉旧甲油  
D. Filing helps the water soak in / 锉过能让水更好渗入  
*Why:* Water absorbs into the nail plate and makes it softer. A soft nail is more easily damaged during filing, so file first, then soak.  
*为什么:* 水会渗入甲板（nail plate），让指甲变软。软的指甲锉的时候更容易受损，所以先锉再泡。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Wet paper tears more easily than dry paper. A soaked nail plate is the same.  
*记重点:* 湿纸比干纸容易撕破。泡过水的甲板也一样。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.155 (PDF 179)

**6. How should a natural nail be filed?**  
天然指甲应该怎么锉？  
A. With a back-and-forth sawing motion / 来回拉锯式锉  
B. From each side toward the center / 从两侧往中间锉 ✅  
C. From the center out to each side / 从中间往两侧锉  
D. In small circles on the free edge / 在游离缘上小圈打转  
*Why:* File from one side to the center, then from the other side to the center. Sawing back and forth can pull apart the nail plate layers and cause splitting and peeling.  
*为什么:* 从一侧锉到中间，再从另一侧锉到中间。来回拉锯会破坏甲板的层次，造成开裂和脱层。  
*Lock this (comparison):* The nail plate is built in layers, like plywood. Sawing pulls those layers apart.  
*记重点:* 甲板像胶合板一样一层层叠起来。拉锯式锉法会把层与层扯开。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.177 (PDF 201)

**7. Nail bleach in a manicure works best on**  
修手时用的指甲漂白剂（nail bleach）对什么效果最好？  
A. fungal discoloration. / 真菌引起的变色。  
B. bruised nail beds. / 甲床瘀青。  
C. ridged nails. / 纵纹甲。  
D. surface stains, such as tobacco. / 表面污渍，例如烟渍。 ✅  
*Why:* Bleaching yellow nails is an optional step. Apply it with a cotton-tipped stick, wear gloves, and keep it off the skin. It works best on surface stains such as tobacco.  
*为什么:* 给发黄的指甲漂白是可选步骤。用包棉花的木棒涂抹，戴手套，不要碰到皮肤。它对烟渍这类表面污渍效果最好。  
*Lock this (contrast):* Stains sitting on top can be lifted. Color from under or inside the nail, like a bruise or fungus, cannot.  
*记重点:* 浮在表面的污渍可以去掉。来自指甲下面或内部的颜色，例如瘀青或真菌，去不掉。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.180 (PDF 204)

**8. After soaking, what does the technician do while drying the client's hand?**  
浸泡后，技师在帮顾客擦干手的同时做什么？  
A. Nips cuticle tags with nippers / 用死皮剪剪掉死皮倒刺  
B. Applies base coat to each nail / 给每个指甲涂底油  
C. Gently pushes back the eponychium / 轻推甲上皮（eponychium） ✅  
D. Bevels the free edge with a board / 用锉板给游离缘倒斜边  
*Why:* Dry the hand with the client's service towel. As you dry, gently push back the eponychium with the towel.  
*为什么:* 用这位顾客专用的服务毛巾擦干手，擦的同时用毛巾轻轻把 eponychium（甲上皮）往后推。  
*Lock this (real-life):* One towel, two jobs: drying and a gentle first push, before any tool touches the skin.  
*记重点:* 一条毛巾做两件事：擦干，顺便先轻推一下，之后才用工具。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.178 (PDF 202)

**9. Where should cuticle remover be applied?**  
去死皮液（cuticle remover）应该涂在哪里？  
A. On the cuticle on the nail plate / 甲板上的死皮 ✅  
B. On the living skin around the nail / 指甲周围的活皮肤  
C. On the palm of the hand / 手掌  
D. Under the free edge only / 只涂在游离缘下面  
*Why:* Cuticle remover goes on the nail plate, not living skin. It is caustic (high pH) and can cause dryness or irritation on live skin.  
*为什么:* cuticle remover（去死皮液）只涂在甲板上的死皮，不要涂在活皮肤上。它是强碱性的（caustic，高 pH），会让活皮肤干燥或受刺激。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Caustic, like drain cleaner: high pH. It dissolves dead tissue, so it can hurt live skin too.  
*记重点:* caustic（强碱性）就像管道疏通剂一样，pH 很高。它能溶解死皮，所以也会伤到活皮肤。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.178 (PDF 202), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.151 (PDF 175)

**10. After cuticle work on both hands, why does the client wash at the sink?**  
两只手的死皮都处理完后，为什么要请顾客去水池洗手？  
A. To soften the nails for polish / 让指甲变软好上甲油  
B. To remove all traces of cuticle remover / 把去死皮液完全洗掉 ✅  
C. To warm the hands for massage / 让手暖起来准备按摩  
D. To disinfect the nail plates / 给甲板消毒  
*Why:* When both hands are complete, the client washes the nail plates with a nail brush at the sink. Leftover cuticle remover on the skin can cause dryness or irritation.  
*为什么:* 两只手都做完后，请顾客到水池用指甲刷清洗甲板。残留在皮肤上的去死皮液会造成干燥或刺激。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Too much cuticle remover on the eponychium can even cause hangnails. Rinse until none is left.  
*记重点:* 甲上皮接触太多去死皮液，甚至会长倒刺（hangnails）。要冲洗到一点不剩。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.179 (PDF 203), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.151 (PDF 175)

**11. Cleaning too hard under the free edge can cause**  
清洁游离缘（free edge）下方太用力，可能会造成什么？  
A. onychophagy. / 咬甲癖（onychophagy）。  
B. nail psoriasis. / 指甲银屑病（nail psoriasis）。  
C. onycholysis. / 甲剥离（onycholysis）。 ✅  
D. ridged nails. / 纵纹甲（ridged nails）。  
*Why:* Clean under the free edge gently. Rough cleaning can break the hyponychium seal and cause onycholysis, the nail plate separating from the nail bed.  
*为什么:* 清洁游离缘下方要轻柔。太用力会破坏 hyponychium（甲下皮）的密封，造成 onycholysis（甲剥离），也就是甲板与甲床分离。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Onycho = nail, lysis = loosening. Onycholysis is the nail loosening from its bed.  
*记重点:* onycho = 指甲，lysis = 松开。onycholysis 就是指甲从甲床上松开。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.179 (PDF 203), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.109 (PDF 133)

**12. At what angle is the underside of the free edge beveled?**  
给游离缘（free edge）下侧倒斜边（bevel）时，锉板保持什么角度？  
A. 10 degrees / 10 度  
B. 75 degrees / 75 度  
C. 90 degrees / 90 度  
D. 45 degrees / 45 度 ✅  
*Why:* Hold a medium-grit board at a 45-degree angle to the underside of the nail and file gently. This removes rough edges and cuticle particles.  
*为什么:* 用中粗砂锉板（medium-grit）以 45 度角贴住指甲下侧轻轻锉。这样可以去掉粗糙边缘和残留的死皮碎屑。  
*Lock this (contrast):* For weak nails, Milady says a fine-grit board or buffer may be better than medium grit.  
*记重点:* 指甲薄弱时，Milady 说用细砂（fine-grit）锉板或抛光块可能比中粗砂更好。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.180 (PDF 204)

哪种甲形的游离缘是方的，但两角是圆的？

*记重点:* squoval = square（方）+ oval（椭圆）：前端是方的，两角像椭圆一样圆润。

**20. Why may polish last longer after a waterless manicure?**  
为什么无水修手（waterless manicure）后甲油可能更持久？  
A. The nails are filed shorter / 指甲锉得比较短  
B. Lotion acts as a base coat / 乳液可以当底油  
C. The nails have not expanded with water / 指甲没有吸水膨胀 ✅  
D. Heated mitts cure the polish / 电热手套能固化甲油  
*Why:* A waterless manicure softens the skin with lotion and heated mitts instead of soaking. Polish is not applied to nails that absorbed water and expanded, so it lasts longer.  
*为什么:* waterless manicure（无水修手）不泡水，而是用乳液和电热手套软化皮肤。甲油不会涂在吸水膨胀的指甲上，所以更持久。  
*Lock this (real-life):* No water needed, so waterless manicures also work where there is no water, like mobile spas.  
*记重点:* 不需要水，所以无水修手也适合没有水的地方，例如流动美甲服务（mobile spa）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.165 (PDF 189)
