# Set 6C — Pedicure Care / 足部护理（修脚）

Manicure and Pedicure Services · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. Why is toenail polish removed first in a pedicure?**  
修脚（pedicure）时为什么要先卸掉脚趾甲油？  
A. To check the toenails for fungus / 检查脚趾甲有没有真菌 ✅  
B. To soften the calluses / 软化老茧  
C. To make the soak work faster / 让浸泡更快见效  
D. To protect the toenails from water / 保护脚趾甲不碰水  
*Why:* Removing polish first lets you check the toenails for fungus. Then you know whether you can move forward with the service without disappointing the client.  
*为什么:* 先卸甲油可以检查脚趾甲有没有真菌（fungus）。这样就知道能不能继续服务，不会做到一半才让顾客失望。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Polish off → fungus spotted → stop before the soak. A fungal nail needs a doctor, not a pedicure.  
*记重点:* 卸掉甲油 → 发现真菌 → 泡脚前就停下。真菌感染的指甲要看医生，不是做修脚。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.213 (PDF 237), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.109 (PDF 133)

**2. How long do the client's feet soak before the pedicure begins?**  
开始修脚前，顾客的脚要泡多久？  
A. About 1 minute / 约 1 分钟  
B. 20 to 30 minutes / 20 到 30 分钟  
C. 5 to 10 minutes / 5 到 10 分钟 ✅  
D. Over an hour / 超过 1 小时  
*Why:* Let the client's feet soak for 5 to 10 minutes to soften and clean them before you begin the pedicure.  
*为什么:* 让顾客的脚浸泡 5 到 10 分钟，软化并清洁双脚，然后再开始修脚。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Clock check: in a one-hour pedicure, the consultation and soak should be finished by minute 12.  
*记重点:* 看时间：一小时的修脚，咨询加浸泡应该在第 12 分钟前完成。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.213 (PDF 237), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.205 (PDF 229)

**3. Before the client's feet go into the bath, what must you check?**  
顾客的脚放进足浴盆之前，你必须检查什么？  
A. The length of the toenails / 脚趾甲的长度  
B. The water temperature / 水温 ✅  
C. The polish color choice / 选好的甲油颜色  
D. The toe separators / 分趾器  
*Why:* Check the temperature of the pedicure bath for safety. Then put on gloves and place the client's feet in the water.  
*为什么:* 为了安全，先检查足浴盆的水温，然后戴上手套，再把顾客的脚放进水里。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Diabetes can damage foot nerves → the client can't feel water that's too hot → can't warn you.  
*记重点:* 糖尿病可能损伤脚部神经 → 顾客感觉不到水太烫 → 没法提醒你，结果被烫伤。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.213 (PDF 237), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.43 (PDF 67)

**4. Unless the client asks otherwise, toenails are trimmed**  
除非顾客另有要求，脚趾甲应该怎么剪？  
A. as short as possible. / 尽量剪短。  
B. to a rounded point. / 剪成圆尖形。  
C. well past the end of the toe. / 留得远超过脚趾尖。  
D. straight across, even with the toe tip. / 平剪，与脚趾尖齐平。 ✅  
*Why:* Trim toenails straight across and even with the end of the toes, unless the client requests otherwise. Leave no rough edges or hooks that could invite infection.  
*为什么:* 除非顾客另有要求，脚趾甲要平剪，与脚趾末端齐平。不要留下粗糙边缘或倒钩，以免引起感染。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Milady warns that the big toenail is usually the hardest to trim. Slow down there.  
*记重点:* Milady 提醒：大脚趾甲通常最难剪，剪到那里要放慢。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.214 (PDF 238)

**5. Why should toenails not be trimmed too short?**  
为什么脚趾甲不能剪得太短？  
A. The polish will not stick / 甲油会粘不住  
B. The nail will grow faster / 指甲会长得更快  
C. Calluses will form on the toe / 脚趾上会长老茧  
D. It can break the hyponychium seal / 可能破坏甲下皮的密封 ✅  
*Why:* Trimming too short can break the seal of the hyponychium. That seal protects the area beneath the toenail from infection.  
*为什么:* 剪得太短可能破坏 hyponychium（甲下皮）的密封。这道密封保护趾甲下面的部位不受感染。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Hypo = under, onych = nail. The hyponychium is the skin seal under the free edge.  
*记重点:* hypo = 下面，onych = 指甲。hyponychium 就是游离缘下面那道皮肤密封。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.197 (PDF 221)

**6. When educating a client about a callus, what does Milady suggest discussing?**  
向顾客讲解老茧（callus）时，Milady 建议讨论什么？  
A. Removing its cause, such as certain shoes / 去除成因，例如不再穿某双鞋 ✅  
B. Shaving it off at home with a razor / 在家用剃刀把它削掉  
C. Soaking the feet in bleach water / 用漂白水泡脚  
D. Stopping all foot lotion / 完全停用足部乳液  
*Why:* Educate the client about how calluses form. Discuss removing the cause, such as no longer wearing a certain pair of shoes, and suggest home-care softening products between visits.  
*为什么:* 向顾客讲解老茧是怎么形成的。讨论去除成因，例如不再穿某双鞋，并推荐在两次护理之间在家使用的软化产品。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* A tight shoe rubs one spot daily → the skin there thickens → callus. Stop the rubbing at its source.  
*记重点:* 紧鞋每天磨同一个地方 → 那里的皮肤变厚 → 长成老茧。要从源头停止摩擦。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.202 (PDF 226)

**7. A client has an ingrown toenail. What should the nail technician do?**  
顾客有嵌甲（ingrown toenail），美甲师应该怎么做？  
A. Use the nail rasp under the edge / 用甲锉刀（nail rasp）伸到边缘下面锉  
B. Refer the client to a podiatrist / 建议顾客去看足科医生 ✅  
C. Cut out the ingrown corner / 把嵌进去的角剪掉  
D. Lift the edge with a curette / 用刮匙（curette）把边缘挑起来  
*Why:* Never use the rasp on a nail that is already ingrown. Caring for ingrown nails is medical treatment for a physician or podiatrist; nail technicians can prevent them but not treat them.  
*为什么:* 已经嵌进去的指甲绝不能用甲锉刀处理。处理嵌甲属于医疗，必须由医生或足科医生（podiatrist）来做；美甲师可以预防嵌甲，但不能治疗。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Podiatrist: pod = foot, iatr = healer. A foot doctor treats it; you prevent it.  
*记重点:* podiatrist：pod = 脚，iatr = 医治者，也就是足科医生。医生治疗，你负责预防。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.198 (PDF 222), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.199 (PDF 223)

**8. In a basic pedicure, the nail rasp is used, if needed, on**  
基础修脚中，甲锉刀（nail rasp）在需要时用在哪里？  
A. every toenail. / 每一个脚趾甲。  
B. the little toe only. / 只用在小脚趾。  
C. the big toe only. / 只用在大脚趾。 ✅  
D. the top of the nail. / 指甲表面。  
*Why:* Use the nail rasp only on the big toe, if needed. Draw it gently along the side of the free edge to smooth sharp points; never use it on top of the nail.  
*为什么:* 甲锉刀只在需要时用在大脚趾上。沿着游离缘的侧边轻轻拉，把尖角磨平；绝不能用在指甲表面。  
*Lock this (real-life):* The rasp files in one direction only; the angled kind is easier to control under the free edge.  
*记重点:* 甲锉刀只能朝一个方向锉；带角度的款式在游离缘下面更好控制。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.214 (PDF 238), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.198 (PDF 222), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.199 (PDF 223)

**9. Where is callus softener applied during a pedicure?**  
修脚时，老茧软化剂（callus softener）涂在哪里？  
A. Over the entire foot and leg / 整只脚和腿  
B. On the toenail plates / 脚趾甲板上  
C. On the callused areas only / 只涂在有老茧的部位 ✅  
D. Between the toes / 脚趾之间  
*Why:* Apply callus softener to the callused areas only, for the time the manufacturer directs. Some are alkaline, so wear gloves; wrong use can hurt the client.  
*为什么:* callus softener（老茧软化剂）只涂在有老茧的部位，停留时间按厂家说明。有些是碱性的，要戴手套；使用不当会伤到顾客。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Strong enough to soften thick callus → strong enough to harm thin, healthy skin.  
*记重点:* 强到能软化厚老茧 → 也强到能伤害薄薄的健康皮肤。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.214 (PDF 238), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.202 (PDF 226)

**10. Why should calluses be softened and smoothed, not removed?**  
为什么老茧（callus）只能软化、磨平，而不是去除？  
A. They contain the nail matrix / 老茧里有甲母  
B. They grow back in one day / 老茧一天就会长回来  
C. Removing them takes too long / 去除老茧太花时间  
D. They protect the skin underneath / 老茧保护下面的皮肤 ✅  
*Why:* Calluses protect the skin under them in stress areas, as on people who stand all day. Soften and smooth them; do not thin them too much or remove them.  
*为什么:* 老茧在受力部位保护下面的皮肤，比如整天站着工作的人。只能软化、磨平，不能磨得太薄或完全去掉。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like a guitarist's fingertips: the hard skin is armor the body built from use.  
*记重点:* 就像吉他手的指尖：那层硬皮是身体因为反复使用而长出的盔甲。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.202 (PDF 226)

**11. Using a razor-type callus shaver can make calluses**  
使用剃刀式去茧刀（callus shaver），可能让老茧怎样？  
A. grow back heavier. / 长得更厚。 ✅  
B. disappear for good. / 永远消失。  
C. turn soft and moist. / 变得柔软湿润。  
D. grow back thinner. / 长得更薄。  
*Why:* Never use a razor-type callus shaver. It is against the law in most states, can cause infection, and makes calluses grow back heavier as the skin protects itself.  
*为什么:* 绝不能用剃刀式去茧刀。在大多数州这是违法的，会引起感染，而且皮肤为了保护自己，老茧会长得更厚。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Safe route: soften with a callus product, then use a foot file to remove only a safe amount.  
*记重点:* 安全做法：先用去茧产品软化，再用脚锉只磨掉安全的量。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.206 (PDF 230)

**12. A pedicure client has tremors. How should you prepare?**  
修脚顾客手脚会不自主颤抖（tremors），你应该怎么准备？  
A. Ask the client to hold their legs still / 请顾客自己把腿保持不动  
B. Support the entire leg, behind knees and ankles / 支撑整条腿，包括膝盖后面和脚踝 ✅  
C. Skip the foot soak / 省略泡脚  
D. Work on one foot only / 只做一只脚  
*Why:* Tremors or shaking a client can't stop may mean a nerve problem. Support the entire leg, including behind the knees and ankles, so the client never has to hold up their own legs.  
*为什么:* 颤抖或不自主动作可能代表神经方面的问题。要支撑整条腿，包括膝盖后面和脚踝，让顾客完全不用自己撑着腿。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Same rule for hands: Milady supports the whole forearm, elbow to palm, on a rolled towel.  
*记重点:* 手也是同样的规则：Milady 用卷起的毛巾把整个前臂，从手肘到手掌都托住。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.210 (PDF 234), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.169 (PDF 193)

**13. When exfoliating with a scrub, where should extra pressure be used?**  
用磨砂膏去角质时，哪里要多用点力？  
A. On the tops of the toes / 脚趾上面  
B. On the heels and callus-prone areas / 脚跟和容易长茧的部位 ✅  
C. On the front of the shin / 小腿前侧  
D. Between the toes / 脚趾之间  
*Why:* Exfoliate the foot with a scrub to remove dry or scaly skin. Use extra pressure on the heels and other areas where calluses and dry skin tend to build up.  
*为什么:* 用磨砂膏（scrub）给脚去角质，去除干燥或脱屑的皮肤。在脚跟和其他容易长茧、干燥的部位多用点力。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Heels carry your weight, and soles have no oil glands, so hard, dry skin builds there.  
*记重点:* 脚跟要承重，脚底又没有皮脂腺（oil glands），所以硬皮和干皮容易积在那里。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.215 (PDF 239), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.67 (PDF 91)

**14. For a client with a chronic illness such as diabetes, which implement should be avoided?**  
顾客有糖尿病等慢性病时，应该避免使用哪种工具？  
A. A curette / 刮匙（curette）  
B. A fine-grit file / 细砂锉  
C. A metal pusher / 金属推棒（metal pusher） ✅  
D. Toe separators / 分趾器  
*Why:* Do not use metal pushers or sharp implements on clients with chronic illnesses. Instead, use a curette gently to remove nonliving tissue and a fine-grit abrasive on the nails.  
*为什么:* 慢性病顾客不能用金属推棒或尖锐工具。可以改用刮匙轻轻去除死组织，并用细砂锉处理指甲。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Slow-healing skin + one tiny unseen cut → infection, and in the worst cases losing a toe or foot.  
*记重点:* 伤口愈合慢 + 一个看不见的小伤口 → 感染，最严重会失去脚趾甚至整只脚。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.199 (PDF 223), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.211 (PDF 235), Milady Standard Foundations p.132 (PDF 627)

**15. There is buildup between the client's toes. What should you do first?**  
顾客脚趾之间有积垢，你应该先做什么？  
A. Soak longer with a urea softener / 用含尿素的软化产品多泡一会儿 ✅  
B. Scrape it with a curette / 用刮匙刮掉  
C. Skip cleaning between the toes / 跳过脚趾间的清洁  
D. Rub it off with a foot file / 用脚锉磨掉  
*Why:* A complete pedicure cleans between the toes. If there is buildup, soak longer with a softening product containing urea, then clean gently with a disposable towelette or washcloth.  
*为什么:* 完整的修脚要清洁脚趾之间。如果有积垢，先用含尿素（urea）的软化产品多泡一会儿，再用一次性湿巾或毛巾轻轻擦干净。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Urea is in many creams for dry, cracked heels. It holds water in hard skin, so it softens.  
*记重点:* 很多治脚跟干裂的乳霜里都有 urea（尿素）。它把水分留在硬皮里，硬皮就变软了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.210 (PDF 234)

**16. Before clipping toenails for a client with health considerations, what should you check?**  
给有健康问题的顾客剪脚趾甲前，应该检查什么？  
A. The color of the old polish / 旧甲油的颜色  
B. How fast the toenails grow / 脚趾甲长得多快  
C. The size of the lunula / 甲半月的大小  
D. Underneath the nail for attached skin / 指甲下面有没有粘连的皮肤 ✅  
*Why:* Always look underneath the nails before clipping. Skin can be attached to the center of the nail, and simply cutting straight across can cut it.  
*为什么:* 剪之前一定要看指甲下面。可能有皮肤粘在指甲中间，直接平剪就会剪到皮肤。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture a sticker stuck to the back of a page: cut the page, and you cut the sticker too.  
*记重点:* 想象一张纸背面贴着贴纸：剪纸的时候，贴纸也会一起被剪到。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.211 (PDF 235)

**17. A pedicure client shaved their legs this morning. What does Milady suggest?**  
修脚顾客今天早上刚刮过腿毛。Milady 建议怎么做？  
A. Go ahead with a full spa pedicure / 照常做完整的水疗修脚  
B. Use a stronger foot soak / 用更强的泡脚产品  
C. Do the leg scrub with extra care / 小心地做腿部磨砂  
D. Offer a waterless pedicure on the feet only / 只做脚部的无水修脚 ✅  
*Why:* Shaving shortly before a pedicure leaves tiny unseen openings (microtrauma) that can let germs in. Offer a basic waterless pedicure on the feet only and reschedule any soak, scrub, or leg work.  
*为什么:* 修脚前刚刮过毛，会留下看不见的细小伤口（microtrauma，微创伤），病菌可能进入。只做脚部的基础无水修脚，泡脚、磨砂或腿部护理另约时间。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Micro = small, trauma = wound. Waxing, plucking, and insect bites can leave the same tiny openings.  
*记重点:* micro = 微小，trauma = 伤口。蜜蜡脱毛、拔毛和蚊虫叮咬也会留下同样的细小开口。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.205 (PDF 229), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.204 (PDF 228), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.197 (PDF 221)

**19. A client wants help with scaly feet. What must happen first?**  
顾客想改善脚部脱屑（scaly feet），首先必须做什么？  
A. Start weekly exfoliation right away / 马上开始每周去角质

*为什么:* 真菌感染看起来可能就像脚部脱屑。顾客必须先看足科医生；真菌检测阴性后，才能开始每周的去角质护理。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Athlete's foot is a fungus, yet it can look like plain dry, peeling skin.  
*记重点:* 足癣（athlete's foot，俗称“脚气”）是真菌感染，看起来却可能只像普通的干燥脱皮。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.206 (PDF 230), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.86 (PDF 110)

**20. A client cannot move into the pedicure chair. What does Milady suggest?**  
顾客没办法坐到修脚椅上。Milady 建议怎么做？  
A. Refer the client to a medical office / 转介到医疗机构  
B. Use a portable basin or a dry pedicure / 用便携式泡脚盆，或做干式修脚 ✅  
C. Do a manicure instead / 改做修手  
D. Skip the pedicure entirely / 完全不做修脚  
*Why:* Use a portable water basin or perform a dry pedicure. In a dry pedicure you still shorten and shape the nails, clean the cuticles, and gently apply lotion.  
*为什么:* 可以用便携式泡脚盆，或做干式修脚（dry pedicure）。干式修脚同样要修短、修形、清理死皮，并轻轻涂乳液。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Bring the pedicure to the client, not the client to the chair. Still check between the toes.  
*记重点:* 把修脚服务带到顾客身边，不必硬把顾客挪到椅子上。脚趾之间照样要检查。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.209 (PDF 233)
