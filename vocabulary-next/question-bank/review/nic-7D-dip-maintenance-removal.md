# Set 7D — Dip Powder, Maintenance, and Removal / 浸粉、维护与卸除

Nail Enhancements: Application, Maintenance, and Removal · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. How does a dip system form a hard coating on the nail?**  
浸粉系统（dip system）怎样在指甲上形成坚硬的涂层？  
A. The resin absorbs the powder. / 树脂吸收了粉。 ✅  
B. It dries in the open air. / 它在空气中自然风干。  
C. The powder is mixed with water. / 粉和水混合。  
D. Heat from a dryer melts it. / 吹风机的热把它融化。  
*Why:* Dip systems use resin and a very fine polymer powder. The resin absorbs the powder to create a hard coating, and activator helps the layers dry.  
*为什么:* dip system（浸粉系统）用树脂和很细的聚合物粉。树脂吸收粉后形成坚硬的涂层，activator（激活剂）帮助各层变干。  
*Lock this (picture):* Dip a sticky fingertip in sugar and it coats evenly. The resin is the sticky part.  
*记重点:* 把发黏的指尖蘸进糖里，会均匀裹上一层。树脂就是那层“黏”。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.284 (PDF 308), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.285 (PDF 309)

**2. Compared with acrylic powder, dip powder is**  
和水晶粉相比，浸粉（dip powder）是怎样的？  
A. coarse and always clear. / 颗粒粗，而且总是透明的。  
B. made only for clear extensions. / 只能用来做透明延长。  
C. very fine, usually heavily pigmented. / 非常细，通常颜色很浓。 ✅  
D. cured under a UV lamp. / 要用 UV 灯固化。  
*Why:* Dip powder is a very fine polymer powder, usually heavily pigmented with color. Milady says it is very different from the powder used for liquid and powder nails.  
*为什么:* dip powder（浸粉）是很细的聚合物粉，通常颜色很浓。Milady 说它和水晶甲（liquid and powder）用的粉很不一样。  
*Lock this (picture):* Think eyeshadow: so fine it clings in a thin, even coat, and packed with color.  
*记重点:* 想象眼影粉：细到能薄薄均匀地附着，而且颜色很浓。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.284 (PDF 308)

**3. One advantage of dip color over gel polish is that dip**  
和甲油胶相比，浸粉上色的一个优点是什么？  
A. needs no UV or LED lamp. / 不需要 UV 或 LED 灯。 ✅  
B. never needs maintenance. / 永远不用维护。  
C. never needs a top resin. / 完全不需要封层树脂。  
D. needs no resin at all. / 完全不需要树脂。  
*Why:* Dip systems give a color service that lasts about 14 days without an LED or UV lamp. Before dip, long-wear color meant gel polish or colored hard gel and a lamp.  
*为什么:* 浸粉能提供持久约 14 天的上色服务，而且不需要 LED 或 UV 灯。在浸粉出现之前，持久的颜色只能靠甲油胶或彩色硬胶，都要照灯。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Dip resin is cyanoacrylate, like super glue: it sets without light, and activator speeds it up.  
*记重点:* 浸粉树脂是氰基丙烯酸酯，和强力胶同类：不用光就会变硬，激活剂让它更快。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.285 (PDF 309), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.282 (PDF 306)

**4. Why wipe the base resin brush before returning it to the bottle?**  
为什么底层树脂的笔刷放回瓶里之前要先擦一下？  
A. It helps the resin dry faster. / 能让树脂干得更快。  
B. It removes the activator odor. / 能去除激活剂的气味。  
C. It adds color to the base. / 能给底层树脂添加颜色。  
D. Powder bits would thicken the resin. / 粉粒会让树脂变稠、拉丝。 ✅  
*Why:* After powder is applied, tiny particles cling to the brush. Put back in the bottle, they make the resin thick, stringy, and unusable, so wipe it on a lint-free towel.  
*为什么:* 上过粉后，细小的粉粒会粘在笔刷上。放回瓶里会让树脂变稠、拉丝而无法使用，所以要先在无尘巾上擦一下。  
*Lock this (real-life):* After touching activator, wipe the brush quickly too, or resin hardens on it and needs a 10-minute acetone soak.  
*记重点:* 笔刷碰过激活剂后也要马上擦干净，否则树脂会在笔上变硬，得泡丙酮约 10 分钟。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.285 (PDF 309)

**5. To avoid dipping many clients' fingers into one powder jar, you can**  
为了避免多位客人把手指伸进同一罐粉里，可以怎么做？  
A. use a small dish and discard leftovers. / 用水晶杯（dappen dish）分装，剩粉丢掉。 ✅  
B. add activator to the powder jar. / 往粉罐里加激活剂。  
C. wipe the jar rim with alcohol. / 用酒精擦罐口。  
D. pour used powder back in the jar. / 把用过的粉倒回罐里。  
*Why:* Germs are unlikely to grow in dip powder. Still, you can pour powder over the nail or use a small dappen dish and throw out the unused powder after each client.  
*为什么:* 病菌不太可能在浸粉里生长，但你可以把粉倒在指甲上，或用水晶杯（dappen dish）装粉，每位客人用完后把剩下的粉丢掉。  
*Lock this (picture):* Like sauce in small side dishes at a restaurant: one dish per guest, leftovers thrown out.  
*记重点:* 就像餐厅给每位客人一小碟酱料：各用各的，剩下的倒掉。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.284 (PDF 308), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.285 (PDF 309)

**6. At dip maintenance, the client wants a different color. You should**  
浸粉维护时，客人想换颜色，应该怎么做？  
A. fill the new growth in the new color. / 用新颜色补新长出的部分。  
B. soak off and do a new service. / 泡掉旧甲，重新做一次。 ✅  
C. dip the new color over the old. / 直接在旧颜色上蘸新颜色。  
D. file off only the top resin. / 只锉掉最上层的封层树脂。  
*Why:* Dip nails need maintenance after two weeks. If the client keeps the same color, the nails can be filled. A new color means soaking off and doing a new service.  
*为什么:* 浸粉甲两周后需要维护。颜色不变就可以补甲（fill）；要换颜色，就得泡掉、重新做。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* A dip fill files off only the clear top resin; the old color stays underneath, so it can't change.  
*记重点:* 浸粉补甲只锉掉表面的透明树脂，旧颜色还留在下面，所以没法换色。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.286 (PDF 310), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.310 (PDF 334)

**7. Moving the apex back into the right place during maintenance is a**  
维护时把甲峰（apex）调回正确位置，叫什么？  
A. dry manicure. / 干式修甲（dry manicure）。  
B. soak-off. / 卸甲（soak-off）。  
C. overlay. / 加固层（overlay）。  
D. rebalance. / 重新平衡（rebalance）。 ✅  
*Why:* A rebalance structurally corrects where the apex sits, to keep the nail strong and balanced. A fill is adding product to the new growth.  
*为什么:* rebalance（重新平衡）是从结构上修正甲峰的位置，让指甲保持牢固和平衡。fill（补甲）是在新长出的部分加产品。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Re- = again: re-balance. As the nail grows out, the apex slides forward toward the tip.  
*记重点:* re- = 再一次：re-balance 就是再次平衡。指甲长出去，甲峰也跟着往前移。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.283 (PDF 307)

**8. At a four-week wrap maintenance, what is added to the new growth?**  
包甲做四周维护时，新长出的部分要加什么？  
A. A fabric strip plus resin / 一小条布料加树脂 ✅  
B. Only a thin coat of resin / 只上一层薄树脂  
C. An acid-based primer / 酸性结合剂  
D. A new nail tip / 一片新甲片  
*Why:* At two weeks, buffing and adding resin to the new growth is enough. At four weeks, the regrowth needs a small strip of fabric plus resin to stay strong.  
*为什么:* 两周维护时，抛磨后在新长出的部分加树脂就够了。到四周时，新长部分要贴一小条布再上树脂，才能保持牢固。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Resin is the glue; fabric is the frame. Four weeks of bare regrowth needs its own frame.  
*记重点:* 树脂是胶，布是骨架。长了四周、没有布的新甲，要补上自己的骨架。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.284 (PDF 308), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.292 (PDF 316)

**9. How often should liquid and powder enhancements get maintenance?**  
水晶甲（liquid and powder）多久需要维护一次？  
A. Every two to three days / 每两三天  
B. Every two to three weeks / 每两到三周 ✅  
C. Every two to three months / 每两三个月  
D. Only when one breaks / 只在断了的时候  
*Why:* Liquid and powder needs maintenance every two to three weeks, depending on nail growth. Without it, enhancements tend to lift, crack, or break and can damage the natural nail.  
*为什么:* 水晶甲需要每两到三周维护一次，视指甲生长速度而定。不维护的话，美甲容易起翘、裂开或断裂，还可能损伤真甲。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Nails grow 2.5 to 3 mm a month, so a bare strip shows at the cuticle within weeks.  
*记重点:* 指甲每月长 2.5 到 3 毫米，几周内甲根处就会露出一截空白。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.330 (PDF 354), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.103 (PDF 127)

**10. During a liquid and powder fill, the ledge at the new growth is**  
水晶甲补甲时，新长出部分的那道台阶（ledge）要怎样处理？  
A. clipped off with nippers. / 用死皮剪剪掉。  
B. left for new product to cover. / 留着，让新产品盖住。  
C. soaked in acetone to soften. / 泡丙酮把它泡软。  
D. filed flush with the nail plate. / 锉到和甲面齐平。 ✅  
*Why:* File the ledge flat until it is flush with the nail plate, and file away any lifting. Take care not to dig into the natural nail plate.  
*为什么:* 把台阶锉平，直到和甲面齐平，并锉掉起翘的部分。小心不要锉伤真甲甲面。  
*Lock this (picture):* Smooth the step into a ramp, so new product blends in with no visible line.  
*记重点:* 把台阶磨成斜坡，新产品才能衔接得看不出接缝。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.343 (PDF 367)

**11. Before repairing a crack in liquid and powder, file the area around it**  
修补水晶甲裂缝之前，裂缝周围要锉到什么程度？  
A. just enough to remove shine. / 只锉掉光泽就好。  
B. down into the natural nail. / 一直锉进真甲里。  
C. until it is paper-thin. / 锉到像纸一样薄。 ✅  
D. not at all; just add product. / 不用锉，直接加产品。  
*Why:* File the area around the crack or break until it is paper-thin, so new product can cover the whole area and reinforce the repair. Then place a form under the nail before adding product.  
*为什么:* 把裂缝或断口周围锉到像纸一样薄，新产品才能覆盖整个区域并加固修补。锉薄后，再在指甲下面放纸托，然后加产品。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Patching a wall? Sand the crack's edges thin first, or the patch sits on top as a bump.  
*记重点:* 补墙的时候，要先把裂缝边缘磨薄，不然补上去会鼓起一个包。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.331 (PDF 355)

**12. How long should liquid and powder enhancements soak in acetone?**  
水晶甲要在丙酮里泡大约多久？  
A. 1 to 2 minutes / 1 到 2 分钟  
B. 5 to 10 minutes / 5 到 10 分钟  
C. 20 to 30 minutes / 20 到 30 分钟 ✅  
D. 2 to 3 hours / 2 到 3 小时  
*Why:* Soak liquid and powder enhancements in acetone or product remover for 20 to 30 minutes, or as long as needed. A wooden pusher gently pushes off the softened product.  
*为什么:* 把水晶甲泡在丙酮或卸甲液里 20 到 30 分钟，或泡到能卸掉为止。用木推棒轻轻推掉变软的产品。  
*Lock this (contrast):* A plain tip comes off in 5 to 10 minutes; acrylic needs the full 20 to 30.  
*记重点:* 单纯的甲片泡 5 到 10 分钟就掉；水晶甲要泡足 20 到 30 分钟。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.331 (PDF 355), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.349 (PDF 373), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.273 (PDF 297)

**13. How can acetone be warmed safely to speed up removal?**  
怎样安全地加热丙酮来加快卸甲？  
A. Heat the acetone in a microwave. / 用微波炉加热丙酮。  
B. Warm it in the towel warmer. / 放进毛巾加热柜里加热。  
C. Blow a hair dryer on it. / 用吹风机对着吹。  
D. Set the acetone bowl in hot water. / 把丙酮碗放进一碗热水里。 ✅  
*Why:* Place the glass bowl of acetone inside another bowl of hot water. This heats the acetone safely and speeds up removal.  
*为什么:* 把装丙酮的玻璃碗放进另一碗热水里，这样可以安全地加热丙酮，加快卸甲。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Acetone is highly flammable: no flame, no microwave, just a hot water bath.  
*记重点:* 丙酮极易燃：不能用火、不能用微波炉，只能隔着热水加热。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.349 (PDF 373)

**14. Why not keep taking nails out of the acetone during removal?**  
卸甲时为什么不要老是把指甲从丙酮里拿出来？  
A. Air makes the acetone stronger. / 空气会让丙酮变得更强。  
B. The natural nail absorbs the product. / 真甲会把产品吸进去。  
C. The softened product quickly hardens again. / 已经变软的产品很快又会变硬。 ✅  
D. Acetone turns the nails white. / 丙酮会让指甲变白。  
*Why:* Product taken out of the remover too often rehardens quickly, so removal takes longer. Leave the nails in until the product melts off, pushing gently with a wooden stick.  
*为什么:* 产品频繁离开卸甲液会很快重新变硬，反而卸得更慢。要一直泡到产品融掉，中途用木棒轻轻推一推。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Acetone evaporates fast in open air, and as it leaves, the softened product firms up again.  
*记重点:* 丙酮在空气中挥发得很快，丙酮一走，变软的产品又会硬回去。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.331 (PDF 355), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.332 (PDF 356)

**15. Why not use cheap hardware-store acetone on clients?**  
为什么不能给客人用五金店买的便宜工业丙酮？  
A. It is too weak to remove product. / 它太弱，卸不掉产品。  
B. It isn't tested or approved for skin. / 它没有经过用于皮肤的测试或批准。 ✅  
C. It hardens enhancements instead. / 它反而会让美甲变硬。  
D. It must be mixed with water first. / 必须先兑水才能用。  
*Why:* Industrial acetone has not been tested or approved for human skin. Professional removers are usually enriched with aloe and other softeners that help prevent dry skin.  
*为什么:* 工业丙酮没有经过用于人体皮肤的测试或批准。专业卸甲产品通常添加了芦荟等柔肤成分，能防止皮肤干燥。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like using kitchen degreaser as hand soap: it may clean, but it was never made for skin.  
*记重点:* 就像拿厨房去油剂当洗手液：也许洗得掉，但它本来就不是给皮肤用的。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.331 (PDF 355)

**16. After removal, natural nails seem thin and bendy. This is because they**  
卸甲后真甲显得又薄又软，这是因为它们  
A. were weakened by the enhancement. / 被美甲削弱了。  
B. were dissolved by the acetone. / 被丙酮溶解了。  
C. hold extra moisture from soaking. / 浸泡后含有额外的水分。 ✅  
D. still have primer on them. / 上面还留着结合剂。  
*Why:* After 30 minutes of soaking, nail plates hold more moisture and bend more easily. They are not weakened, and they feel firmer again as they dry over the next 24 hours.  
*为什么:* 浸泡 30 分钟后，甲板含有更多水分，所以更容易弯。它们并没有变弱，接下来 24 小时内水分散失后就会重新变硬。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Like nails after a long bath: soft and bendy, then firm again once the water dries out.  
*记重点:* 就像泡了很久的澡后的指甲：先是又软又弯，水分散掉后又变硬。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.332 (PDF 356)

**17. After filing off most of a hard gel, the thin layer left is**  
硬胶大部分锉掉后，剩下的薄薄一层怎么办？  
A. soaked off in acetone. / 用丙酮泡掉。  
B. left to grow out with the nail. / 留着，随指甲长出去。 ✅  
C. pried off with a pusher. / 用推棒撬掉。  
D. peeled off with tweezers. / 用镊子撕掉。  
*Why:* Hard gel can't be soaked off. Thin it about 60 percent with a coarse file, keep reducing with a medium file, and leave a very thin layer to grow out.  
*为什么:* 硬胶不能泡掉。先用粗锉条锉薄约 60%，再用中号锉条继续锉薄，最后留一层很薄的随指甲长出去。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Filing down to bare nail risks filing the nail itself. Leaving a thin film is the safe choice.  
*记重点:* 一直锉到露出真甲，很容易连真甲一起锉伤。留一层薄膜才安全。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.385 (PDF 409), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.361 (PDF 385)

**18. Before soaking off gel polish, why file the topcoat?**  
泡卸甲油胶之前，为什么要先锉封层？  
A. To let acetone get into the gel. / 让丙酮渗进胶里。 ✅  
B. To thin the natural nail plate. / 把真甲甲面锉薄。  
C. To warm the gel so it melts. / 让胶变热融化。  
D. To remove the cuticle. / 去掉甲小皮。  
*Why:* File the topcoat with a medium to fine grit so the acetone can penetrate. The topcoat is more cross-linked than the color layer, for protection and shine.  
*为什么:* 用中细目锉条锉一锉封层，丙酮才能渗进去。封层的交联程度比色胶层更高，用来保护和增亮。  
*Lock this (picture):* The topcoat is a raincoat that keeps acetone out. Filing cuts openings in the raincoat.  
*记重点:* 封层像一件雨衣，把丙酮挡在外面。锉一锉，就是在雨衣上开口子。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.387 (PDF 411), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.354 (PDF 378)

**19. Why wrap each nail in cotton and foil instead of using a bowl?**  
为什么用棉片加锡纸包住每个指甲，而不用碗泡？  
A. It can soak off hard gel too. / 连硬胶也能泡掉。  
B. It uses less remover and cuts odor. / 卸甲液用得少，气味也小。 ✅  
C. It needs no acetone at all. / 完全不需要丙酮。  
D. It cures any leftover gel. / 能把残留的胶固化。  
*Why:* Wrapping each nail with remover-soaked cotton and foil reduces odor, evaporation, and the amount of remover needed. It also lets the client move around while soaking.  
*为什么:* 用浸了卸甲液的棉片和锡纸包住每个指甲，可以减少气味、挥发和卸甲液用量，客人泡的时候也能活动。  
*Lock this (picture):* Foil is a lid on each nail: the acetone stays on the nail instead of drifting into the room.  
*记重点:* 锡纸就像每个指甲的盖子：丙酮留在指甲上，而不是飘满整个房间。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.361 (PDF 385)

**20. How do you know soaked gel polish is ready to remove?**  
怎么知道泡过的甲油胶可以卸了？  
A. It looks hard and glossy. / 看起来又硬又亮。  
B. It is stuck tight to the nail. / 还牢牢粘在指甲上。  
C. The foil feels warm. / 锡纸摸起来是热的。  
D. It is flaking off the nail. / 它正在从指甲上片片剥落。 ✅  
*Why:* When soak-off gel is ready, the polish should be flaking off. Ease the rest away with a wooden or stainless pusher, then lightly buff with a fine grit.  
*为什么:* 可卸胶泡好时，甲油胶应该正在片片剥落。用木推棒或不锈钢推棒把剩下的轻轻推掉，再用细目抛光条轻轻抛一下。  
*Lock this (real-life):* If you must scrape hard, it isn't ready. Soak a few more minutes instead of prying.  
*记重点:* 如果要用力刮，说明还没泡好。多泡几分钟，不要硬撬。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.388 (PDF 412), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.273 (PDF 297)
