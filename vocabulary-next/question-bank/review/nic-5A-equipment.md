# Set 5A — Equipment / 设备

Nail Service Tools · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. What kind of surface must a manicure table have?**  
美甲桌（manicure table）的桌面必须是什么样的？  
A. Soft and padded for comfort / 柔软、有衬垫，更舒适  
B. Hard and impenetrable, like glass / 坚硬、不渗透的，例如玻璃 ✅  
C. Unfinished wood that absorbs spills / 能吸收洒出液体的原木  
D. Fabric-covered to hide stains / 包一层布，遮住污渍  
*Why:* The table is cleaned and disinfected after every client, so its surface must be hard and impenetrable, such as Formica or glass, and kept clear of clutter.  
*为什么:* 每位顾客之后都要清洁并消毒桌面，所以桌面必须坚硬、不渗透，例如 Formica（福米加防火板）或玻璃，并保持整洁。  
*Lock this (picture):* Spill water on glass: it wipes right off. On raw wood it soaks in, and germs go with it.  
*记重点:* 水洒在玻璃上一擦就掉；洒在原木上会渗进去，病菌也跟着渗进去。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.137 (PDF 161)

**2. Why are fluorescent bulbs popular for manicure table lamps?**  
为什么美甲桌台灯常用荧光灯（fluorescent bulb）？  
A. They make polish dry faster. / 让指甲油干得更快。  
B. They disinfect the work surface. / 能给桌面消毒。  
C. They make skin look more golden. / 让皮肤看起来更偏金黄。  
D. They give off cooler light. / 发出的光比较凉。 ✅  
*Why:* Fluorescent bulbs emit cooler light and do not heat objects under the lamp. Heat from a high-watt incandescent bulb can make some enhancement products cure too fast, causing cracking and lifting.  
*为什么:* 荧光灯光线较凉，不会烤热灯下的东西。高瓦数白炽灯（incandescent bulb）的热量会让某些加固产品固化太快，导致开裂和翘起（lifting）。  
*Lock this (real-life):* A regular bulb can burn your finger. A fluorescent one stays much cooler. It puts less heat on curing product.  
*记重点:* 普通灯泡可能烫伤手指。荧光灯（fluorescent bulb）凉得多。照到正在固化的产品上的热量也少得多。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.137 (PDF 161), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.138 (PDF 162)

**3. Which chair at the manicure table should have wheels?**  
美甲桌旁哪把椅子应该有轮子？  
A. The client's chair / 顾客的椅子  
B. The technician's chair / 美甲师的椅子 ✅  
C. Both chairs / 两把都要  
D. Neither chair / 两把都不要  
*Why:* The technician's chair has wheels to move around and hydraulics to adjust height. The client's chair should not have wheels; it is unstable and can cause falls for elderly or weak clients.  
*为什么:* 美甲师的椅子有轮子方便移动，有液压（hydraulics）可调高度。顾客的椅子不应有轮子：不稳，年长或体弱的顾客可能摔倒。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture an older client pushing up from a rolling office chair: it slides back. That's the fall.  
*记重点:* 想象一位年长顾客扶着带轮子的办公椅站起来：椅子往后一滑，人就摔了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.137 (PDF 161)

**4. After each client, a finger bowl must be**  
每位顾客用完后，泡手碗（finger bowl）必须怎样处理？  
A. cleaned and disinfected. / 清洁并消毒。 ✅  
B. rinsed and refilled only. / 只冲一冲再加水。  
C. thrown away. / 扔掉。  
D. stored with the water in it. / 连水一起收起来。  
*Why:* A finger bowl is used on client after client, so it is a multiuse item, not thrown away. Multiuse items must be cleaned and disinfected, not just rinsed, before the next client.  
*为什么:* 泡手碗（finger bowl）一位接一位顾客重复使用，所以它是可重复使用的（multiuse）物品，不是用完就丢。可重复使用的物品在下一位顾客之前必须清洁并消毒，不能只冲一冲。  
*Lock this (picture):* Warm, wet, and touched by skin: the perfect place for germs. Disinfect before the next hands go in.  
*记重点:* 温暖、潮湿、又碰过皮肤：正是病菌最爱的地方。下一双手放进去之前必须消毒。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.138 (PDF 162), Milady Standard Foundations p.106 (PDF 601), Milady Standard Foundations p.118 (PDF 613)

**5. A disinfection container must be large enough to**  
消毒缸（disinfection container）必须大到能怎样？  
A. hold the tips of implements only. / 只浸泡工具的尖端。  
B. hold one implement at a time. / 一次只放一件工具。  
C. fully cover implements, handles included. / 把工具完全浸没，包括手柄。 ✅  
D. store a week of used implements. / 存放一周用过的工具。  
*Why:* It must hold enough disinfectant to completely immerse several service sets. Containers that cannot submerge the entire implement, including handles, are not acceptable. Keep it closed when not in use.  
*为什么:* 它要装足够的消毒液，能完全浸没好几套工具（service sets）。不能把整件工具（包括手柄）浸没的容器不能用。不用时要盖好。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* You hold a tool by its handle. If the handle sticks out of the liquid, its germs survive.  
*记重点:* 你拿工具时手握的是手柄。手柄露在液面外，上面的病菌就活下来了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.138 (PDF 162)

**6. UV and LED lamps are designed to do what to gel polish?**  
UV 灯和 LED 灯（光疗灯）是用来对甲油胶（gel polish）做什么的？  
A. Dry it by blowing warm air / 吹暖风把它吹干  
B. Cure (harden) it with light / 用光让它固化（cure） ✅  
C. Disinfect it before use / 使用前给它消毒  
D. Thin it for easier brushing / 把它变稀，好刷  
*Why:* Many clients call them dryers, but UV and LED light units cure, not dry. Their light starts a reaction in the gel. Regular polish dries when its solvents evaporate.  
*为什么:* 很多顾客把它们叫“烘干机”，但 UV 和 LED 灯是固化（cure），不是吹干（dry）。灯光让甲油胶发生反应。普通指甲油是溶剂挥发后才变干。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* The Chinese word means "become solid": the light changes liquid gel into a solid.  
*记重点:* 固化：固 = 固体，化 = 变化。灯光把液态的凝胶变成固体。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.139 (PDF 163), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.354 (PDF 378)

**7. Which device can spread disease and should never be used in a salon?**  
哪种设备可能传播疾病，绝不能在沙龙里使用？  
A. Autoclave / 高压蒸汽灭菌器（autoclave）  
B. Disinfection container / 消毒缸  
C. Covered trash container / 带盖垃圾桶  
D. Bead sterilizer / 玻璃珠消毒器（bead sterilizer） ✅  
*Why:* Electric, bead, and baby sterilizers can spread infectious diseases and must never be used in a salon. UV light units do not disinfect either. Most states require liquid disinfecting solutions.  
*为什么:* 电热消毒器、玻璃珠消毒器和婴儿用品消毒器可能传播传染病，绝不能在沙龙使用。UV 灯箱也不能消毒。多数州要求使用液体消毒剂。  
*Lock this (name-trap):* Sterilizer is in its name, yet it can spread disease. Trust a tested autoclave or liquid disinfectant.  
*记重点:* 名字里有“消毒器”，却可能传播疾病。要靠定期检测的高压灭菌器或液体消毒剂。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.140 (PDF 164)

**8. What does the automatic thermostat on a paraffin tub do?**  
蜡疗机（paraffin tub）上的自动恒温器（thermostat）有什么作用？  
A. Keeps the wax at the ideal temperature / 让蜡保持在合适的温度 ✅  
B. Sterilizes used wax for reuse / 给用过的蜡灭菌以便再用  
C. Cools the client's skin after use / 用完后给顾客皮肤降温  
D. Measures the client's body temperature / 测量顾客的体温  
*Why:* The automatic thermostat turns the heat up or down by itself, so the wax stays at the ideal temperature for hands and feet. It does not clean wax: used paraffin is thrown away.  
*为什么:* 自动恒温器（thermostat）会自己调高或调低热度，让蜡一直保持在适合手脚的理想温度。它不会清洁蜡：用过的石蜡（paraffin）要丢掉。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Thermo = heat, stat = stay still. A thermostat keeps the heat at one level.  
*记重点:* thermo = 热，stat = 停住不动。恒温器让温度停在一个水平；恒温的“恒”就是一直不变。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.140 (PDF 164), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.141 (PDF 165)

**9. On an electric file, torque is**  
打磨机的扭力（torque）是指什么？  
A. the top speed of the bit. / 磨头的最高转速。  
B. the size of the bit's shank. / 磨头柄（shank）的粗细。  
C. its power to keep turning under pressure. / 受压时继续转动的力量。 ✅  
D. the coarseness of the bit. / 磨头的粗糙程度。  
*Why:* Torque is the machine's power, its ability to keep turning when you press during filing. High-torque machines do more at lower speeds. If the handpiece slows or stops under pressure, the speed is too low.  
*为什么:* 扭力（torque）是机器的力量，也就是打磨时受压仍能继续转动的能力。高扭力机器在较低转速下就能做更多。手柄一受压就变慢或停转，说明转速太低。  
*Lock this (chinese-character):* The Chinese name means "twisting power." Torque is turning power, not top speed.  
*记重点:* torque = 扭力：扭 = 扭转，力 = 力量。是压下去时还能继续转的力量，不是最高转速（那是 RPM）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.231 (PDF 255)

哪种修脚泡脚盆（footbath）不能移动？

**11. Which causes heat to build up on the nail during electric filing?**  
电动打磨时，什么会让指甲上积热？  
A. Lifting the bit off the nail often / 经常把磨头抬离指甲  
B. Keeping the bit parallel to the nail / 让磨头与指甲保持平行  
C. Using a dust-extracting device / 使用吸尘装置  
D. Leaving the bit in one spot too long / 磨头在同一处停留太久 ✅  
*Why:* Heat comes from too much pressure (often because speed is too low), too high a speed with natural nail buffing bits, or leaving the bit in one place too long. Lift the bit often.  
*为什么:* 积热来自：用力太大（常因转速太低）、用天然甲抛光磨头时转速太高，或磨头在同一处停留太久。要经常抬起磨头。  
*Lock this (picture):* Hold a hot iron still on a shirt and it scorches. Keep the bit moving and lifting.  
*记重点:* 熨斗在衬衫上停着不动就会烫焦。磨头也要不停移动、经常抬起。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.247 (PDF 271)

**12. How should you hold an electric file handpiece?**  
应该怎样握打磨机手柄（handpiece）？  
A. Like a pencil, with a firm, steady grip / 像握铅笔一样，稳稳握住 ✅

C. Loosely, with just the fingertips / 只用指尖松松地捏着  
D. Pointed straight down at the nail / 垂直朝下对着指甲  
*Why:* Hold the handpiece like a pencil, with a firm, steady grip that is not so tight your hands cramp. Rest your forearms on the table and brace with a pinky as the fulcrum.  
*为什么:* 像握铅笔一样握手柄，握得稳但不要太紧，否则手会抽筋。前臂放在桌上，用小指当支点（fulcrum）稳住双手。  
*Lock this (body-cue):* Squeeze a pen hard: your hand hurts. Too loose: your writing wobbles. Firm and steady is in between.  
*记重点:* 握笔太用力，手会痛；握太松，字会歪。握得稳稳的、不松也不紧，刚好在中间。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.238 (PDF 262), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.249 (PDF 273)

微电机打磨机（micromotor electric file）的电机在哪里？

**14. On an electric file, RPM measures**  
电动打磨机上的 RPM 测量的是什么？  
A. full turns of the bit per minute. / 磨头每分钟转的整圈数。 ✅  
B. the weight of the handpiece. / 手柄的重量。  
C. the heat on the nail surface. / 指甲表面的温度。  
D. the grit size of the bit. / 磨头的粗细（grit）。  
*Why:* RPM means revolutions per minute: how many full circles the bit spins in one minute. Working in the middle of the machine's range makes the motor last longer.  
*为什么:* RPM = revolutions per minute（每分钟转数），即磨头一分钟转几整圈。在机器转速范围的中间档工作，电机更耐用。  
*Lock this (real-acronym):* R = Revolutions (full turns), P = Per, M = Minute. Like a car's speedometer, but for the bit.  
*记重点:* RPM：R = Revolutions（整圈），P = Per（每），M = Minute（分钟），就是每分钟转几整圈。教材说：把 RPM 想成汽车的速度表。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.231 (PDF 255)

**15. A concentric bit is one that**  
concentric bit（同心磨头）是指什么样的磨头？  
A. has a rounded safety tip. / 有圆头安全设计。  
B. is used once, then thrown away. / 用一次就扔。  
C. is balanced and does not wobble. / 平衡，不晃动。 ✅  
D. has grooves called flutes. / 表面有叫 flutes（刃槽）的槽纹。  
*Why:* Concentric bits are balanced, with the shank centered on the head, so they do not wobble or vibrate. Wobbling bits can damage the handpiece and the client's nail.  
*为什么:* 同心磨头（concentric bit）是平衡的，柄（shank）正对磨头中心，所以不晃也不震。晃动的磨头会损坏手柄和顾客的指甲。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Con = together, centric = center. Head and shank share one center, so it spins without wobbling.  
*记重点:* con = 共同，centric = 中心。头和柄共用一个圆心，转起来就稳。同心 = 同一个圆心。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.234 (PDF 258), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.251 (PDF 275)

**16. Which electric file bit should never be used on the natural nail?**  
哪种打磨头绝不能用在真甲（natural nail）上？  
A. Silicone bit / 硅胶磨头  
B. Chamois bit / 麂皮抛光磨头  
C. Carbide bit / 钨钢磨头（carbide bit） ✅  
D. Goat hair bit / 山羊毛抛光磨头  
*Why:* Never use a carbide bit on the natural nail. Its flutes shave enhancement product. Silicone bits and natural bits like chamois and goat hair lightly buff or shine natural nails.  
*为什么:* 绝不能在真甲上用钨钢磨头（carbide bit），它的刃槽（flutes）是用来削掉加固产品的。硅胶磨头和麂皮、山羊毛等天然磨头只是轻轻抛光真甲。  
*Lock this (picture):* Carbide flutes shave like tiny knives: good for hard product, too rough for a thin natural nail.  
*记重点:* 钨钢刃槽像许多把小刀，专削硬的产品；对薄薄的真甲太粗暴了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.235 (PDF 259)

哪种打磨头只能用一次？

*记重点:* 纸一泡就烂。纸砂圈、纸锉条：一位顾客用完就扔。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.236 (PDF 260), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.237 (PDF 261)

打磨机或 UV 灯上的 UL 标志说明什么？

*记重点:* UL = Underwriters Laboratories（美国保险商实验室）：一家检测电器安全的实验室。看到 UL 标志，说明用电安全通过了认证。

**19. What is the round third prong on a plug for?**  
插头上第三个圆形插脚是做什么用的？  
A. Making the motor run faster / 让电机转得更快  
B. Switching the appliance on / 开启电器  
C. Changing AC to DC / 把交流电变成直流电  
D. Grounding, to protect from shock / 接地（grounding），防止触电 ✅  
*Why:* The round third prong is a grounding pin. It gives electricity a safe path and protects the user from shock, even if a wire comes loose. Never alter it to fit a two-prong outlet.  
*为什么:* 第三个圆插脚是接地插脚（grounding pin），给电流一条安全通路，即使电线松脱也能防止使用者触电。绝不要改动它去插两孔插座。  
*Lock this (picture):* Ground means the earth. If electricity leaks, the third prong sends it to the ground, not through you.  
*记重点:* 接地 = 接到大地。第三个插脚给漏出来的电一条通往大地的路，而不是穿过你的身体。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.188 (PDF 683), Milady Standard Foundations p.191 (PDF 686)

**20. What does a fuse do in an electric circuit?**  
保险丝（fuse）在电路里起什么作用？  
A. Measures the electricity used / 测量用电量  
B. Boosts current for faster motors / 增大电流，让电机更快  
C. Stops excess current by melting / 电流过大时熔断，阻止过量电流 ✅  
D. Grounds the appliance's metal case / 让电器的金属外壳接地  
*Why:* A fuse prevents excessive current from passing through a circuit. A wire too small for its current overheats; the fuse melts first. Circuit breakers do the same job and can be reset.  
*为什么:* 保险丝防止过量电流通过电路。电线太细、承载不了电流就会过热，保险丝会先熔断。断路器（circuit breaker）作用相同，跳闸后可以复位。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Too many plugs, too much current, a hot wire: the fuse melts first so the wall doesn't catch fire.  
*记重点:* 插头太多 → 电流太大 → 电线发热：保险丝先熔断，墙里的电线才不会起火。
