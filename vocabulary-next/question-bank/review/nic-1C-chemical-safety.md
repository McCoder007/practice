# Set 1C — Chemical Safety and Injury Prevention / 化学品安全与伤害预防

Infection Control and Safety Practices · 20 questions · status: reviewed

**1. How many categories does every Safety Data Sheet (SDS) have?**  
每份安全数据表（SDS）都分成几个类别（categories）？  
A. 9 / 9 个  
B. 12 / 12 个  
C. 16 / 16 个 ✅  
D. 18 / 18 个  
*Why:* Every SDS uses the same 16 sections in the same order. In 2015, the SDS replaced the older Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS).  
*为什么:* 每份 SDS（安全数据表）都用同样的 16 个部分、同样的顺序。2015 年，SDS 取代了旧的 MSDS（材料安全数据表）。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Same order on every SDS from every maker: first aid is always Section 4.  
*记重点:* 不管哪家厂商，SDS 的顺序都一样：急救措施永远在第 4 部分。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.174 (PDF 669), Milady Standard Foundations p.173 (PDF 668)

**2. Where should a salon keep its SDSs?**  
店里的 SDS 应该放在哪里？  
A. Where all employees can reach them / 所有员工都能拿到的地方 ✅  
B. In a locked office / 上锁的办公室里  
C. On a manager-only computer / 只有经理能用的电脑上  
D. At the owner’s home / 店主家里  
*Why:* OSHA requires an SDS for every chemical in the salon, immediately available to all employees. A locked office or a manager-only computer is not acceptable.  
*为什么:* OSHA 要求店里每种化学品都要有 SDS，而且所有员工都能立刻拿到。锁在办公室里，或放在只有经理能用的电脑上，都不合格。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Splash in the eye at 8 p.m., manager gone home → whoever is on shift needs the SDS now.  
*记重点:* 晚上 8 点有人被溅到眼睛，经理已经下班 → 当班的人必须马上找到 SDS。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.174 (PDF 669)

**3. Which SDS section explains how to clean up a spill?**  
SDS 的哪个部分说明如何清理泄漏（spill）？  
A. First-Aid Measures / 急救措施  
B. Accidental Release Measures / 意外泄漏处理措施 ✅  
C. Ecological Information / 生态信息  
D. Fire-Fighting Measures / 消防措施  
*Why:* Section 6, Accidental Release Measures, explains spill cleanup, the protective equipment needed, and the emergency steps to follow.  
*为什么:* 第 6 部分 Accidental Release Measures（意外泄漏处理措施）说明如何清理泄漏、需要哪些防护装备，以及要采取的紧急措施。  
*Lock this (picture):* Picture a tipped-over bottle of acetone: the product is “released” by “accident.”  
*记重点:* 想象一瓶丙酮被打翻了：产品“意外”地“释放”出来了。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.175 (PDF 670)

**4. OSHA’s Hazard Communication Standard requires that employees be**  
OSHA 的危害沟通标准（Hazard Communication Standard）要求员工：  
A. licensed by OSHA. / 取得 OSHA 执照。  
B. tested yearly for allergies. / 每年做过敏测试。  
C. certified in first aid. / 取得急救证书。  
D. told about hazardous chemicals at work. / 被告知工作场所中的危险化学品。 ✅  
*Why:* The Hazard Communication Standard requires that employees be told about any chemical at work that could be hazardous. Manufacturers share this information through the SDS.  
*为什么:* Hazard Communication Standard（危害沟通标准）要求告知员工工作场所中任何可能有危险的化学品。厂家通过 SDS 提供这些信息。  
*Lock this (real-acronym):* OSHA = Occupational Safety and Health Administration. It protects workers: your “right to know.”  
*记重点:* OSHA = Occupational Safety and Health Administration（职业安全与健康管理局），保护员工：你有“知情权”（right to know）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.173 (PDF 668), Milady Standard Foundations p.97 (PDF 592)

**5. How should salon chemicals be stored?**  
店里的化学品应该如何存放？  
A. Locked up, in original containers / 锁好，放在原装容器里 ✅  
B. Combined if they are similar / 相似的就合在一起  
C. Out on the dispensing counter / 摆在配料台上  
D. In unlabeled spray bottles / 装在没贴标签的喷瓶里  
*Why:* Store chemicals in their original containers in a designated, locked place. Never combine similar chemicals or leave them sitting out, even in the dispensary.  
*为什么:* 化学品要放在原装容器里，存放在指定的、上锁的地方。即使相似的化学品也不能混放；化学品不能摆在外面，配料区也不例外。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Milady’s rule of thumb: if an adult can get to the chemicals, so can a child.  
*记重点:* Milady 的经验法则：大人拿得到的化学品，小孩也拿得到。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.172 (PDF 667)

**6. Why should hydrogen peroxide not be stored next to acetone?**  
为什么双氧水（hydrogen peroxide）不能和丙酮（acetone）放在一起？  
A. Acetone makes peroxide odorless. / 丙酮会让双氧水没有气味。  
B. Both lose strength in the dark. / 两者在暗处都会失效。  
C. Mixed, they can start a fire. / 两者混合可能起火。 ✅  
D. They form a sticky film. / 会形成一层黏膜。  
*Why:* Hydrogen peroxide is an oxidizer and acetone is a flammable solvent. If they mix, they can start a fire, so know how stored chemicals react before placing them together.  
*为什么:* hydrogen peroxide（双氧水）是氧化剂，acetone（丙酮）是易燃溶剂。两者一旦混合就可能起火，所以存放前要知道相邻的化学品会怎么反应。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Fire needs fuel, oxygen, and heat. Acetone is the fuel; peroxide gives off extra oxygen.  
*记重点:* 起火需要燃料、氧气和热量。丙酮是燃料，双氧水会额外释放氧气。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.172 (PDF 667), Milady Standard Foundations p.169 (PDF 664)

**7. Mixing bleach with vinegar creates**  
漂白水和醋混在一起，会产生：  
A. a stronger disinfectant. / 更强的消毒剂。  
B. a gentle cleaner. / 温和的清洁剂。  
C. an odorless foam. / 无味的泡沫。  
D. chlorine gas. / 氯气。 ✅  
*Why:* Never mix bleach with other cleaning products unless the label says to. Even vinegar and bleach make chlorine gas, so store bleach away from other chemicals.  
*为什么:* 除非标签说可以，否则漂白水绝不能和其他清洁产品混合。连醋加漂白水都会产生氯气，所以漂白水要和其他化学品分开存放。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Bleach + an acid like vinegar → chlorine gas → Milady warns the vapors can be fatal.  
*记重点:* 漂白水 + 醋这类酸 → 氯气 → Milady 警告这种气体可能致命。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.172 (PDF 667), Milady Standard Foundations p.123 (PDF 618)

**8. A flammable liquid has a flash point below**  
易燃（flammable）液体的闪点低于：  
A. 32°F / 华氏 32 度  
B. 100°F / 华氏 100 度 ✅  
C. 200°F / 华氏 200 度  
D. 300°F / 华氏 300 度  
*Why:* Flammable liquids ignite more easily than combustible ones. A flammable liquid’s flash point is below 100°F; a combustible liquid’s is between 100°F and 200°F.  
*为什么:* flammable（易燃）液体比 combustible（可燃）液体更容易着火。易燃液体闪点低于 100°F；可燃液体闪点在 100°F 到 200°F 之间。  
*Lock this (name-trap):* Name trap: inflammable means flammable too. The “in” here does not mean “not.”  
*记重点:* 名字陷阱：inflammable 也是“易燃”。这里的 in 不是“不”的意思。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.176 (PDF 671)

**9. A concentrate mixed into a spray bottle must be labeled with**  
浓缩液兑好装进喷瓶后，瓶上必须标明：  
A. product name, ingredients, and hazards. / 产品名称、成分和危害。 ✅  
B. only the date it was mixed. / 只写调配日期。  
C. the client’s name. / 客人的名字。  
D. nothing if the liquid is clear. / 液体是透明的就不用标。  
*Why:* When a concentrate is mixed into a secondary container, such as a spray bottle, label it with the product name, ingredients, and any hazards the manufacturer lists.  
*为什么:* 浓缩液兑好装进 secondary container（二次容器，比如喷瓶）后，要标明产品名称、成分，以及厂家列出的所有危害。  
*Lock this (real-life):* A clear liquid could be water or disinfectant. Nobody should have to guess, or sniff.  
*记重点:* 透明液体可能是水，也可能是消毒剂。不能让人靠猜或靠闻。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.173 (PDF 668), Milady Standard Foundations p.123 (PDF 618)

**10. How should chemicals be transported in your car?**  
开车运送化学品时，应该怎么放？  
A. In the trunk on a hot day / 大热天放在后备箱  
B. Upright, in their original containers / 直立放好，用原装容器 ✅  
C. Opened bottles, mixed in one bag / 开过封的瓶子混装在一个袋子里  
D. Loose on a sunny back seat / 散放在晒着太阳的后座  
*Why:* Carry chemicals upright in their original containers, and never leave them in a hot car. Do not transport opened or partly used containers; many chemicals get more dangerous as they break down.  
*为什么:* 运送化学品要直立放好、用原装容器，绝不能留在高温的车里。开过封或用过一部分的容器不要运送，因为很多化学品分解后会更危险。  
*Lock this (real-life):* Heat builds pressure in a closed bottle, so it can leak or burst. Check the label’s storage temperature.  
*记重点:* 受热后瓶内压力升高，可能漏出甚至爆开。看看标签上的储存温度。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.171 (PDF 666)

**11. Which SDS section shows warning pictograms and says if a product is flammable?**  
SDS 的哪个部分有警示图标，并说明产品是否易燃？  
A. Composition/Information on Ingredients / 成分信息  
B. Transport Information / 运输信息  
C. Other Information / 其他信息

*记重点:* 第 1 部分说它是什么，第 2 部分说它会怎么伤人。危险信息排在最前面。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.174 (PDF 669)

**12. A rash caused by a harsh substance, not by an allergy, is called**  
由会伤皮肤的物质（而不是过敏）引起的皮疹，叫作：  
A. allergic contact dermatitis. / 过敏性接触性皮炎。  
B. psoriasis. / 银屑病。  
C. irritant contact dermatitis. / 刺激性接触性皮炎。 ✅  
D. tinea manus. / 手癣。  
*Why:* Contact dermatitis is skin inflammation from touching a substance. If the substance irritates the skin, it is irritant contact dermatitis. If the skin becomes allergic, it is allergic contact dermatitis.  
*为什么:* contact dermatitis（接触性皮炎）是皮肤接触某种物质后发炎。物质刺激皮肤，就是 irritant contact dermatitis（刺激性接触性皮炎）；皮肤对它过敏，就是 allergic contact dermatitis（过敏性接触性皮炎）。  
*Lock this (word-parts):* Derma = skin, -itis = inflammation. Contact dermatitis is the most common avoidable skin disease for nail techs.  
*记重点:* derma = 皮肤，-itis = 发炎。contact dermatitis（接触性皮炎）是美甲师最常见的、可以避免的皮肤病。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.87 (PDF 111), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.90 (PDF 114)

**13. A greatly increased allergic sensitivity to a product is called**  
对某种产品的过敏反应大大增强，这叫作：  
A. sensitization. / 致敏。 ✅  
B. neutralization. / 中和。  
C. polymerization. / 聚合。  
D. sterilization. / 灭菌。  
*Why:* Sensitization is a greatly increased or exaggerated allergic sensitivity to products. With each skin exposure during a service, the risk of sensitization rises.  
*为什么:* sensitization（致敏）是对产品的过敏反应大大增强。服务中皮肤每接触一次，致敏风险就更高一些。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Once allergic, the same technique only makes it worse. Stop using the product and find the cause.  
*记重点:* 一旦过敏，继续用同样的方法只会越来越糟。先停用这个产品，找出原因。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.94 (PDF 118), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.88 (PDF 112), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.89 (PDF 113)

**14. Which best removes vapors and dust at a nail table?**  
哪一种最能在美甲台把蒸气和粉尘排走？  
A. A fan on the table / 桌上的电风扇  
B. Local source-capture ventilation / 局部源头捕集通风 ✅  
C. An open window / 打开的窗户  
D. A home air purifier / 家用空气净化器  
*Why:* Source-capture systems pull vapors and dust away at the source, before they escape into the salon. Fans and open windows only move them around the room.  
*为什么:* source-capture（源头捕集）系统在源头就把蒸气和粉尘吸走，不让它们散到店里。电风扇和开窗只会把它们在屋里吹来吹去。  
*Lock this (comparison):* Like a kitchen range hood: catch the smoke right above the pan, before it fills the house.  
*记重点:* 就像厨房抽油烟机：在锅上方就把油烟吸走，不让它飘满屋子。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Nail Technology p.128 (PDF 152), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.129 (PDF 153)

**15. When lifting a box of supplies, you should**  
搬一箱用品时，应该：  
A. bend at the waist and lift fast. / 弯腰快速抬起。  
B. twist your body as you lift. / 边抬边扭身体。  
C. hold it far from your body. / 让箱子离身体远一点。  
D. bend at the knees, not the back. / 弯膝盖，不要弯腰。 ✅  
*Why:* Keep your feet shoulder-width apart, bend at the knees, keep the load close, and lift with your legs. Do not twist or make fast, jerky moves.  
*为什么:* 双脚与肩同宽，弯曲膝盖，让重物贴近身体，用腿部发力抬起。不要扭身，也不要猛地发力。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* The farther a load is from your body, the harder your back works. Never reach past 18 inches.  
*记重点:* 东西离身体越远，腰背越吃力。伸手不要超过 18 英寸（约 45 厘米）。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.89 (PDF 584)

**16. After removing your gloves, how can you help prevent skin problems?**  
摘下手套后，怎样做能帮助预防皮肤问题？  
A. Rinse and leave hands damp / 冲一下，让手保持湿润  
B. Use hand sanitizer all day / 整天用免洗洗手液  
C. Wash, dry thoroughly, then apply lotion / 洗手，彻底擦干，再涂护手霜 ✅  
D. Soak hands in very hot water / 把手泡在很烫的水里  
*Why:* Gloves limit chemical contact, but wash and thoroughly dry your hands after you take them off. Then use lotion or cream to restore moisture. Avoid harsh soaps and constant hand sanitizer.  
*为什么:* 手套能减少接触化学品，但摘下后要洗手并彻底擦干，再涂乳液或护手霜补充水分。避免用刺激性强的肥皂，也不要频繁使用免洗洗手液。  
*Lock this (reason-chain):* Damp skin and chemicals, day after day → cracked skin barrier → irritation and allergies.  
*记重点:* 皮肤天天又湿又碰化学品 → 皮肤屏障受损 → 刺激和过敏。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.83 (PDF 578), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.90 (PDF 114)

**17. Which disposable gloves does Milady recommend instead of latex?**  
Milady 推荐用哪种一次性手套代替乳胶手套？  
A. Petroleum-based / 石油基手套  
B. Nitrile / 丁腈手套 ✅  
C. Cotton / 棉手套  
D. Leather / 皮手套  
*Why:* Avoid latex, because latex allergies are increasingly common. Petroleum-based gloves break down when they touch petroleum products. Nitrile gloves avoid both problems.  
*为什么:* 要避免乳胶手套，因为对乳胶过敏的人越来越多。石油基手套碰到含石油的产品会分解。nitrile（丁腈）手套这两个问题都没有。  
*Lock this (real-life):* OSHA recommends nitrile for pedicures and chemical work. A new pair for every client, never reused.  
*记重点:* 做足疗和接触化学品时，OSHA 建议戴丁腈手套。每个客人换新手套，绝不重复用。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.128 (PDF 623), Milady Standard Nail Technology p.200 (PDF 224)

调配化学品的地方必须备有哪种用品？

**19. During a manicure, how should you avoid reaching across the table?**  
做美甲时，怎样避免越过桌子去够客人的手？  
A. Have the client extend their hand to you. / 请客人把手伸到你这边。 ✅

D. Hold your elbows far from your body. / 手肘远离身体。

*为什么:* ergonomics（人体工学）就是让身体在工作中动得舒适、安全。请客人把手伸过桌子给你，保持背部、颈部和手腕挺直。  
*Lock this (word-origin):* Ergonomics comes from Greek ergon, “work,” and nomos, “law”: the rules for working without injury.  
*记重点:* ergonomics 来自希腊语：ergon = 工作，nomos = 法则。就是不受伤地工作的法则。  
*Source:* Milady Standard Foundations p.88 (PDF 583), Milady Standard Foundations p.85 (PDF 580)

**20. Where should a salon’s fire extinguishers be kept?**  
店里的灭火器应该放在哪里？

*记重点:* 不是每种灭火器都适合每种火：SDS 第 5 部分 Fire-Fighting Measures（消防措施）会列出该用哪种。
