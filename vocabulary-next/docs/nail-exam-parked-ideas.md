# Parked ideas: nail exam practice (ESL level 2)

Ideas the owner liked but set aside on 2026-10-23. Not scheduled.

## Tap a hard word inside a question
While answering a quiz question, the learner taps a hard word (e.g. "hyponychium") and sees the Chinese term (甲下皮) and hears it, without turning on full Chinese. Most useful in practice tests, where the Chinese toggle gives too much help. The word reels now have this (2026-10-23): `data/word-reel/hard-words.json` holds about 1,000 hard words and terms with Chinese and an easy meaning, and `lib/sentence-words.ts` finds them in a sentence. Quiz questions use many other words, so the list would need to grow to cover them.

## Review my mistakes
Missed questions come back later (e.g. the next day) until the learner gets each one right twice. The app keeps scores per set today but has no retry pile of missed questions.
