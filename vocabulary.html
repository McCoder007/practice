Day 29

Avoid

避免
verb

Wear a helmet to avoid injuries.

戴头盔可以避免受伤。

Construction laborer

建筑工人
noun

The construction laborer wears a hard hat at work.

建筑工人在工作时戴安全帽。

Contact

联系
verb

You can contact OSHA for help.

你可以联系 OSHA 寻求帮助。

Employers

雇主
noun (plural)

Employers must follow safety rules.

雇主必须遵守安全规定。

Gear

装备
noun

Workers should wear safety gear.

工人应该穿戴安全装备。

Hazards

危险
noun (plural)

Workers should report workplace hazards.

工人应该报告工作场所的危险。

Injury

受伤
noun

He had an injury at work.

他在工作时受伤了。

Inspection

检查
noun

The restaurant passed the health inspection.

这家餐厅通过了卫生检查。

Inspectors

检查员
noun (plural)

OSHA inspectors visit workplaces.

OSHA 检查员会检查工作场所。

Janitor

保洁员
noun

The janitor cleans the school every day.

保洁员每天打扫学校。

Nursing assistant

护理助理
noun

The nursing assistant helps patients in the hospital.

护理助理在医院帮助病人。

Obey

遵守
verb

Students must obey the school rules.

学生必须遵守校规。

Occupational

职业的
adjective

She teaches an occupational safety class.

她教授职业安全课程。

Occupational injury

职业工伤
noun

The company tracks occupational injuries each year.

公司每年统计职业工伤。

Retail assistant

零售店员
noun

The retail assistant helped me find a shirt.

零售店员帮我找了一件衬衫。

Right

权利
noun

Workers have the right to a safe workplace.

工人有权拥有安全的工作场所。

Standards

标准
noun (plural)

This product meets safety standards.

这个产品符合安全标准。

Toxic chemicals

有毒化学品
noun phrase

Workers should stay away from toxic chemicals.

工人应该远离有毒化学品。

Truck driver

卡车司机
noun

The truck driver delivers products across the country.

卡车司机把货物运送到全国各地。

Workplace

noun

Everyone should feel safe in the workplace.

每个人都应该在工作场所感到安全。

Workplace injury

工作场所受伤
noun

The company wants to prevent workplace injuries.

公司希望防止工作场所受伤事故。
