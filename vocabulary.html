New Words | 生词

Day 28

Affects

影响
verb

Lack of sleep affects my mood.

缺乏睡眠会影响我的心情。

Complaints

抱怨
noun (plural)

The restaurant received many complaints.

这家餐厅收到了很多投诉。

Comfort

舒适 / 安慰
noun

This chair gives me comfort.

这把椅子让我觉得很舒适。

Consume

消耗 / 摄取
verb

I try not to consume too much sugar.

我尽量不摄取太多糖分。

Contain

含有
verb

This drink contains caffeine.

这种饮料含有咖啡因。

Daisy

雏菊
noun

She picked a daisy from the garden.

她从花园里摘了一朵雏菊。

Dependence

依赖
noun

Too much dependence on phones is not healthy.

过度依赖手机并不健康。

Dizzy

头晕的
adjective

I feel dizzy when I stand up too fast.

我站得太快时会头晕。

Effects

影响 / 效果
noun (plural)

The medicine has good effects.

这种药有很好的效果。

Impulsiveness

冲动
noun

His impulsiveness got him into trouble.

他的冲动让他惹上了麻烦。

Irritable

易怒的
adjective

I become irritable when I do not sleep well.

睡不好时我会变得容易生气。

Lead

铅
noun

Old houses may have lead paint on the walls.

老房子的墙上可能有含铅油漆。

Pig tails

双马尾辫
noun

The little girl has pig tails.

那个小女孩梳着双马尾。

Ponytail

马尾辫
noun

She wears her hair in a ponytail.

她把头发扎成马尾辫。

Severe

严重的
adjective

He has a severe headache.

他头痛得很厉害。

Temper

脾气
noun

My brother has a bad temper.

我哥哥的脾气不好。
